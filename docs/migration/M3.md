# M3: bounded entity research queue

The replacement queue has no dependency graph, classifier, alert or operation
history. Each company (issuer) or idea has at most one pending/running/failed
row. Separate listed instruments retain their IDs and pages; the lexically first
security ID represents company scheduling only. Reasons and maximum priority are
merged deterministically. Identical reasons do not change timestamps.

Defaults in `research.ini`: 30 pending, two attempts, three operations per daily
cycle, a 15-minute self-expiring lease. A live lease prevents concurrent research.
After one failure, retry that entity after six hours; other entities remain
eligible. An exhausted company/idea leaves the queue and becomes needs_attention;
its last accepted valuation survives. Manual requests can restart research.
Automatic scheduling does not immediately recreate exhausted work.

Priorities: explicit manual request 100; verified material company evidence 90;
severe price movement 80; high-confidence valuation needing review 70; material
technical context 65; overdue assessment 60; ordinary unresearched company 20.
Thresholds for price attention are 8% in one session or 15% in five sessions;
secondary confidence/technical reasons require at least 5% movement. RSI alone
cannot create an operation. Compare features with the pre-market snapshot so an
unchanged observation cannot replay price/technical causes. All reasons merge
into the company task, including reasons from multiple share classes. Research
completed within seven days suppresses ordinary/price-driven reconsideration;
verified material developments and manual requests can still take priority.

At capacity, new automatic work stays in the catalog unless its priority exceeds
a lower-priority automatic task. A manual request displaces automatic work,
never another manual request or running task. If all capacity is protected,
manual admission raises an explicit error. No extra blocked rows are created.

## Legacy disposition

The M0 full census lives outside active data and remains recoverable from the
verified tag. Rebuild is read-only by default:

```sh
uv run python -m papertrader.research_queue \
  --revision 9056d3dff03740a6c3310e086a240186bca4527a \
  --as-of 2026-10-18T17:00:00Z
```

All 648 legacy rows receive exactly one disposition, with blocked-reason and
waiting-relationship counts reported separately. The 320 capacity blocks are
never replayed. The other 20 blocked rows lose their failed operation artifacts
and dependencies; any useful company goal is independently selected from current
state, rather than admitted on an old failure or frozen price hash. The 13 waiting
relationship rows become optional inline research, without mandatory company
follow-ups. Search/transcript leads lack independent verification and do not
produce automatic tasks. Their lawful current citations remain in the registry.

Three distinct still-relevant manual company goals survive (Crocs, MP Materials,
SoFi). Duplicate capacity-blocked MP/SoFi requests are discarded, while their
separate current manual requests are preserved. Ordinary overdue/unresearched
companies fill remaining capacity deterministically. Every other company and
idea stays in its canonical catalog. No old row IDs or dependencies migrate.

The replacement producer/controller is independently implemented and tested.
The active legacy queue is still frozen: M4 switches canonical readers and M7
activates scheduling; M8 removes old producer code and data after the migration
gates. No legacy scheduler or halfway-migrated public view is enabled here.
