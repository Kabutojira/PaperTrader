# Podcast editorial outline

## introduction

Open with the required PaperTrader welcome and frame the episode around a single investor question: when unusual trading volume points to a real economic development, and when it merely reflects ownership transfers or financial engineering.

## teaser

Preview three standalone stories: Parrot's sovereign-drone growth versus cash consumption, dLocal's sponsor share sale versus issuer dilution, and Strategy's effort to protect liquidity while issuing common shares and reshaping preferred claims.

## chapters

Candidate slate and mapping:

- Selected: Parrot S.A. full review. Chapter one explains the sovereign small-drone mechanism, first-half operating progress, cash conversion, thin liquidity, scenario valuation, and Hold / Watch conditions.
- Selected: dLocal quick check. Chapter two explains emerging-market payment rails, the General Atlantic secondary sale, why high volume was not dilution, current operating evidence, valuation, the missing accepted causal relationship, and Medium / Watch conditions.
- Selected: Strategy Inc repeat review. Chapter three explains the leveraged Bitcoin-treasury residual claim, the two new weekly filings, common issuance, Bitcoin sale, preferred repurchases, reserve growth, sum-of-parts valuation, and Hold / Watch conditions.
- Excluded: CrowdStrike quick check. The selloff had no new operating filing, changed valuation, or changed conclusion, so it is routine unchanged maintenance without enough audience value for a chapter.
- Excluded: Coinbase repeat review. The immediately prior podcast already covered the same Q2 evidence, valuation, Strong Sell / Avoid conclusion, and conditions; the new volume anomaly supplied no material advance.

Each selected development appears in exactly one chapter. The chapters are related by the distinction between visible market activity and changes in per-share economics, but remain self-contained.

## overall_summary

Contrast operating progress, a secondary ownership transfer, and balance-sheet engineering. Emphasize that volume identifies a question rather than answering it, and that cash conversion, claims senior to common stock, dilution, valuation, and causal evidence determine the investment conclusion.

## greetings

Close with the two required exact sentences and no additional spoken copy afterward.
