Contrast four distinct lessons: Prosus demonstrates shareholder-value transmission that is strong enough for one small pending action; Uber and Tencent remain attractive but portfolio-constrained; Crocs demonstrates that a low quote does not repair weak causal and downside evidence; NVIDIA demonstrates that explosive operating growth can simultaneously raise fair value and financial risk.
