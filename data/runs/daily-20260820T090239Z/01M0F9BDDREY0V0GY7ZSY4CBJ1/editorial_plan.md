# Daily podcast editorial plan

## Candidate slate

| Development | Disposition | Chapter | Editorial reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recursion Pharmaceuticals quick check | selected | one | A sharp, high-volume rise without a disclosed operating catalyst creates a useful standalone test of whether an AI-drug-discovery price move reflects clinical progress or only market attention. |

Every successful development in the frozen window is mapped once above.

## introduction

Use the required opening sentence and frame the episode around a common question: when a chart flashes, what economic evidence would make the move investable?

## teaser

Preview three cases in listener-facing terms: unexplained enthusiasm around an artificial-intelligence drug developer, a semiconductor manufacturer spending heavily on domestic capacity, and a solar company whose project financing does not automatically repair module economics.

## chapters

1. Recursion Pharmaceuticals: explain the AI-native discovery loop, the exact volume move, unchanged filing and trial evidence, cash burn and dilution, why valuation is unsupported, and the next clinical proof points. End with Unrated / Watch, the reason, Buy conditions, and Sell conditions.
2. Texas Instruments: explain analog semiconductors, the lower Bollinger signal, current operating and capital-spending evidence, the nearshoring mechanism and limits, scenario valuation, and why a lower quote is not yet a bargain. End with Hold / Watch, the reason, the approximately USD 205.20 Buy boundary plus evidence gates, and Sell conditions.
3. Canadian Solar: explain the split between modules, battery storage, and project development; the bullish MACD signal; Cobalt Solar financing; weak module, cash-conversion, leverage, and working-capital evidence; scenario valuation; and the missing relationship and payoff gates. End with Buy / Initiate at watch-tier conviction but allocation-ineligible, the approximately USD 15.02 entry boundary plus evidence gates, and Sell conditions.

## overall_summary

Contrast clinical optionality, semiconductor capacity, and energy-project financing. Emphasize that technical signals are triage tools, while primary evidence, per-share economics, causal relationships, downside, and valuation determine the decision.

## greetings

Use the two required closing sentences exactly.
