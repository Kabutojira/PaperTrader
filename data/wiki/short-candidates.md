# Short candidates

Short candidates are companies researched for potentially weak fundamentals or excessive market expectations. Membership does not imply an active short recommendation, and valuation opinions are not option-trading signals.

Research examines business economics, financing, dilution and expectations, with explicit thesis invalidation. Only current supported bearish opinions are ordered as bearish appraisals. Other opinions and Unrated names remain visible separately. Prices and opinions describe the published snapshot; check their dates. [Rating methodology](rating-methodology).
