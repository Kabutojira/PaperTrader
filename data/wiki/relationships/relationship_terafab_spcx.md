# Terafab AI industrial stack to SpaceX listed equity

## Decision

**Accepted — positive beneficiary, high sensitivity and medium confidence.** SpaceX is both the
proposed owner and the principal user of Terafab, so a successful campus can materially affect its
compute, launch and connectivity economics. The same structure makes SpaceX the public security
most exposed to construction, yield, utilization, capital-allocation and related-party risks.

## Causal mechanism

The [[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial stack]] is intended to coordinate
masking, logic, memory, packaging and complete compute systems around SpaceX, xAI and Tesla demand.
For [[securities/security_664f93a7eaca72e76e9b|SpaceX listed equity]], successful internal
packaging, selected silicon and system production could reduce supply bottlenecks, improve design
iteration and support faster Starlink, xAI and orbital-compute deployment. Value appears only when
those operating gains exceed capital intensity, learning-curve losses and the opportunity cost of
funding a semiconductor campus alongside Starship, Starlink and AI infrastructure.

Sensitivity is **high** because the current SpaceX research already shows property and equipment
purchases far above operating cash flow and continuing losses in Space and AI. Terafab can therefore
be either a material integration advantage or another large claim on shareholder capital.

## Evidence

- The [Texas JETI application](https://assets.comptroller.texas.gov/open-data/jeti/J0037/J0037-terafab-anderson-appsupp1.pdf)
  describes a proposed multiphase semiconductor, advanced-packaging and system-integration campus
  associated with SpaceX, xAI and Tesla. It is an application and incentive record, not operating
  proof.
- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  describes the one-terawatt compute-production ambition and the integrated workflow, while also
  expecting significant third-party compute sourcing to continue.
- The maintained SpaceX review records a strong post-IPO cash position but extraordinary capex,
  Space and AI operating losses, and concentrated controller governance. These are the economic
  constraints through which Terafab must create value.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are binding phase financing and construction commitments; disclosed tool, process and IP
agreements; first qualified packaging or silicon products; measurable yield, cost or deployment
benefits; and Terafab-supported compute capacity converting into durable segment revenue and cash
flow.

Contrary evidence is that current project records are prospective and the SpaceX prospectus says
important frameworks may not become definitive agreements. Invalidate the positive edge if phases
remain uncommitted; construction, utility or qualification schedules slip materially; yield and
unit cost remain uncompetitive; the campus is underutilized; related-party decisions transfer value;
or cumulative Terafab capex and operating losses outweigh supply-security and deployment gains.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after binding phase economics, first-wafer or qualification
evidence, a material financing change, or a revised Terafab schedule.
