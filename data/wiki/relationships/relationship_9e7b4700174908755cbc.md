This accepted causal edge identifies direct but unproven exposure to AI-enabled discovery and
precision-biology workflows. It is not a trading recommendation and does not treat candidate
creation, early pharmacokinetics, or platform activity as proof of clinical or shareholder value.

## Causal mechanism

Recursion applies computational models, biological data and automated experimentation to identify
and advance drug candidates. If those tools produce repeatable improvements in target selection,
cycle time or clinical translation, Recursion can capture value through its own pipeline and
partner milestones. The exposure is high because platform-derived programs and data capabilities
are central to the company, but the equity transmission is conditional on successful trials,
partner economics and per-share value after operating burn and financing.

Current evidence supports platform activity rather than validated economics. The maintained Q1
2026 review records clinical or regulatory progress for REC-1245, REC-4881 and REC-4539, while the
SEC-filed Form 10-Q reports USD 654.5 million of cash and cash equivalents, 530.6 million shares
outstanding, and a USD 117.5 million quarterly net loss. Additional capital is expected to be
required. These facts keep confidence low: the thematic exposure is causal, but clinical success,
commercial value, cash conversion and dilution-adjusted shareholder benefit remain unproved.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Strengthen the edge if blinded clinical data support efficacy and safety, FDA engagement produces
clear development paths, partner milestones become repeatable, measured discovery-cycle or
probability-of-success improvements are disclosed, and operating burn declines without financing
that overwhelms per-share value. REC-4881 and REC-1245 readouts, partner economics and the next
quarterly filing are bounded review points.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if programs fail safety or efficacy tests, development timelines or
costs do not improve, candidates are repeatedly deprioritized, partnership revenue remains
immaterial, proprietary-data commitments consume capital without measurable output, or financing
and dilution absorb the platform's enterprise value. Broad AI adoption elsewhere does not preserve
this company-specific edge if Recursion cannot demonstrate clinical translation.
