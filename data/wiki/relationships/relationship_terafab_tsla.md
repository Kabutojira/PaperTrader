# Terafab AI industrial stack to Tesla

## Decision

**Accepted — positive beneficiary, medium sensitivity and medium confidence.** Tesla has a direct
custom-chip and edge-compute use case, but the disclosed Terafab framework is not a binding capacity,
capital or commercial agreement.

## Causal mechanism

[[securities/security_dc7a111e297be528d96b|Tesla]] designs inference hardware for vehicle,
autonomy and robotics workloads. A qualified [[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab]]
process and packaging route could shorten design-to-production cycles, improve supply security and
coordinate Tesla's specialized edge products with SpaceXAI's larger compute stack. The benefit
requires competitive process technology, packaging, yield, cost and product qualification; shared
ownership or engineering proximity alone does not create public-shareholder value.

Sensitivity is **medium** because custom inference hardware matters to Tesla's autonomy and Optimus
ambitions, yet the current valuation already depends on those unproven profit pools and Tesla retains
external fabrication options. Capital allocation and controller-related-party terms can offset any
technical advantage.

## Evidence

- Tesla's [AI page](https://www.tesla.com/AI) supports its internal inference-chip design and
  workload-specific hardware program, establishing a plausible product channel.
- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  describes shared engineering and a Tesla framework, but states that the framework may not lead
  to a definitive agreement.
- The maintained Tesla review records strong liquidity alongside more than USD 25 billion of
  expected 2026 capex and unproven autonomy and robotics economics, making incremental returns and
  governance terms decisive.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are binding Tesla–Terafab terms; a named Tesla chip or package; disclosed volume, cost,
performance or design-cycle improvements; qualified production; and Terafab-supported autonomy,
Optimus or edge products contributing measurable revenue or cash flow.

Contrary evidence is the absence of disclosed capacity, ownership, price, capital, schedule or
product commitments. Invalidate the positive edge if the framework remains non-definitive; Tesla's
products remain external without an economic disadvantage; Terafab yield, cost or schedule is
uncompetitive; related-party terms transfer value; or capital and organizational complexity exceed
supply-security and iteration benefits.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after definitive Tesla terms, a named product qualification,
material capex disclosure or a related-party governance filing.
