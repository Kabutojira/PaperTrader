## Causal mechanism

Electric-load growth, data-centre construction, utility capital expenditure, and grid congestion
increase demand for electrical raceway, conduit, cable-management, and related infrastructure.
Atkore's Electrical segment supplies these products, so realised project construction can raise
volume and revenue. The second-quarter evidence supports the first half of the mechanism:
Electrical sales increased 8.1%, including a USD 28.4 million volume contribution.

The link to shareholder returns is conditional. Commodity and input-cost changes pass through with
a lag, and competitive pricing can transfer the demand benefit to customers. In the same quarter,
USD 82.1 million of higher input costs overwhelmed a USD 10.2 million selling-price benefit;
Electrical adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 18.5% to 14.0%. Volume sensitivity is therefore clearer
than profit or cash-flow sensitivity.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm higher sensitivity only if Electrical volume remains positive while selling prices catch
input costs, segment adjusted EBITDA margin recovers, and operating cash flow turns sustainably
positive. Delivery of fiscal-2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance and post-divestiture reporting that
isolates the electrical portfolio are near-term checkpoints.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Reject or downgrade the edge if electrical-infrastructure demand does not produce segment volume,
if input inflation or competition prevents price recovery, if project delays weaken orders, or if
working capital and restructuring consume cash despite revenue growth. The equity implication is
invalidated even with strong end-market demand if Atkore cannot earn adequate margins and free cash
flow from that demand.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research operation is justified now. A current mark exists, but the security page has
no supportable fair-value range, margin recovery is unconfirmed, cash conversion is negative, and
post-divestiture normalised earnings are unclear. Reconsider only after those blockers are resolved;
do not manufacture an entry rule from thematic exposure alone.
