This accepted edge identifies direct operating exposure to lithium demand and realized pricing. It is
not a trading recommendation: contract lags, cost and inventory timing, capital requirements,
leverage, supply response, taxes, and dilution can prevent a lithium recovery from reaching common
shareholders.

## Causal mechanism

Growth in EV and stationary-storage lithium demand can tighten the market and raise realized lithium
prices. Albemarle's Energy Storage segment converts and sells lithium products, so stronger prices
and volumes can expand sales, EBITDA, operating cash flow, and the value of its resource and
conversion footprint. Q1 2026 demonstrates this transmission: Energy Storage price rose 51%, volume
rose 14%, segment sales rose 70%, and adjusted EBITDA rose 196%.

Sensitivity is high but not one-for-one. Management's 2026 adjusted-EBITDA scenarios range from
USD 0.9-1.0 billion near USD 10/kg LCE to USD 4.2-4.4 billion near USD 30/kg. Forty percent of salts
volume is under long-term agreements, while inventory timing, operating costs, capex, taxes, asset
sales, debt, and mandatory-convertible preferred shares determine how commodity recovery accrues to
the listed common stock.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if EV and storage demand remain durable; Albemarle sustains higher
realized prices and volumes after contract lags; Energy Storage margins and operating cash flow
remain positive without favorable inventory timing; capex stays disciplined; leverage falls through
operations; and the scheduled Q2 release supports the Q1 recovery.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if Chinese or other idled supply restarts, new projects, recycling,
sodium-ion adoption, material efficiency, or weaker EV and storage demand restores surplus; realized
prices fail to follow benchmarks; volumes or margins weaken; inventory or working capital consumes
cash; capex, taxes, debt, or dilution absorb operating gains; or Energy Storage economics decouple
from the lithium cycle. A positive causal operating edge can remain accepted while normalized
valuation makes the common stock unattractive.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The current assessment is ineligible because the wide
commodity scenarios do not support probability-weighted downside and base common-equity values after
contract lag, tax, terminal-multiple, and mandatory-convertible assumptions. Reconsider after the
scheduled Q2 release on 2026-08-06 or after material lithium-price, supply, cash-conversion, leverage,
or valuation evidence.
