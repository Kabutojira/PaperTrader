## Causal mechanism

Residential solar installations create direct demand for Enphase microinverters; adding storage
and time-of-use optimisation increases demand for batteries, gateways, and energy-management
software. A durable reset in financing, installer health, channel inventory, tariffs, and policy
would therefore affect both Enphase shipment volume and product mix. This exposure is direct enough
for high sensitivity, but positive shareholder value still depends on price, margin, tax-credit
quality, warranties, and cash conversion.

The current evidence demonstrates both sides. Enphase shipped 1.41 million microinverters and
103.1 MWh of batteries in Q1 2026 and generated USD 83.0 million of company-defined free cash flow.
However, US sell-through fell 48% sequentially and 18% year over year, battery shipments declined
from 150.1 MWh in Q4, and USD 34.5 million of quarterly revenue came from safe-harbour activity.
The Section 25D residential credit expiry and a 3.5%-3.75% federal-funds target preserve direct
policy and financing transmission to the business.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm the positive edge when US sell-through stabilises without inventory accumulation, battery
shipments resume growth, tariff-adjusted gross margin holds, and free cash flow remains positive
after separating policy-timed safe-harbour revenue and manufacturing-credit sales. Broader European
growth or commercial-system adoption would reduce dependence on US residential demand.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Reject or downgrade the edge if financing and lost incentives keep residential demand structurally
lower, channel or company inventory requires discounting, safe-harbour commitments fail to become
end-customer installations, tariffs compress margin, battery attach rates stall, or warranty and
purchase obligations consume liquidity. The edge is also invalidated as an equity thesis if solar
and storage deployment grows but Enphase loses share or returns on capital deteriorate.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research operation is justified now. The market mark is current, but the security page
has no supportable fair-value range, US demand has not stabilised, reported revenue includes
policy-timed shipments, and the next quarterly result is imminent. Reconsider after Q2 evidence can
separate underlying sell-through, normalised margin, and free cash flow; do not turn product
exposure alone into an entry signal.
