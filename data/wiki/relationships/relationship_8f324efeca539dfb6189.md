# AI infrastructure and power bottlenecks to Powell Industries

## Endpoints and disposition

- Idea: [[ideas/idea_ai_infrastructure_power|AI infrastructure and power bottlenecks]]
- Security: [[securities/security_0b67d6c8e3fd3366a257|Powell Industries, Inc. common stock]]
- Relationship: `relationship_8f324efeca539dfb6189`
- Type: `beneficiary`
- Direction: **positive**
- Sensitivity: **high**
- Confidence: **medium**
- Status: **accepted**

Powell has a direct physical transmission path from higher-density compute to medium-voltage
switchgear, packaged electrical rooms, control, monitoring and protection equipment. The edge is
accepted because more than USD 800 million of data-center awards entered fiscal-2026 bookings,
including one order above USD 400 million. Acceptance confirms the causal relationship; it does not
make the shares attractive at the current price.

## Causal and economic test

| Test | Evidence | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does AI-power demand reach Powell products? | Data centers need medium-voltage distribution and protection between grid/on-site generation and compute loads; Powell reported specific awards for these systems. | Pass. |
| Is the exposure material? | More than USD 800m of fiscal-2026 data-center awards equals roughly one-third of the USD 2.4bn June backlog. | Pass at high sensitivity. |
| Is it diversified? | Electric Utility was 24% of backlog and Oil & Gas excluding petrochemical 24%, but one data-center order exceeded USD 400m and customer identities are undisclosed. | Partial; confidence remains medium. |
| Is demand becoming earnings and cash? | Q3 revenue grew 9%, gross margin reached 30.6%, and nine-month operating cash flow reached USD 195m. | Pass, with customer-advance and project-timing caveats. |
| Does the edge support a trade? | USD 181.17 is above the USD 180 base value and USD 144 buy-below price. | No; relationship accepted, valuation gate closed. |

## Confirmation and invalidation

Strengthen the relationship if data-center awards broaden across customers, sites and architectures;
Commercial & Other Industrial backlog converts into disclosed revenue and cash; and grid/LNG demand
keeps total book-to-bill above 1x without margin dilution. Better data-center revenue and customer
disclosure would raise confidence.

Reject or downgrade the edge if the >USD 400 million project is cancelled, materially delayed or
repriced; data-center awards fail to broaden; medium-voltage architecture or customer insourcing
reduces Powell content; backlog fails to convert to cash; or capacity constraints produce sustained
margin loss, damages or working-capital stress.
