# Terafab AI industrial stack to Samsung Electronics common GDR

## Decision

**Accepted — positive bridge beneficiary, medium sensitivity and low confidence.** Samsung has
relevant HBM, memory, foundry and packaging capabilities, but no Terafab-specific supply agreement
or volume has been disclosed.

## Causal mechanism

The [[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial stack]] and SpaceXAI require HBM,
server memory, foundry and packaging capacity while internal logic and memory are developed and
qualified. [[securities/security_567d0d575bbd30aaa91d|Samsung Electronics common GDR]] can receive
the economics through Nvidia-aligned HBM and an integrated memory, foundry and packaging stack.
Successful Terafab memory or logic production would eventually turn part of that bridge demand into
customer insourcing.

Sensitivity is **medium** because AI memory economics are currently important to Samsung and the
potential deployment is large. Confidence is **low** because current evidence establishes general
capability and Nvidia alignment—not a Terafab customer, product, price or volume. The Vienna GDR's
liquidity and valuation blockers remain instrument-specific and separate from the business edge.

## Evidence

- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  expects significant third-party sourcing during the Terafab ramp.
- Samsung's [GTC 2026 release](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-unveils-hbm4e-showcasing-comprehensive-ai-solutions-nvidia-partnership-and-vision-at-nvidia-gtc-2026)
  presents HBM4E and an Nvidia-aligned AI memory and semiconductor roadmap, supporting supplier
  capability without establishing a SpaceXAI or Terafab contract.
- Nvidia's [annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
  confirms dependence on external memory, foundry and packaging partners including Samsung.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are disclosed SpaceXAI or Terafab HBM, memory, foundry or packaging volume; qualified
Samsung content in relevant Nvidia systems; and continued external sourcing after internal phases
begin.

Contrary evidence is the absence of a named customer agreement and Terafab's ambition to internalize
memory, logic and packaging. Invalidate the edge if the deployment creates no material Samsung
content; Samsung loses relevant HBM or foundry qualifications; Terafab internalizes competitive
production faster than expected; or long-run displacement outweighs transition revenue.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after a named supply agreement, HBM qualification change, first
internal Terafab memory or logic, or material sourcing disclosure.
