# Critical minerals and the lithium cycle to Lithium Americas

This accepted edge identifies direct development-stage exposure to future lithium demand and price
economics. It is not a trading recommendation or current producer exposure: Lithium Americas is
pre-revenue, owns 62% of Thacker Pass, and must still convert construction, financing, offtake, and
commissioning milestones into cash flow attributable to the common shares.

## Causal mechanism

Growth in EV and stationary-storage demand, combined with delayed supply, can improve expected
long-run lithium pricing and the economics of Thacker Pass. Better expected pricing can raise the
project's operating-margin potential, support funding draws and construction, strengthen the value
of GM's long-term offtake, and increase the option value attributable to Lithium Americas' common
shares. The exposure is causal because Thacker Pass is a concentrated lithium project, not because
its share price happens to correlate with lithium benchmarks.

Sensitivity is high at the underlying project but attenuated and uncertain for common shareholders.
At 31 March 2026, USD 1.1 billion of the technical report's USD 2.93 billion Phase 1 capex estimate
had been spent, mechanical completion was targeted for late 2027, and production ramp was expected
in 2028. GM owns 38% of the joint venture; DOE funding conditions, restricted cash, offtake terms,
warrants, remaining capex, ramp risk, and observed ATM issuance determine how much project value
reaches the listed security.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if EV and stationary-storage demand remains durable; lithium-price
and offtake economics support the project; construction remains within the disclosed cost and
schedule; DOE and other funding draws satisfy their conditions; dilution stays bounded; mechanical
completion occurs in late 2027; and commissioning produces qualified battery-grade lithium on the
path to the planned 2028 ramp.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if supply restarts, new projects, recycling, sodium-ion adoption,
material efficiency, or weaker demand restores a durable surplus; lithium prices no longer support
Thacker Pass economics; capex rises materially; funding conditions fail; construction or
commissioning slips; product qualification or offtake economics disappoint; or dilution and
encumbering claims transfer project value away from existing common shareholders. A positive causal
project edge can remain accepted while unsupported common-equity valuation makes the security
uninvestable.
