This accepted edge is causal rather than a correlation: Recursion's computational models,
proprietary biological data and automated experiments are used to originate and advance drug
candidates. The exposure is central to the issuer, but present evidence establishes platform
activity rather than repeatable clinical or shareholder economics. Acceptance does not establish a
trade case.

The maintained [[relationships/relationship_9e7b4700174908755cbc|precision-biology relationship]]
shares the same security endpoint but is not an equivalent edge. That page tests a broader
healthcare-automation idea; this edge specifically tests whether AI-native discovery loops can
create drug-program value after clinical validation, partner bargaining, cash burn and dilution.

## Causal mechanism

AI models trained on Recursion's biological and chemical data prioritize hypotheses and compounds;
automated wet-lab experiments generate feedback for the next model-and-experiment cycle. If this
loop improves candidate selection, development time or success rates, Recursion can capture value
through owned-pipeline economics and partner milestones. Sensitivity is high because these platform
capabilities and platform-derived programs are central to the company rather than an incidental
revenue stream.

Transmission to common shareholders remains conditional. Candidates must survive toxicology,
clinical and regulatory validation, partnership terms must preserve material economics, and the
resulting value must exceed operating burn, data commitments, financing and dilution. Large pharma
can also internalize similar workflows, limiting Recursion's bargaining power even if the broader
idea succeeds.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Strengthen the edge if blinded REC-4881 efficacy and safety data and further REC-1245 dose-
escalation evidence support translation, FDA engagement produces clear development paths, partner
milestones become repeatable, measured cycle-time or success-rate improvements are disclosed, and
operating burn falls below guidance without financing that overwhelms per-share value. The next
quarterly filing and material program readouts are bounded review points.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if platform-derived programs fail safety or efficacy tests, discovery
timelines or costs do not improve against relevant baselines, candidates are repeatedly
deprioritized, partnership economics remain immaterial, or pharma internalizes the workflow. The
company-specific edge also fails if data obligations consume capital without measurable output or
financing and dilution absorb the platform's enterprise value. Broad adoption of AI in pharma does
not preserve this edge without Recursion-specific clinical and economic translation.
