# Nearshoring and friendshoring manufacturing capacity to Ternium

## Endpoints and disposition

- Idea: [[ideas/idea_nearshoring_friendshoring_manufacturing|Nearshoring and friendshoring manufacturing capacity]]
- Security: [[securities/security_2c779e81c27b78c556bb|Ternium S.A. ADS]]

- Sensitivity: **medium**
- Confidence: **medium**
- Status: **accepted**

This accepted edge identifies direct but diluted exposure to North American regional manufacturing
capacity. Ternium is spending heavily on USMCA-oriented steel capacity in Pesquería, Mexico, but
project commissioning, utilization, trade rules, steel pricing, cash conversion, and the issuer's
Brazilian and Argentine operations determine whether regionalization benefits ADS holders.

## Causal mechanism

Nearshoring and friendshoring can increase demand for locally compliant flat steel as manufacturers
regionalize production and qualify North American suppliers. Ternium's Pesquería downstream lines
and new DRI-EAF steel shop can serve that demand, raising Mexican shipments, utilization, revenue,
and margins if the facilities commission and customers adopt the output. The link is causal because
the issuer is deploying capital into regional steelmaking capacity designed for the integrated
North American market, not because its ADS price happens to correlate with nearshoring headlines.

Sensitivity is medium rather than high. Q1 2026 Pesquería-focused capex of USD 406 million makes the
investment material, and downstream facilities entered ramp-up, but Ternium has not disclosed
contracted nearshoring volumes, utilization, or project returns. Steel-cycle pricing, working
capital, Usiminas allocation, non-controlling interests, currencies, and operations outside Mexico
can dominate the regional-capacity contribution.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if the downstream lines achieve disclosed utilization and customer
qualification; the DRI-EAF shop commissions on the revised early-2027 schedule without material
cost escalation; USMCA review outcomes preserve workable regional rules; Mexican shipments and
margins rise; and operating cash flow covers remaining capex while attributable ADS-holder earnings
improve.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if USMCA or tariff changes fragment rather than reinforce regional
supply chains; manufacturers do not qualify or use Pesquería output; startup slips again; power,
water, logistics, or security constraints impede utilization; imports or weak end demand depress
steel pricing; or working capital, capex, Usiminas commitments, currencies, minority interests, and
non-Mexican operations absorb the regional benefit. An accepted operating edge can coexist with an
unattractive security valuation.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The current baseline assessment has an 8.7% base-case
downside and 48.9% downside case versus the market mark, while commissioning has slipped and project
returns remain undisclosed. Reconsider after Q2 results and updated Pesquería schedule, utilization,
cash-conversion, and cost-to-complete evidence, due for review by 2026-08-07.
