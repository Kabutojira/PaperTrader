# Terafab AI industrial stack to NVIDIA

## Decision

**Accepted — positive beneficiary, medium sensitivity and medium confidence.** The investable edge
is current SpaceXAI and orbital demand for Nvidia's integrated compute platform, not an unannounced
agreement for Terafab to manufacture Nvidia dies.

## Causal mechanism

The [[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial stack]] requires immediately
deployable general-purpose training, inference, networking and software while the proposed fab is
built and qualified. [[securities/security_33d9c44facc75c726c7d|NVIDIA]] can convert that demand
through Vera Rubin systems, Space-1 orbital modules, networking, interconnect and CUDA software into
revenue and cash flow. Longer term, qualified domestic packaging or selected support silicon could
improve supply diversity, but there is no disclosed Nvidia investment, capacity guarantee, process
port or main-die manufacturing agreement.

Sensitivity is **medium** because a very large SpaceXAI deployment can be economically meaningful,
but Nvidia's business is diversified across hyperscalers, enterprises, sovereign customers and
other industries. Terafab also creates a long-run substitution risk if SpaceX and Tesla internalize
inference or support silicon.

## Evidence

- Nvidia's [space-computing release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/space-computing) introduced
  a Vera Rubin-derived module for orbital deployments, establishing a product channel aligned with
  the idea.
- Nvidia's [Vera release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-unveils-vera-the-cpu-for-agents)
  names SpaceXAI among organizations planning to adopt the platform.
- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  says significant third-party compute sourcing is expected to continue while Terafab develops
  internal capability.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are disclosed SpaceXAI system orders, successful orbital deployment, recurring Nvidia
networking and software content, a co-designed space product, or a binding package, chiplet or
capacity agreement with measurable economics.

Contrary evidence is that current product announcements do not quantify SpaceXAI revenue and do not
bind Terafab manufacturing. Invalidate the positive edge if SpaceXAI deployment is immaterial,
cancelled or moves materially to internal or competing accelerators; if orbital economics fail; if
export, supply or power constraints prevent delivery; or if customer-designed silicon erodes more
Nvidia revenue than Terafab-related demand creates.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after a binding Nvidia–SpaceX agreement, quantified deployment,
first orbital shipment, or a disclosed custom-silicon substitution.
