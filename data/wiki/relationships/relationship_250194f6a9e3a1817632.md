# Anduril product-company disruption to Palantir Technologies

## Endpoints and disposition

- Idea: [[ideas/idea_anduril_product_company_disruption|Anduril product-company disruption and future-IPO exposure]]
- Security: [[securities/security_bdc2f87dadf134760c3a|Palantir Technologies Inc. Class A common stock]]
- Relationship: `relationship_250194f6a9e3a1817632`
- Type: `beneficiary`
- Direction: **positive**
- Sensitivity: **low**
- Confidence: **low**
- Status: **rejected**

The operational association is real, but the proposed public-proxy edge is rejected. Anduril uses
Palantir Warp Speed and the companies participate in the Army TITAN ecosystem, creating a possible
positive demand channel. Current evidence does not quantify Anduril-derived Palantir revenue,
margin, contract duration, concentration, or incremental cash flow. That prevents a defensible claim
that success of the Anduril product-company model is material to Palantir common-stock economics.
Rejection does not dispute Palantir's broader business quality; it rejects Anduril as a sufficiently
supported explanation for owning the security.

## Causal mechanism test

If Anduril scales production and deploys common software across more programs, it could buy more
Warp Speed software or create joint-program pull-through for Palantir. Palantir would benefit only
if those activities produce durable, incremental, and economically material revenue and cash flow.
The direction would be positive, but sensitivity is low because Palantir's reported growth spans a
much wider U.S. government and commercial business, while no source isolates Anduril economics.

The maintained [[ideas/idea_anduril_product_company_disruption|idea review]] also separates
Anduril's private procurement validation from investable public exposure. The maintained
[[securities/security_bdc2f87dadf134760c3a|Palantir review]] records exceptional company-wide
growth and cash generation, but explicitly treats the Anduril connection as economically
unquantified. Association and ecosystem participation alone do not pass the relationship gate.

## Catalyst and reconsideration

Reconsider acceptance if Palantir discloses material Anduril-derived revenue, contract duration,
software attachment, margins, or cash flow; Anduril production awards generate measurable recurring
Warp Speed demand; or joint programs award Palantir identifiable economics that are material
relative to its wider business. The next bounded review is 2026-08-14, after fresher Palantir
quarterly evidence, or sooner upon a material contract disclosure.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The candidate channel fails if Anduril internalizes the relevant software stack, selects competing
infrastructure, joint programs do not award Palantir material economics, or Warp Speed remains an
incidental customer relationship. Continued omission of Anduril exposure after relevant reporting
would also support immateriality rather than a hidden proxy thesis. Palantir's independent growth
would not validate this edge.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The relationship is rejected, Anduril materiality is
unquantified, and the maintained security assessment reports a 60.4% downside case and 40.4% base-
case downside at the current mark. Reconsider only after primary evidence establishes material
transmission and a downside-aware valuation passes the margin-of-safety gate.
