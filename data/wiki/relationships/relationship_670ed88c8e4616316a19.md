This accepted edge identifies direct operating exposure to lithium-concentrate demand, realized
pricing, and production. It is not a trading recommendation: acute liquidity, debt, negative working
capital, receivable collection, offtake prepayments, controls, environmental proceedings, and
expansion funding can prevent favorable lithium conditions from reaching common shareholders.

## Causal mechanism

EV and stationary-storage demand can tighten lithium markets and support concentrate prices and
volumes. Sigma's producing Grota do Cirilo hard-rock operation sells lithium concentrate, so stronger
lithium demand and realized pricing can increase revenue, mine utilization, margins, operating cash
generation, and the option value of Phase 2 capacity. The Q1 2026 accounts show that the exposure is
financially material: Sigma reported USD 42.3 million of revenue and USD 16.7 million of adjusted
EBITDA from its lithium operation.

Sensitivity is high but shareholder transmission is impaired. Q1 production and sales fell to 23.6
kt during mine restructuring, operating cash flow was negative USD 2.7 million, cash was only USD
3.9 million, loans and export prepayment totaled USD 133.9 million, and negative working capital was
USD 144.5 million. Second-quarter production improved to 35 kt, 6% above guidance, but that single
operating update did not refresh liquidity or prove cash conversion.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if lithium-concentrate demand and realized pricing remain durable;
Sigma sustains production and recovery; receivables convert to cash; operating cash flow turns
positive; debt and offtake obligations remain serviceable; Phase 2 obtains non-dilutive committed
funding; and controls and environmental matters are remediated. Reassess after Q2 accounts or a
material financing, cash-collection, production, pricing, regulatory, or balance-sheet update.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if supply restarts, new projects, recycling, sodium-ion adoption,
material efficiency, or weaker EV and storage demand restores surplus; realized pricing or volumes
fall; output or recovery remains unstable; receivables do not convert; supplier, debt, or prepayment
claims tighten liquidity; environmental restrictions impair operations; Phase 2 requires deeply
dilutive funding; or lithium economics cease to provide positive operating leverage. Even while the
edge remains causal, financing and solvency outcomes can dominate commodity upside for shareholders.
