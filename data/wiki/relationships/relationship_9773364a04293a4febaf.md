# Critical minerals and the lithium cycle to SQM

This accepted edge identifies direct operating exposure to lithium demand, realized prices, and
volumes. It is not a trading recommendation: Chilean state take, partnership attribution, contract
pricing, permits, capital spending, minority interests, debt, and commodity cyclicality can prevent
strong lithium conditions from accruing proportionately to ADS holders.

## Causal mechanism

Growth in EV and stationary-storage demand can tighten lithium markets and raise realized prices.
SQM produces lithium products through Chilean brine operations and Australian hard-rock and
conversion assets, so higher lithium prices and volumes can increase revenue, gross profit,
earnings, cash generation, and the option value of future capacity. Q1 2026 shows this transmission:
lithium-equivalent sales volume rose 25% to 69 thousand tonnes, realized Chilean lithium price rose
about 95% to USD 17.8/kg, and lithium supplied 75% of consolidated gross profit.

Sensitivity is high but not one-for-one. SQM also owns iodine and specialty-plant-nutrition
businesses, and Nova Andino Litio's partnership and state-payment economics affect how lithium gross
profit reaches the listed ADS. More than USD 530 million was paid to the Chilean state in Q1, while
Salar Futuro permitting, Kwinana ramp execution, capital spending, long-term debt, and minority
interests remain material transmission gates.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if EV and stationary-storage demand remains durable; SQM sustains
higher realized lithium prices and volumes; earnings convert to cash after Chilean state payments,
tax, and capex; Kwinana approaches nameplate; Salar Futuro permitting advances; and debt remains
controlled. Reassess after Q2 primary evidence or another material lithium-price, supply, permitting,
partnership, cash-conversion, or balance-sheet change.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if Chinese or other idled supply restarts, new projects, recycling,
sodium-ion adoption, material efficiency, or weaker EV and storage demand restores surplus;
realized prices or volumes reverse; Chilean partnership economics or state take worsens; Salar
Futuro or Australian conversion slips; capex, tax, debt, or minority interests absorb operating
gains; or lithium ceases to be financially material to SQM. An accepted positive operating edge can
coexist with an unattractive or unsupported ADS valuation.
