# Terafab AI industrial stack to Intel

## Decision

**Rejected — hypothetical positive beneficiary, low sensitivity and low confidence.** Intel's
capabilities fit the project, but a proposed contribution without binding scope or measurable
economics is not an accepted causal edge to Intel common stock.

## Causal mechanism assessed

[[securities/security_dfa34d4b9050964b465e|Intel]] could provide process integration,
fabrication, advanced packaging, IP or manufacturing know-how to the
[[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial stack]]. A binding arrangement could
create service fees, utilization, licensing or foundry revenue and could validate Intel's external-
customer turnaround. The current evidence does not identify a process node, package, tool set,
capacity, work scope, ownership, price, IP terms, milestones, revenue, margin or cash flow.

Sensitivity is therefore **low** and confidence **low**. Intel's current economics are dominated by
its product franchise, internal foundry utilization, advanced-node roadmap and broader external-
foundry execution. A named prospective framework cannot yet alter those economics.

## Evidence

- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  calls Intel's Terafab contribution proposed and states that the framework may not result in a
  definitive agreement.
- Intel's maintained security research, grounded in its Q2 filing, reports only USD 293 million of
  quarterly third-party foundry and assembly-and-test revenue and shows that capital discipline and
  external-customer wins remain central to the turnaround.
- Intel's [Q1 results release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/50863/000005086326000077/q126earningsrelease.htm)
  supports the broader process and packaging capability, but does not establish Terafab economics.

## Upgrade gates, contrary evidence and invalidation

Upgrade this edge only after a definitive Intel–SpaceX agreement identifies the technology or service
scope and links it to measurable fees, utilization, revenue, margin, cash flow or a material qualified
product. Process and packaging milestones should be independently observable.

The present contrary evidence is the explicit possibility that no definitive agreement follows.
The hypothesis fails if Intel contributes no material technology or services; terms are immaterial or
uneconomic; milestones slip; IP or governance conflicts block execution; or Intel continues to report
no measurable Terafab economics. Rejection prevents a named participant from becoming an investment
edge before shareholder transmission is established.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or immediately after definitive Intel–Terafab terms or an Intel filing with
material project economics.
