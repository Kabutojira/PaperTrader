This accepted edge identifies a direct path from regulated crypto activity, stablecoin adoption,
custody, and tokenized settlement to Coinbase revenue and cash generation. The edge does not establish
a current investment case: transaction cyclicality, rate and counterparty exposure, custody risk,
stock-based compensation, and an unsupported per-share valuation remain material limits.

## Causal mechanism

Clearer rules and broader adoption can increase regulated trading, custody, stablecoin balances,
payments, financing, and on-chain activity. Coinbase can monetize those flows through transaction
fees, stablecoin revenue, subscriptions and services, institutional products, custody, and its Base
network. Exposure is high because these activities are core platform economics rather than an
incidental end market.

Shareholder transmission is conditional. Lower crypto prices or volumes can reduce transaction
revenue; lower interest rates or weaker partner terms can compress stablecoin economics; competition
can reduce take rates; and custody, cyber, credit, or regulatory failures can create losses. Revenue
concentration and stock-based compensation can also prevent platform growth from becoming durable
per-share cash generation.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Strengthen the edge if later primary evidence shows stablecoin balances and non-speculative payment
usage growing despite lower rates, recurring subscription and custody revenue expanding, transaction
share remaining resilient without unsustainable take rates, and GAAP cash earnings persisting through
a crypto drawdown. Confirmation also requires lower counterparty concentration, strong custody and
cyber controls, and dilution-adjusted cash generation.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if adoption remains predominantly speculative, crypto activity
contracts without resilient recurring revenue, falling rates or adverse partner terms overwhelm
stablecoin balance growth, competitors or incumbents capture the economics, or fees compress
structurally. The company-specific edge fails after a material custody, cyber, credit, or regulatory
loss, or if stock-based compensation and dilution prevent operating growth from reaching shareholders.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The current security assessment is ineligible because
a supportable downside and base per-share valuation is unavailable, while transaction cyclicality,
counterparty concentration, custody exposure, and dilution remain unresolved. Reconsider after Q2
primary evidence and a downside-aware valuation that satisfies the configured margin-of-safety gate.
