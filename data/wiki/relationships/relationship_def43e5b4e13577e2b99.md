This accepted edge identifies direct operating exposure to copper scarcity and pricing. It is not a
trading recommendation: mine disruption, costs, capital requirements, minority interests and
jurisdictional terms can prevent commodity strength from reaching common shareholders.

## Causal mechanism

Structural copper scarcity can raise realized copper prices and support margins for a producer with
large copper sales. Freeport's Q2 2026 results show that transmission directly: it realized USD
6.17 per pound of copper against a USD 1.97 per pound unit net cash cost while generating USD 2.048
billion of operating cash flow. Copper is produced across the Americas and Indonesia, so scarcity
can also increase the value of reserves and brownfield growth options.

The relationship is highly sensitive but not one-for-one. Q2 copper production fell to 786 million
pounds from 963 million and sales fell to 710 million pounds from 1,016 million even as stronger
pricing supported earnings. Grasberg restoration, capital spending, Indonesian operating rights,
minority interests and by-product credits determine how much copper-price upside accrues to the
listed common stock.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if copper prices remain supported while safe Grasberg restoration,
Americas leach initiatives and brownfield projects lift attributable sales; unit costs remain
controlled; operating cash flow exceeds sustaining and growth capital needs; and final Indonesian
rights preserve value for common shareholders.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if new mine supply, recycling, substitution or weaker industrial demand
removes scarcity; Freeport's production recovery stalls; unit costs, restoration costs or project
capex absorb pricing gains; Indonesian fiscal or ownership terms worsen; safety incidents recur; or
attributable free cash flow fails to rise with realized copper prices. The causal operating edge can
remain positive while normalized valuation makes the equity unattractive.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The current baseline assessment has a 24.5% base-case
downside and 48.0% downside case versus the market mark, below the configured margin-of-safety gate.
Reconsider after material Grasberg, Indonesian-rights, cost, copper-balance or valuation evidence.
