This accepted edge identifies a direct path from rising data-centre electricity demand and grid
constraints to GE Vernova's generation and electrification equipment, services, orders, backlog,
revenue, margins, and cash flow. Q2 2026 Power and Electrification demand, data-centre orders,
backlog, margins, and cash generation confirm issuer-specific transmission. Delivery schedules,
customer concentration, backlog quality, working-capital normalization, Wind execution, capacity
investment, acquisition integration, and valuation remain material limits, so the edge does not
establish a current investment case.

## Causal mechanism

Higher-density compute and broader electrification increase demand for reliable generation,
interconnection, grid equipment, and services. GE Vernova can convert that demand through gas
turbines and associated services in Power and through grid equipment and systems in
Electrification. Q2 2026 provides direct evidence: orders reached USD 24.2 billion, total backlog
reached USD 176 billion, data-centre orders exceeded USD 5 billion year to date, adjusted EBITDA
margin reached 11.3%, and first-half free cash flow reached USD 5.1 billion.

Sensitivity is high because the relevant demand is already visible in GE Vernova's orders and
backlog, including 116 GW of gas-equipment backlog and slot reservations, and because more than 55%
of total backlog is services. Shareholder transmission remains conditional: project power and
permitting constraints can defer deliveries, capacity expansion can reduce scarcity economics,
customer over-ordering can reverse backlog, and first-half cash benefited from working capital.
Growth benefits the common shares only if backlog converts into durable revenue, margin, and cash
before capacity, competition, execution costs, or demand normalization erodes returns.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Strengthen the edge if later primary evidence shows Power and Electrification orders remain broad,
data-centre and utility projects proceed on schedule, backlog converts into revenue, service mix and
margins remain durable, free cash flow normalizes after working-capital benefits, and capacity
investment earns acceptable returns. Confirmation requires customer concentration, cancellation
rates, and slot reservations to remain controlled rather than demand merely reflecting temporary
over-ordering.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if data-centre or utility projects are cancelled or delayed, compute
efficiency or architecture changes reduce power-equipment demand, customers add competing supply or
internal generation, capacity expansion causes price or utilization deterioration, or Power and
Electrification orders fail to become revenue and normalized cash. The common-share relationship
also weakens if Wind losses absorb gains, warranties or tariffs pressure margins, acquisitions or
capacity projects miss returns, customer concentration rises, or backlog undergoes a material
correction.
