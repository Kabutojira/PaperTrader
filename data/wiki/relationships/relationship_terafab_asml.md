# Terafab AI industrial stack to ASML

## Decision

**Accepted — positive beneficiary, medium sensitivity and medium confidence.** Any credible
Terafab move into advanced logic or memory requires production lithography, software and service;
the edge does not assume that a Terafab-specific ASML order has already been placed.

## Causal mechanism

The advanced-wafer phases of the [[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial
stack]] require patterning systems, process-control software, upgrades and field service.
[[securities/security_ef35e41886220d51c22c|ASML]] can convert a qualified ramp into system revenue
and recurring installed-base economics. Packaging, test or mature-node phases would have much lower
ASML content, so the relationship depends on Terafab disclosing a process and actually accepting
advanced lithography tools.

Sensitivity is **medium** because a large greenfield campus can require multiple systems and years
of service, but ASML already serves a diversified global logic and memory customer base. The edge is
incremental rather than essential to ASML's thesis.

## Evidence

- Texas project records describe proposed semiconductor fabrication and advanced packaging, but do
  not identify a process node, scanner configuration or ASML purchase order.
- ASML's [Q1 2026 results](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/q1-2026-financial-results)
  describe AI-driven leading-edge Logic and Memory demand, supporting the general transmission from
  advanced capacity to lithography and service economics.
- NIST's [xLight award](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/06/department-commerce-announces-finalization-chips-incentives-xlight-support)
  funds a prototype free-electron-laser source at Albany NanoTech. It is neither a production
  scanner nor a disclosed Terafab replacement for ASML systems.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are a named process node, binding ASML tool orders, site acceptance, first wafers and
recurring installed-base service or upgrade revenue. A measurable order and delivery schedule would
raise confidence.

Contrary evidence is that the campus is prospective and may begin with packaging, test or selected
silicon rather than leading-edge wafers. Invalidate the edge if advanced-wafer phases are cancelled
or indefinitely delayed; no ASML tools are ordered; export, permitting or project economics block
delivery; or an alternative lithography route is qualified at competitive production scale.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after a named process, lithography purchase order, first-tool
delivery or material xLight production evidence.
