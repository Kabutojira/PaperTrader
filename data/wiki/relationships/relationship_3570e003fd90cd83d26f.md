This accepted causal edge identifies direct operating exposure to procedure automation. It is not a
trading recommendation and does not imply that every robotic procedure improves outcomes or lowers
total healthcare cost.

## Causal mechanism

Healthcare providers adopting robotically assisted procedures use Intuitive's installed da Vinci
and Ion systems. Higher system availability and procedure utilization drive demand for instruments
and accessories used with procedures, as well as service revenue. The mechanism is observable in Q2
2026: combined procedures grew about 16%, the da Vinci installed base grew 12% to 11,710, the Ion
base grew 21% to 1,096, and instruments and accessories revenue rose 18% to USD 1.73 billion.

The equity benefit remains conditional. Adoption must convert through recurring revenue, operating
margin and cash while preserving safety, training quality and clinical value. Q2 revenue grew 19%
and GAAP operating income grew 31%, supporting current positive sensitivity, but this evidence does
not prove universal clinical superiority or favorable economics at every hospital.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Confirm and strengthen the edge if procedure growth remains durable, da Vinci 5 and Ion utilization
expand, recurring instruments and service revenue grows with the installed base, and operating
margin remains resilient after tariffs and supply costs. Evidence of improved outcomes, efficient
training and favorable hospital economics would strengthen the broader healthcare-automation claim.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if procedure utilization or installed-base growth stalls, instruments
and service revenue decouples from adoption, hospital capital budgets defer placements, safety or
training evidence weakens, reimbursement or regulation constrains use, competitors erode installed-
base returns, or tariffs and sole-source supply constraints prevent growth from converting to cash.
The operating relationship can remain positive while an excessive valuation makes the common stock
unattractive.

## Strategy gate

No strategy research is justified by this relationship review. The current security assessment has
only 2.5% base-case upside versus a configured 20% margin-of-safety target and a 23.2% downside case.
Reconsider after Q3 evidence or a material valuation reset; do not create a signal from procedure
growth alone.
