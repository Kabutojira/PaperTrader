# Digital attention, gaming, and consumer ecosystems to Prosus

## Decision

**Accepted — positive beneficiary, high sensitivity and medium confidence.** Prosus has material
indirect exposure through Tencent and direct private-platform exposure, but the holding-company
discount, debt, governance and capital allocation mediate what reaches PRX shareholders.

## Mechanism and evidence

[[ideas/idea_digital_attention_gaming_ecosystems|Digital attention, gaming, and consumer ecosystems]]
reaches [[securities/security_8b703a8adf5f864acaa4|Prosus]] mainly through its Tencent stake and
also through ecommerce platforms. Prosus reported USD150.4 billion of NAV and EUR61.5 NAV per share
on August 27, of which Tencent represented USD117.0 billion. Repurchases below NAV have reduced
roughly 30% of free float and added about 18% to NAV per share, establishing a real shareholder-
transmission mechanism rather than mere correlated exposure.

Sensitivity is high because Tencent dominates look-through NAV. Confidence is medium because
private marks, net debt, governance, cross-holdings and the discount can overwhelm operating value.
The accepted edge does not by itself resolve current downside or concentration gates.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Catalysts are Tencent and private-platform value growth, broader ecommerce profitability,
controlled debt, transparent NAV and accretive repurchases. Invalidate if operating exposure becomes
immaterial after debt, Tencent or private-platform economics deteriorate, governance blocks value
transmission, or the discount widens persistently despite underlying value creation.
