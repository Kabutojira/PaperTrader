# Terafab AI industrial stack to TSMC

## Decision

**Accepted — positive bridge beneficiary, medium sensitivity and medium confidence.** Terafab's
staged ramp extends external leading-node and packaging demand before creating a conditional long-run
insourcing threat.

## Causal mechanism

SpaceXAI and Tesla need current compute while the
[[ideas/idea_terafab_ai_industrial_stack|Terafab AI industrial stack]] is built, equipped and
qualified. [[securities/security_ce9b78a4d0773c950765|TSMC]] can monetize the transition through
leading-node wafers and advanced packaging used by Nvidia and customer-designed silicon. The SpaceX
prospectus explicitly expects significant third-party sourcing to continue, supporting the bridge.

Sensitivity is **medium**: unusually large AI demand can affect advanced-node utilization and
packaging, but neither SpaceXAI nor Terafab-specific TSMC volume is disclosed and TSMC serves a broad
customer base. If Terafab ultimately qualifies competitive leading-edge logic and packaging, the
same idea changes from bridge demand into partial customer insourcing.

## Evidence

- The [SpaceX prospectus](https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX%20-%20EU%20Prospectus%20%28Approved%20by%20Bafin%29%20-%20June%205%2C%202026.pdf)
  expects significant third-party compute sourcing to continue while internal capability develops.
- Nvidia's [annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
  confirms dependence on external foundry, memory and packaging partners including TSMC, linking
  current Nvidia-led demand to TSMC production.
- TSMC's [2025 annual report](https://investor.tsmc.com/static/annualReports/2025/english/index.html)
  and maintained Q2 review support leading-node, advanced-packaging and manufacturing-scale
  capability; neither establishes a Terafab contract.

## Catalysts, contrary evidence and invalidation

Catalysts are disclosed TSMC-fabricated SpaceXAI, Tesla or Nvidia volumes; advanced-packaging
capacity expansion tied to the demand; and sustained external sourcing after internal Terafab
phases begin. A direct foundry or packaging commitment would raise confidence.

Contrary evidence is the project's explicit ambition to internalize logic and packaging. Invalidate
the positive bridge if demand is immaterial or moves away from TSMC-fabricated products; Terafab
qualifies competitive leading-edge production much faster than expected; TSMC loses relevant
designs; or long-run insourcing outweighs incremental ramp revenue.

Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after a named supply agreement, first qualified internal product,
material customer-design change or TSMC disclosure of relevant demand.
