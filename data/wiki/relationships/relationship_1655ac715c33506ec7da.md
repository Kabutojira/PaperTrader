# Defense and aerospace replenishment cycle to RTX

This accepted edge identifies a direct but diversified path from allied replenishment and defense
modernization to RTX's operating economics. Raytheon converts missile, air-defense, naval, sensor,
and related demand into sales and backlog, while Collins and Pratt add military content and
aftermarket exposure. RTX is not a pure defense instrument: commercial aerospace, engine
remediation, debt, contract execution, and valuation materially dilute shareholder transmission.

## Causal mechanism

Higher funded budgets and stockpile replenishment can become awards for Patriot, Standard Missile,
AMRAAM, naval, sensor, and engine programs. RTX can convert those awards through qualified
production, deliveries, and sustainment into revenue, segment profit, operating cash flow, and
backlog. Q2 2026 provides company-specific confirmation: defense backlog reached USD 119 billion,
Raytheon sales rose 18%, and Raytheon adjusted operating margin rose to 12.6% from 11.6%.

Sensitivity is medium at the common-share level. Defense backlog is substantial but represented USD
119 billion of RTX's USD 289 billion total backlog; USD 170 billion was commercial. Collins and
Pratt therefore expose the same equity to commercial aviation, original-equipment mix, aftermarket
demand, and Pratt's powder-metal inspection program. Volume benefits shareholders only if pricing,
productivity, mix, and learning exceed labor, material, fixed-price, warranty, and remediation costs.

## Catalyst and confirmation

Strengthen the edge if funded missile, air-defense, naval, sensor, and engine awards lift qualified
output; defense backlog converts into deliveries; Raytheon margins and group free cash flow improve;
and Pratt remediation costs decline without new quality failures. Confirmation requires inventory,
receivables, contract assets, and fixed-price provisions to remain controlled while capacity expands.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

Downgrade or reject the edge if defense budgets fail to become funded awards, replenishment urgency
fades, export approvals or supplier constraints prevent delivery, production expansion creates
quality failures, or fixed-price charges absorb volume. The common-share relationship also weakens
if commercial aerospace declines, Pratt remediation liabilities rise, leverage constrains capital
allocation, or defense growth fails to produce sustained group-level free cash flow.

## Strategy gate

No strategy-research follow-up is justified. The maintained security assessment is baseline
eligible, but both bounded valuation scenarios remain below the current mark and overbought market
conditions provide no margin of safety. Reconsider after material award and cash-conversion evidence,
engine-liability improvement, or a valuation that satisfies the configured margin-of-safety gate.
