## Economics and thesis

The Q2 filing and current public trial registry advance but do not validate the platform-to-clinic
thesis. REC-4881 study NCT05552755 and REC-1245 study NCT06678659 are recruiting. REC-7735 received
IND clearance for a planned second-half 2026 Phase 1/2 start, Genentech moved a first neuroscience
target into joint early discovery, additional REC-4881 Phase 2 data are scheduled for 2 November,
and further REC-1245 dose-escalation data remain expected in the second half. These milestones do
not yet prove approval, comparative efficacy, commercial value, or a better industry-wide
probability of success.

At June 30, cash and cash equivalents were USD 545.7 million and cash including restricted cash was
USD 556.8 million, down from USD 753.9 million at year-end. Six-month operating cash use was USD
187.0 million; Q2 revenue was USD 7.7 million and net loss USD 131.0 million. Management reduced
2026 cash operating-expense guidance to below USD 375 million and retained runway guidance into
early 2028 without financing, while the 10-Q still expects substantial future losses and likely
additional capital. Shares outstanding increased to 534.1 million at June 30 and 535.3 million by
July 31, keeping dilution central to common-share value.
