## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 produced early platform-to-clinic evidence: REC-1245 showed predictable pharmacokinetics and
no dose-limiting toxicity reported to date, REC-4881 produced a Phase 2 signal with FDA engagement
underway, and REC-4539 dosed its first patient. These are development observations, not proof of
approval, comparative efficacy, commercial value, or a better industry-wide probability of success.

Cash and restricted cash fell from USD 754 million to USD 665 million. Revenue was USD 6.5 million,
operating cash use USD 81.1 million, and net loss USD 117.5 million. Management guides to less than
USD 390 million 2026 operational burn and runway into early 2028 without financing, while the 10-Q
states that additional capital will likely be required. Tempus data commitments total up to USD 160
million in cash or equity, and 530 million Class A and B shares were outstanding in May.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable valuation is recorded. A risk-adjusted pipeline model requires blinded clinical data,
development costs and timing, program-specific probabilities, milestone and royalty terms, cash
burn, Tempus obligations, and a fully diluted share count. Platform rhetoric is not assigned value
without measured improvements in cycle time or clinical success.

Catalysts are REC-4881 FDA-path clarity and data, REC-1245 escalation data, partner milestones, and
burn below guidance. Invalidate if safety or efficacy disappoints, programs are deprioritized,
partnership revenue stays immaterial, burn accelerates, data obligations consume capital, or funding
causes heavy dilution.
