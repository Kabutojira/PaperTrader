## Thesis and business economics

Recursion uses proprietary biological and chemical datasets, computational models, and automated laboratories to discover and design drug candidates. Economic value can reach shareholders through wholly owned medicines or through partner-funded programs, milestones, and royalties. The platform can therefore create valuable candidates without Recursion funding every program through commercialization, but discovery speed alone is not sufficient: candidates must demonstrate acceptable safety and efficacy, obtain regulatory approval, produce commercial cash flow, and create value exceeding corporate spending and dilution.

The October update strengthens evidence that programs are moving from platform output into clinical and partner development. It does not yet establish repeatable approval or commercial economics.

## Current clinical evidence

### REC-4881

In the issuer's Phase 1b/2 FAP update, 87% of evaluated patients had a reduction in total gastrointestinal polyp burden after 12 weeks and the median reduction was 47%. For upper-GI disease, 92% had a reduction and the median was 55%; 92% remained below baseline after the 12-week off-treatment period. Six of 12 patients improved by at least one Spigelman stage.

The signal is encouraging but preliminary. The release describes a small, single-arm dataset rather than a controlled registrational result. Four patients discontinued because of adverse events, although none of those discontinuations was attributed to a Grade 3 treatment-related event. Recursion targets registrational-trial initiation in the second half of 2027, subject to FDA discussions. Trial design, required endpoints, duration, cost, and approval probability remain unresolved.

### REC-1245

The early DAHLIA dose-escalation dataset included two confirmed partial responses and one unconfirmed response among heavily pretreated patients. At doses of at least 45 mg, the reported disease-control rate was 11 of 12. Recursion selected 45 mg for expansion, with preliminary endometrial-cancer expansion data expected in the second half of 2027.

This is a useful activity signal, not a valuation-ready efficacy estimate. The response evidence comes from very few patients in an early, open-label dose-escalation setting and may change with larger cohorts and longer follow-up.

## Partnerships

Sanofi optioned an oral immunology development candidate and is expected to fund and lead further development and commercialization. The option generated a USD 10 million milestone, bringing reported upfront and milestone receipts under that collaboration to USD 144 million. For this program, Recursion reports eligibility for more than USD 300 million of additional milestones and average low-double-digit royalties on net sales if approved.

The option is evidence that a partner found one program sufficiently attractive to advance. It does not establish the timing or probability of future milestone payments, approval, sales, or royalties. The August update separately reported that Genentech advanced a neuroscience target into joint early discovery, further supporting partner engagement while remaining far from clinical validation.

## Liquidity, burn, and dilution

Recursion reported approximately USD 561 million of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash at September 30, 2026. That amount was preliminary and unaudited. Including expected net proceeds from the concurrent offering, management extended expected operating runway into 2029.

The offering priced 61.54 million new Class A shares at USD 3.25 per share for approximately USD 200 million of gross proceeds before underwriting discounts and expenses. This materially improves liquidity and funds additional clinical milestones, but it also demonstrates that dilution is an active component of the business model. The offering price is a financing observation, not evidence of intrinsic value.

The prior August update reported reduced 2026 cash operating-expense guidance below USD 375 million. Even with the longer runway, Recursion is not self-funding, and the evidence does not establish that current cash will carry the internal pipeline through approval and commercialization.

## Valuation assessment

**Research status: valuation unsupported.**

Risk-adjusted NPV is the appropriate method for a clinical-stage biotechnology company. A defensible per-share model would require, for each material program:

1. technical, regulatory, and approval probabilities;
2. final trial designs, timing, and remaining development costs;
3. eligible treated populations, net pricing, penetration, and treatment duration;
4. launch timing, margins, commercialization expense, and exclusivity duration;
5. milestone and royalty timing under each partnership;
6. corporate cash use, future financing, and obligations; and
7. a fully diluted share count through the valuation horizon.

The current releases provide preliminary clinical observations, selected partnership terms, approximate liquidity, and the immediate offering share count. They do not bound the remaining inputs. Cash cannot be treated as freely distributable because continued development consumes capital, and neither the USD 3.25 offering price nor a market quotation is a fair-value substitute.

## Downside and contrary evidence

- Interim and preliminary clinical findings may not predict larger or registrational studies.
- REC-4881's regulatory path remains dependent on FDA discussions.
- REC-1245 activity is based on a very small early-stage cohort.
- Treatment-related adverse events and discontinuations matter for therapies intended for repeated or chronic use.
- Partners control important advancement decisions, and contingent milestones may never be earned.
- The 61.54 million-share offering extends runway while immediately diluting existing shareholders.
- Continued research spending or slower development could require additional financing before commercialization.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Research catalysts include disclosure of the FDA-aligned REC-4881 registrational design, maturation of REC-4881 follow-up, additional REC-1245 monotherapy and expansion data, initiation of the Sanofi candidate in Phase 1, further partner milestones, and audited financial statements showing post-offering liquidity, cash use, and capitalization.

The constructive thesis would be invalidated or materially weakened if REC-4881's effect fails to reproduce in a larger regulator-acceptable trial, chronic safety prevents sustained dosing, regulatory requirements materially delay development, REC-1245 responses disappear in expansion cohorts, partner programs stall or are returned, collaboration receipts remain small relative to spending, or dilution outpaces program-value creation.

Valuation support requires primary evidence that bounds the missing clinical probabilities, costs, timelines, commercial assumptions, financing needs, and future share count. Until then, scenario values should not be manufactured.

## Conclusion

The October evidence improves confidence that Recursion can generate clinically active and partner-selected programs, while simultaneously showing that common-share value remains dependent on early clinical outcomes and external capital. Confidence is **low** because the most important valuation inputs remain unbounded. Reassess by 2026-11-17 or when regulator-aligned REC-4881 trial requirements, larger clinical datasets, audited post-offering financials, or other material primary evidence becomes available.
