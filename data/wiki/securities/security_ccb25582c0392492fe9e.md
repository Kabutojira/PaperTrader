# Evonik Industries AG — Security Research

## Research update

This is the first research page for the security. It establishes the share identity, reviews the latest available Q2/H1 2026 evidence, and introduces a 12-month bear/base/bull valuation. The main valuation restraint is that management identified a material portion of Q2 strength as a supply-disruption windfall rather than a structural improvement.

## Identity reconciliation

Evonik reports **466,000,000 registered no-par-value shares**, ISIN **DE000EVNK013**, ticker **EVK**, trading in Frankfurt’s Prime Standard. Its share page also supplies Xetra Reuters and Bloomberg identifiers. This supports the issuer, share class, ticker and EUR valuation currency. The issuer does not state the literal MIC `XETR` or Yahoo symbol `EVK.DE`, so those provider fields retain modest reconciliation uncertainty and have not been changed. ## Business and current economics

Evonik’s continuing operations combine Custom Solutions—principally additives, care and health-care applications—with Advanced Technologies, including inorganics, specialty organics and Animal Nutrition. The economics depend on specialty positioning and customer-specific performance, but results remain exposed to industrial demand, utilization, raw-material conditions, currencies and product cycles.

Q2 2026 sales rose 11% to **€3.893 billion**, with both volume and price up 7%. Adjusted EBITDA increased 24% to **€630 million**, producing a 16.2% margin. Advanced Technologies benefited from stronger Animal Nutrition prices and constrained Asian competition, while Custom Solutions also improved. Management raised full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance to **€2.0–€2.2 billion** and retained an approximately 40% cash-conversion target. [source_web_0cbf3fd45b6a35eb29ea03a2](https://www.evonik.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/08/q2-2026.html?owner=evonik%3Aorganization%2FF%2FF-IR&type=evonik%3Anews-type%2Fpress-release---ir-news%2Finvestor-relations-news) [source_web_d857f258211d1849c4aa7384](https://www.evonik.com/content/dam/evonik/documents/260804_Analyst%20Investor%20Fact%20Sheet%20Q2%202026.pdf)

The contrary evidence is material. Management described the disruption-related benefit as an economic windfall that did not remove the industry’s fundamental challenges. Reported Q2 net income was only **€84 million**, versus €120 million a year earlier, because the quarter contained **€207 million of adjustments**, including restructuring and an Oxeno impairment. Q2 free cash flow was **€49 million** despite the strong EBITDA result. [source_web_0cbf3fd45b6a35eb29ea03a2](https://www.evonik.com/en/news/press-releases/2026/08/q2-2026.html?owner=evonik%3Aorganization%2FF%2FF-IR&type=evonik%3Anews-type%2Fpress-release---ir-news%2Finvestor-relations-news) [source_web_e65d6006668b69bfd585f894](https://www.evonik.com/content/dam/evonik/documents/260804_Evonik%20-%20Financials%20Q2%202026.pdf)

## Balance sheet and cash generation

At Q2, Evonik reported **€3.620 billion of net financial debt**, up from €3.311 billion at year-end following the dividend payment. Pension provisions were **€1.538 billion**. H1 free cash flow improved to €232 million from €16 million, but working capital absorbed cash in Q2. These obligations do not enter the adjusted-EPS arithmetic directly, but they justify restrained valuation multiples and reduce resilience in a downturn. [source_web_d857f258211d1849c4aa7384](https://www.evonik.com/content/dam/evonik/documents/260804_Analyst%20Investor%20Fact%20Sheet%20Q2%202026.pdf) [source_web_e65d6006668b69bfd585f894](https://www.evonik.com/content/dam/evonik/documents/260804_Evonik%20-%20Financials%20Q2%202026.pdf)

## Valuation method

A scenario adjusted-EPS multiple is used because the company provides the necessary operating guidance and because directly annualizing Q2 would overstate normalized earnings. The arithmetic is:

`Adjusted EPS = ((adjusted EBITDA − adjusted D&A − adjusted net financial expense) × (1 − tax rate) − adjusted NCI) ÷ 466 million shares`

The scenarios use the company’s €2.0–€2.2 billion EBITDA guidance, indication that adjusted D&A should be slightly below 2025’s €1.013 billion, approximately €162 million of adjusted net financial expense, a roughly 25% adjusted tax rate, and €12 million of adjusted non-controlling interests as the working reference. The multiples are analyst assumptions, not company targets.

|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 30% | €1.80bn EBITDA; €1.00bn D&A; disruption benefit reverses; weak cycle | €1.00 | 10x | **€10.01** |

The supplied market close of **€21.42 on October 16, 2026** stands above the base estimate, implying that the market already discounts some persistence of the strong operating conditions or assigns a higher multiple than the base case.

## Thesis

Evonik owns differentiated specialty-chemical franchises and is showing better pricing, utilization and operating discipline. Nevertheless, the latest earnings uplift includes an explicitly temporary supply-chain component, while debt, pensions and recurring adjustment risk limit the quality of headline EBITDA. The base case therefore uses the bottom of guidance and does not support treating the recent market price as fair value. The bull case remains achievable if Animal Nutrition strength, utilization improvements and cost savings persist after supply conditions normalize.

## Downside and contrary case

- Asian supply recovery could reverse methionine, crosslinker and C4 pricing.
- Weak European industrial demand or adverse currencies could offset internal savings.
- Restructuring costs and impairments may continue to separate adjusted from reported profit.
- Net debt and pension obligations constrain flexibility and could depress the multiple.
- Cash conversion may disappoint even when adjusted EBITDA is strong.

## Catalysts

- Delivery near the upper end of 2026 EBITDA guidance.
- Evidence in Q3 that volume and pricing gains survived the initial disruption.
- Benefits from methionine backward integration and better Organics utilization.
- Measurable savings from the extended Evonik Tailor Made program.
- Cash conversion near 40% and sustained net-debt reduction.

## Invalidation conditions

The constructive thesis would be invalidated by EBITDA tracking below €1.8 billion, persistently weak cash conversion, higher net debt without matching earnings growth, or recurring adjustments that prevent adjusted earnings from reaching shareholders. Conversely, the bear case would be too pessimistic if upper-guidance earnings persist after supply chains normalize and leverage declines materially.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: medium.** Q2/H1 2026 was the latest reported period because Q3 publication was scheduled for November 3, after this assessment. The quarterly-statement PDF was inaccessible in the research interface, so this review uses Evonik’s accessible release, fact sheet and detailed financial tables. There is no independently verified 2027 consensus, peer-multiple study, pension cash-payment schedule or normalized segment forecast. Scenario multiples are therefore judgmental and should be revisited after Q3.
