## Economics and thesis

Lithium Americas is a pre-revenue developer whose value is concentrated in the 62%-owned Thacker
Pass joint venture with General Motors. Phase 1 targets late-2027 mechanical completion and 2028
production ramp. The technical-report capex estimate is USD 2.93 billion; USD 1.14 billion of that
estimate had been spent by March 2026, and 2026 construction guidance is USD 1.2-1.5 billion.

Funding is substantial but conditional. Cash and restricted cash were USD 1.21 billion, including
restricted project cash. GM invested for a 38% asset interest, DOE financing is drawn subject to
conditions, and a 20-year Phase 1 offtake can cover up to all production based on GM requirements.
The company remains loss-making, issued 43.3 million shares through a prior ATM, opened another USD
250 million ATM, and issued DOE a near-zero-strike warrant for 5% of then-outstanding shares.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable common-share valuation is recorded without a fresh mark and a project model that
attributes 62% ownership, DOE/Orion claims, warrants, offtake pricing, remaining capex, dilution,
ramp probability, and lithium-price scenarios. Technical-report NPV cannot be treated as equity
value.

Catalysts are construction milestones, cost control, DOE draws, commissioning, and qualified
battery-grade production. Invalidate if cost-to-complete rises materially, financing conditions
fail, schedule slips, product qualification misses, lithium prices weaken, or dilution transfers
project value away from current holders.
