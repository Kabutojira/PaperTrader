## Economics and thesis

ABB's current operating business is performing strongly. Q2 2026 orders were USD 12.0 billion, up
30%, revenue was USD 9.5 billion, up 14%, operational EBITA margin was 20.2%, and continuing-
operations cash flow rose 34% to USD 1.3 billion. Net debt to EBITDA was 0.3. This supports ABB as a
high-quality electrification and industrial-automation company.

It does not support ABB as a durable public robotics proxy. ABB agreed to sell its Robotics division
to SoftBank for USD 5.375 billion, expects about USD 4.8 billion net proceeds, and still expects the
transaction to close in the second half of 2026. The division represented about 7% of 2024 group
revenue and had a 12.1% operational EBITA margin. RobotStudio HyperReality is being tested ahead of
a planned release to 60,000 RobotStudio customers, but ABB does not separately disclose recurring
RobotStudio revenue or installed-base service economics, and shareholders lose that exposure after
closing.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable valuation is recorded. The repository has no validated current ADR mark, while a
sum-of-the-parts model must incorporate the Robotics disposal, tax and separation costs, Rotork's
purchase price and integration, capital returns, diluted shares, and normalized segment cash flow.

Catalysts are disposal completion at expected net proceeds, disciplined redeployment, sustained
positive book-to-bill, and margin/cash conversion. Invalidate the physical-AI relationship on the
Robotics sale; invalidate the broader quality thesis if large acquisitions dilute returns, data-
centre and industrial orders reverse, margins normalize sharply, or cash conversion weakens.
