## Business economics

GE Vernova supplies generation equipment and services, wind equipment, and grid-electrification
systems. Its installed fleets create long-duration service revenue, while turbine and grid
equipment depend on capacity, project underwriting, customer schedules, warranties, and execution.

In Q2 2026, orders were USD 24.2 billion, revenue USD 11.1 billion, net income USD 0.6 billion,
adjusted EBITDA USD 1.2 billion, and free cash flow USD 5.1 billion. Backlog reached USD 176 billion;
more than 55% of backlog is services according to investor materials. Gas equipment backlog and
slot reservations reached 116 GW, while year-to-date data-centre orders exceeded USD 5 billion.

## Thesis and contrary evidence

GE Vernova is a direct positive-sensitivity exposure to generation and grid bottlenecks. Q2
provides unusually strong confirmation that demand is converting into priced backlog, margins, and
cash. However, one working-capital-rich quarter is not normalized free cash flow, non-GAAP margin
guidance still requires delivery, Wind remains an execution counterweight, and planned capacity and
acquisition spending expose shareholders to cycle and integration risk.

| Evidence | Interpretation |

| Orders grew 88% organically and backlog rose USD 13 billion sequentially. | Strong demand, but delivery timing and cancellation terms still matter. |

| Data-centre orders exceeded USD 5 billion year to date. | AI-load demand is material, but customer concentration and project power availability require monitoring. |

## Valuation

No supportable valuation range is recorded. The repository has no fresh deterministic market mark
for GEV, and extrapolating a single quarter's working-capital-sensitive free cash flow would create
false precision. A later valuation should use a fresh mark and diluted share count, segment-level
normalized EBITDA, cash and acquisition obligations, and downside scenarios for Wind losses,
backlog conversion, and multiple compression.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Catalysts are profitable backlog conversion, higher service mix, turbine-capacity delivery,
Electrification margin expansion, and data-centre orders becoming revenue. Invalidate the positive
case if cancellations or delays rise, equipment margin deteriorates, Wind losses consume Power and
Electrification gains, capacity expansion overruns, acquisition integration weakens cash returns, or
customer over-ordering causes a backlog correction.
