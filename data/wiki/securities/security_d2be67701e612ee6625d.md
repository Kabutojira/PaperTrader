## Investment thesis

Xylem participates across municipal and industrial water infrastructure through pumps, treatment, measurement and control, analytics, and services. Management describes water as increasingly strategic to utilities and industrial customers, including semiconductor, power, mining, food-and-beverage, and life-sciences users.

The current evidence is constructive but mixed. In the second quarter of 2026, revenue was $2.336 billion and grew only 1% organically. Adjusted EPS increased 16% to $1.46, and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 150 basis points to 23.3%. Management raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.55-$5.70 and expects a 23.1%-23.5% adjusted EBITDA margin. Orders increased 41% organically, but most of that increase came from Water Solutions and Services; order conversion timing and profitability therefore remain important uncertainties. [Source](https://www.xylem.com/en-us/resources/press-releases/xylem-reports-second-quarter-2026-results/) (`source_web_3d1c93a8dd8c07fb3c3cbf52`)

## Business economics

For the six months ended June 30, 2026, Xylem reported $4.461 billion of revenue, $1.766 billion of gross profit, $634 million of operating income, and $456 million of net income attributable to Xylem. First-half adjusted operating margin rose to 17.7% from 16.6%. Pricing, productivity, and mix more than offset inflation and lower volume in the second quarter.

Revenue diversity is useful, but not every segment is accelerating. Second-quarter organic growth was 3% in Water Infrastructure, 3% in Applied Water, negative 1% in Measurement and Control Solutions, and 1% in Water Solutions and Services. The 41% company-wide organic order increase was disproportionately driven by Water Solutions and Services, whose organic orders rose 147% in the quarter. This concentration is both a potential catalyst and a material execution risk. [Source](https://www.xylem.com/en-us/resources/press-releases/xylem-reports-second-quarter-2026-results/) (`source_web_3d1c93a8dd8c07fb3c3cbf52`)

## Valuation

**Method:** 12-month forward adjusted-EPS multiple.

The issuer guides to 2026 adjusted EPS of $5.55-$5.70. Because detailed long-range cash-flow forecasts were not available, the valuation estimates 2027 adjusted EPS and applies scenario-specific multiples rather than constructing a granular DCF.

| Scenario | 2027 adjusted EPS assumption | Multiple | Fair value | Probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | $5.40 | 16x | **$86.40** | 25% |
| Base | $6.10 | 21x | **$128.10** | 50% |
| Bull | $6.60 | 25x | **$165.00** | 25% |

The probability-weighted fair value is:

`25% × $86.40 + 50% × $128.10 + 25% × $165.00 = $126.90 per share.`

### Scenario interpretation

- **Bear:** Project delays, weak order conversion, persistent low organic growth, and fading price/productivity benefits push 2027 earnings below the midpoint of 2026 guidance. A 16x multiple reflects weaker execution and cyclicality.
- **Base:** Modest organic growth, orderly backlog conversion, and largely retained margin gains produce $6.10 of adjusted EPS. A 21x multiple recognizes durable water exposure but does not assume rapid growth.
- **Bull:** Strong orders convert profitably, industrial demand broadens, and margins remain around or above the 2026 outlook. A 25x multiple assumes premium-compounder economics persist.

These EPS estimates, multiples, and probabilities are research assumptions, not company forecasts.

## Downside and risks

- Utility or industrial customers may defer capital projects because of funding, rates, or macroeconomic pressure.
- The unusually strong order intake may convert slowly, at lower-than-expected margins, or not at all.
- Large projects expose Xylem to execution, warranty, performance-guarantee, timeline, and customer-budget risk.
- Tariffs, inflation, supply constraints, geopolitical conditions, foreign exchange, and labor availability can pressure costs.
- Connected products create cybersecurity, data, and system-disruption exposure.
- Product, environmental, regulatory, and hazardous-material liabilities could create unexpected costs.
- Acquisition integration and the large goodwill and acquired-intangible balances create execution and impairment risk.

## Catalysts

- Conversion of second-quarter orders into profitable revenue.
- Delivery of the raised 2026 adjusted-EPS and margin outlook.
- Improvement in Measurement and Control Solutions.
- Sustained demand from municipal infrastructure and water-intensive industrial markets.
- Free-cash-flow improvement toward the issuer's 10.2%-11.0% full-year margin outlook.

## Invalidation conditions

The thesis requires revision if organic growth remains near zero while orders fail to convert, margins retreat despite productivity claims, free cash flow persistently misses guidance, or debt-funded capital returns materially weaken financing resilience. Major project losses, a sustained municipal-funding contraction, loss of pricing power, or material cyber, regulatory, product-liability, or impairment events would also invalidate important assumptions.
