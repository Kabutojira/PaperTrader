This review tests Palantir as an indirect public exposure to
[[ideas/idea_anduril_product_company_disruption]]. It is not ownership in Anduril.

## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 revenue grew 85% to USD 1.633 billion. U.S. commercial revenue grew 133% to USD 595
million and U.S. government revenue grew 84% to USD 687 million. GAAP operating margin reached
46%, cash from operations was USD 899 million, and adjusted free cash flow was USD 925 million.
Management raised full-year revenue guidance to USD 7.650-7.662 billion and adjusted free-cash-flow
guidance to USD 4.2-4.4 billion. These are unusually strong economics, but the adjusted measures
exclude stock compensation and related payroll taxes.

The balance sheet is a strength. At 31 March 2026, cash and equivalents plus marketable securities
were USD 8.026 billion against USD 1.643 billion of total liabilities. Q1 stock compensation was USD
202 million, and diluted weighted-average shares were 2.571 billion, so dilution remains relevant
even though GAAP profitability and liquidity are strong.

The Anduril link is operational but economically unquantified. Anduril uses Palantir Warp Speed in
production, and the companies participate together in the Army TITAN ecosystem. Palantir does not
disclose Anduril-derived revenue, margin, contract duration, or concentration. The Army's separate
Anduril enterprise contract has a maximum potential value up to USD 20 billion, but the Army says
that amount is not obligated; it is not Palantir revenue.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

The 27 July 2026 paper mark is USD 131.53. At 2.571 billion diluted shares, this implies about USD
338.15 billion of equity value and 43.3 times the midpoint of 2026 guided revenue after subtracting
USD 6.384 billion of net liquid assets. A bounded 12-month scenario starts with the issuer's USD
7.656 billion guidance midpoint and uses the current diluted share count:

| Scenario | Explicit assumptions | Value per share | Return from USD 131.53 |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Downside | 25% 2027 revenue growth, 15x revenue multiple, USD 6.384 billion net liquid assets | USD 58.32 | -55.7% |
| Base | 40% 2027 revenue growth, 25x revenue multiple, USD 6.384 billion net liquid assets | USD 106.71 | -18.9% |

This is a sensitivity range rather than a precise target. Even the base case assumes rapid growth
and a premium multiple yet remains below the current mark. It also does not credit the Anduril Army
ceiling because neither revenue attribution nor obligation is disclosed. A lower multiple, slower
growth, continued share issuance, or weaker government-contract conversion would increase downside.

Catalysts are sustained organic growth, disclosed Warp Speed customer expansion, durable GAAP cash
conversion, and measurable defence-manufacturing revenue. Invalidate the proxy if Anduril-linked
economics remain immaterial; invalidate the broader thesis if growth decelerates sharply, government
concentration or termination rights hurt visibility, dilution offsets earnings, or valuation leaves
no downside margin.
