This review tests Palantir as an indirect public exposure to
[[ideas/idea_anduril_product_company_disruption]]. It is not ownership in Anduril.

## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 revenue grew 85% to USD 1.633 billion. U.S. commercial revenue grew 133% to USD 595
million and U.S. government revenue grew 84% to USD 687 million. GAAP operating margin reached
46%; operating cash flow was USD 899 million and adjusted free cash flow was USD 925 million.
Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term U.S. Treasury securities were USD 8.0 billion. The SEC-hosted
release reported 2.571 billion diluted weighted-average shares and USD 202 million of stock-based
compensation for the quarter.

The Anduril link is operational but economically unquantified. Anduril uses Palantir Warp Speed in
production, and the companies participate together in the Army TITAN ecosystem. Palantir does not
disclose Anduril-derived revenue, margin, contract duration, or concentration. The Army's separate
Anduril enterprise contract has a maximum potential value up to USD 20 billion, but the Army says
that amount is not obligated; it is not Palantir revenue.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

A bounded 12-month scenario uses the issuer's USD 4.2-4.4 billion adjusted free-cash-flow guidance,
USD 8.0 billion of cash and short-term Treasury securities, and 2.571 billion diluted weighted-
average shares. The downside applies 30 times USD 4.2 billion plus net cash, producing about USD
52.12 per share. The base case applies 45 times USD 4.3 billion plus net cash, producing about USD
78.38 per share. Even these demanding multiples imply **60.4% downside** and **40.4% base-case
downside** against the 2026-07-27 USD 131.53 close. The scenarios are deliberately bounded rather
than precise forecasts: adjusted free cash flow does not charge stock compensation, the share count
can rise, and neither case attributes Anduril's procurement ceiling to Palantir.

Catalysts are sustained organic growth, disclosed Warp Speed customer expansion, durable GAAP cash
conversion, and measurable defence-manufacturing revenue. Invalidate the proxy if Anduril-linked
economics remain immaterial; invalidate the broader thesis if growth decelerates sharply, government
concentration or termination rights hurt visibility, dilution offsets earnings, or valuation leaves
no downside margin.
