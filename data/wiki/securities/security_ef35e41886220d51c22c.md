## Business economics

ASML supplies lithography systems, software, upgrades, and services used in semiconductor
manufacturing. Its leading-edge EUV position, expanding installed base, and close integration with
advanced Logic and Memory roadmaps create high switching costs and recurring service economics.
Those strengths are offset by customer concentration, cyclicality, long and complex supply chains,
technology-execution risk, and export controls that can restrict shipments and service.

Q2 2026 revenue was EUR 9.3265 billion, including EUR 6.5648 billion of system sales and EUR 2.7617
billion of service and field-option sales. Gross margin reached 54.0%, net income was EUR 2.9176
billion, and diluted EPS was EUR 7.58. Management raised 2026 guidance to EUR 43-45 billion of
revenue and a 54%-56% gross margin, evidence that AI-related Logic and Memory capacity demand is
converting into ASML's outlook.

## Thesis and contrary evidence

ASML is a scarce, high-quality supplier to leading-edge semiconductor capacity. The Q2 result
supports the thesis through revenue growth, margin expansion, installed-base sales, raised
guidance, and plans to increase NXE and NXT immersion capacity. Contrary evidence is material:
orders and revenue are lumpy, capacity expansion must be executed before demand normalizes, export
controls can narrow the addressable market, customer roadmaps can delay High-NA adoption, and an
exceptional franchise can still deliver poor returns when purchased at an excessive multiple.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Catalysts are delivery against raised 2026 guidance, conversion of AI-related Logic and Memory
capacity commitments, higher installed-base revenue, successful NXE/NXT capacity expansion,
High-NA adoption, and continued cash returns. Invalidate the positive case if bookings and customer
commitments weaken materially, export restrictions impair service or shipments beyond guidance,
gross margin falls structurally, capacity expansion overruns, working capital fails to convert, or
leading-edge roadmap delays weaken lithography intensity.
