## Business economics

ASML supplies lithography hardware, software, upgrades and field services used by semiconductor manufacturers. Its economics depend on the importance of lithography to advanced chip production, the technical difficulty of replacing its systems, close integration with customer roadmaps and recurring revenue from the installed base. These advantages support high margins and substantial research spending, but do not remove semiconductor cyclicality, customer concentration, supply-chain complexity, export restrictions or technology-execution risk.

The current issuer release reported Q3 2026 sales of **EUR 11.662 billion**, including **EUR 3.289 billion** of installed-base management sales. Gross margin was **57.5%**, net income was **EUR 4.960 billion**, and basic EPS was **EUR 12.95**. Cash and short-term investments ended the quarter at **EUR 12.735 billion**. New lithography systems sold increased from 86 in Q2 to 113 in Q3.

## Current outlook and contrary evidence

Management expects 2026 sales of **EUR 44-45 billion** and gross margin around **56%**. Q4 guidance calls for EUR 14.2-15.2 billion of sales and gross margin around 57%. Management also said customers were increasing short- and medium-term demand forecasts, primarily because of expected AI investment, and that some customers had expressed interest in orders extending to 2029 or 2030. ASML is investigating additional 2028 capacity beyond the increase previously communicated.

This evidence supports the franchise thesis, but customer forecasts and expressions of interest are not the same as binding orders or recognized revenue. The release itself identifies risks from order cancellation or delay, export controls, customer-capacity decisions, supply availability, technology adoption and the ability to execute production increases. Capacity expansion could also add cost before demand is realized. Q3's 57.5% margin should therefore not be treated automatically as a permanent run rate.

### Bear case

The bear case assumes slower order conversion, tighter export restrictions, customer delays, added capacity costs and material margin normalization. EUR 40 of forward EPS at 28x recognizes that ASML could remain a strong business while receiving a lower cyclical valuation. Its 25% probability reflects meaningful policy, concentration and execution risks despite strong current demand.

### Base case

The base case assumes substantial delivery of the EUR 44-45 billion sales outlook, resilient installed-base demand, continued AI-related Logic and Memory investment and premium—but not permanently peak—margins. EUR 47 at 35x yields USD 1,851.12. A 50% probability is assigned because current guidance supports this path, but the accessible evidence cannot eliminate order-conversion and cycle uncertainty.

### Bull case

The bull case assumes durable AI investment, conversion of later-year customer interest into orders, successful capacity growth, strong productivity-upgrade demand and increasing leading-edge lithography intensity. EUR 54 at 42x produces USD 2,552.18. Its 25% probability reflects the demanding execution and duration assumptions behind both the earnings and multiple.

## Key assumptions and downside

The valuation assumes one-for-one economic exposure of the Nasdaq instrument to ordinary-share earnings, uses a constant exchange rate solely for translation, and assumes normalized forward EPS between EUR 40 and EUR 54. It also assumes continued access to critical suppliers, substantial conversion of stated customer demand and the ability to expand output without severe margin dilution.

Material downside could result from:

- customer order delays, cancellations or capital-spending reductions;
- export rules restricting new systems, upgrades or installed-base service;
- slower High-NA adoption or reduced lithography intensity;
- supply shortages or costly capacity expansion;
- gross-margin normalization from the exceptional Q3 level;
- weak cash conversion or unexpected working-capital needs; or
- compression of ASML's premium earnings multiple.

The **USD 1,260.34** bear value demonstrates that business quality does not eliminate valuation risk.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Potential catalysts are delivery near the upper end of 2026 guidance, Q4 margin near 57%, continued installed-base growth, conversion of AI-related forecasts into firm orders and revenue, successful EUV and DUV immersion capacity expansion, broader High-NA adoption, and cash returns supported by durable cash generation.

Invalidate or materially revise the positive thesis if bookings or backlog conversion weaken persistently, customers reduce forecasts, export restrictions exceed the bear assumptions, gross margins deteriorate structurally, capacity or product roadmaps suffer repeated delays, leading-edge lithography intensity declines, or accounting earnings fail to convert into durable cash generation. The base valuation also ceases to be supportable if EUR 47 of normalized forward EPS or a 35x multiple is no longer reasonable.
