## Current economics and thesis

W&T is a Gulf of America offshore producer whose equity outcome is highly sensitive to oil and gas
prices, production uptime, operating cost and end-of-life obligations. Q2 production was 34.7 MBoe/d,
49% liquids, up 3% year over year and within guidance. Revenue was USD 162.6 million, Adjusted EBITDA
USD 54.4 million and company-defined Free Cash Flow USD 31.4 million. Production declined 4% from Q1,
but lease operating expense of USD 71.6 million was below guidance.

Liquidity improved: unrestricted cash reached USD 150.7 million, total debt was USD 351.6 million,
and net debt fell to USD 200.9 million. The revolving facility had USD 43.4 million available and the
company reported compliance with applicable covenants. This supports near-term resilience, but it
does not eliminate the USD 573.2 million estimated asset-retirement obligation or the 10.75% secured
note burden. Q2 diluted weighted-average shares were 157.7 million versus 149.7 million basic shares,
so dilution is economically relevant.

## Catalysts, risks, and invalidation

Catalysts are production toward the upper half of guidance, sustained realized prices, repeatable
free cash flow after working-capital movements, additional net-debt reduction, successful low-cost
workovers and recompletions, disciplined acquisitions, and measurable reduction of retirement
liabilities. A favorable surety-litigation outcome is optional upside, not part of fair value.

Invalidate or downgrade if production falls below guidance, lease operating costs or plugging spend
rise materially, commodity prices compress cash flow, reserve replacement disappoints, net debt or
refinancing risk increases, dilution accelerates, covenant headroom narrows, retirement estimates
rise, hurricane or concentration losses impair assets, or capital allocation consumes liquidity
without durable per-share value.
