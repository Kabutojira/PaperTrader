## Economics and thesis

SQM produces lithium brine products through the Chilean Nova Andino Litio partnership with Codelco,
plus Australian hard-rock and conversion assets, iodine, and specialty plant nutrition. Q1 2026
revenue rose 69.8% to USD 1.76 billion, net income reached USD 364.7 million, and adjusted EBITDA
was USD 837 million. Lithium volume rose 25% to 69 thousand tonnes LCE, realized Chilean lithium
price rose about 95% to USD 17.8/kg, and lithium supplied 75% of gross profit.

The brine asset offers scale and cost potential, but state contributions are economically material:
Nova Andino paid more than USD 530 million to the Chilean state in the quarter. The partnership,
royalty and tax structure, environmental approvals for Salar Futuro, water use, international ramp,
and contract-price transmission must be valued explicitly. Long-term debt rose to USD 4.79 billion
while cash was USD 2.83 billion.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable valuation is recorded without a fresh deterministic ADS mark and normalized lithium
prices, Chilean state take, partnership attribution, debt, capex, minority interest, and depositary
terms. Current quarterly margin cannot be annualized safely.

Catalysts are sustained realized prices and volumes, Salar Futuro permitting, Kwinana ramp, and
cash generation after state payments and capex. Invalidate if Chinese supply restores surplus,
realized pricing falls, partnership economics worsen, environmental approvals slip, Australian
conversion misses nameplate, or debt grows without attributable returns.
