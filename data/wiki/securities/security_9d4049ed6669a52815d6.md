## Economics and thesis

SQM produces lithium brine products through the Chilean Nova Andino Litio partnership with Codelco,
plus Australian hard-rock and conversion assets, iodine, and specialty plant nutrition. Q1 2026
revenue rose 69.8% to USD 1.76 billion, net income reached USD 364.7 million, and adjusted EBITDA
was USD 837 million. Lithium volume rose 25% to 69 thousand tonnes LCE, realized Chilean lithium
price rose about 95% to USD 17.8/kg, and lithium supplied 75% of gross profit.

The brine asset offers scale and cost potential, but state contributions are economically material:
Nova Andino paid more than USD 530 million to the Chilean state in the quarter. The partnership,
royalty and tax structure, environmental approvals for Salar Futuro, water use, international ramp,
and contract-price transmission must be valued explicitly. Long-term debt rose to USD 4.79 billion
while cash was USD 2.83 billion.

and cost-curve optionality, but also increases execution, approval, capital-allocation, and
commodity-cycle exposure. The ADS rose only 0.54% on 22 July and then fell 3.38% through 28 July,

## Valuation, market state, catalysts, and risks

The deterministic 7 August 2026 close is USD 71.95. The provider row is current and `ok`; the close
is below the 50-day average and close to the 200-day average, while trading liquidity remains
adequate for comparison. The current USD/EUR conversion record is retained for base-currency

A downside-aware per-ADS valuation remains unsupported. Q1 net income of USD 1.28 per share and
47.6% adjusted EBITDA margin reflect sharply rebounding lithium prices, and annualizing either
would silently assume an unsupportable lithium-price path. The filing does not provide enough
normalized price, Chilean state-take, partnership attribution, capex, minority-interest, and ADS
economic inputs to produce a defensible downside and base value. The comparable assessment
therefore records `valuation_unsupported` rather than inventing a target. The July volume anomaly
does not establish a valuation or buy zone.

Catalysts are sustained realized prices and volumes, Salar Futuro permitting, Kwinana ramp, Mt
Holland expansion execution, and cash generation after state payments and capex. Invalidate if
Chinese supply restores surplus, realized pricing falls, partnership economics worsen,
environmental approvals slip, Australian conversion or expansion misses milestones, or debt grows
without attributable returns.
