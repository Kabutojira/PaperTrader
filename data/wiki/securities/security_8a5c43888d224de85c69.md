## Business economics

PLS retains strategically meaningful scale through its wholly owned Pilgangoora lithium operation. Expanded capacity could improve throughput and resource recovery, but economic value depends on realized spodumene prices, recoveries, operating costs, royalties, sustaining capital, taxes and the return earned on new project spending. Yahoo Finance reports positive trailing profitability, cash flow and AUD2.29 billion of cash, but those aggregates are not a substitute for reconciled issuer financial statements.

The business-quality score is **60/100**, between mixed economics and durable profitable advantages. Scale and apparent positive cash generation are favorable; lithium cyclicality, asset concentration and unverified full-cycle returns prevent a higher durable-advantage score.

## Risks, catalysts and invalidation

Downside could arise from weaker lithium prices, lower production or recoveries, cost inflation, project overruns, commissioning delays or financing that consumes liquidity without sufficient per-share returns. Capital is expected to be spent years before P2000 reaches production, increasing sensitivity to the intervening commodity cycle.

Potential catalysts include readable issuer financial and feasibility disclosures, proof that operating cash generation can fund expansion without materially weakening liquidity, delivery against current production and cost guidance, disciplined project contracting, and sustainable prices that support acceptable full-cycle returns.

Invalidate the favorable operating thesis if lithium economics remain below full-cycle costs, Pilgangoora performance deteriorates, P2000 materially exceeds its cost or schedule, or financing weakens per-share value. Invalidate this valuation-unsupported conclusion once current primary evidence permits auditable operating, balance-sheet, project and diluted-share reconciliation into ordered per-share scenarios.
