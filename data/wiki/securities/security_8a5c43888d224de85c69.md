# PLS Group Limited ordinary shares

## Decision

**Sell / Avoid; allocation ineligible; no strategy.** PLS has a scaled, long-life,
low-cost Pilgangoora lithium operation and a strong net-cash position, but AUD5.40 discounts a
large share of the favorable cycle and growth optionality. AUD1.67/AUD3.67/AUD6.69 scenarios imply
AUD3.93 probability-weighted value. The base and expected returns are negative, downside dominates
upside, and the lithium-cycle relationship is still a candidate rather than a canonical edge.

## Identity and business economics

The immutable identity is `security_8a5c43888d224de85c69`: PLS Group Limited ordinary shares on
ASX (`XASX`), provider symbol `PLS.AX`, AUD. The issuer's FY2026 report confirms that the group
explores, mines and processes lithium at the wholly owned Pilgangoora operation in Western
Australia. The FY2026 presentation describes 1.2 million tonnes per year of installed spodumene
capacity, a 31-year mine life, and additional P2000 and Colina growth options.

FY2026 revenue was AUD2.017 billion, underlying EBITDA AUD1.137 billion, and net profit after tax
AUD809 million. Production reached 879.5 thousand tonnes and the average realized SC5.2 price was
AUD2,164 per tonne. For FY2027, management guides 1.03-1.10 million tonnes production and
AUD575-625 per tonne FOB operating cost. These figures support a real low-cost scale advantage,
but owner returns remain highly sensitive to lithium pricing, grade, freight, royalties, project
capital and conversion execution.

At 30 June 2026, cash was AUD2.290 billion. The results presentation separately identifies AUD853
million borrowings and AUD98 million leases, leaving about AUD1.339 billion of simple net cash
before the declared dividend, Brazil mineral-right acquisition and other commitments. The final
five-cent dividend totaled AUD161.2 million, implying about 3.224 billion shares for per-share
scenario normalization.

## Thesis, catalysts, risks and invalidation

The positive thesis is that a wholly owned, long-life, scaled hard-rock asset can compound volume
and free cash flow when electric-vehicle and stationary-storage demand absorbs industry supply.
Catalysts are sustained realized lithium prices, delivery inside FY2027 volume/cost guidance,
P2000 approvals and economics, Colina permitting and studies, Korean hydroxide ramp, and cash
conversion after growth capital.

Contrary evidence is the extreme commodity-price sensitivity, the 32% pre-review rerating, heavy
project optionality embedded in market value, and the gap between reported FOB cost and full owner
economics after freight, royalties, sustaining capital, tax and development spending. Invalidate
the favorable operating thesis if lithium supply restores a persistent surplus, realized prices or
recoveries fall, FY2027 cost/volume guidance is missed, growth projects destroy value, or net cash
is consumed without attributable per-share returns.
