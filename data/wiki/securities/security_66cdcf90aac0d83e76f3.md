## Economics and thesis

Albemarle combines lithium conversion and resource interests with Specialties. Q1 2026 sales rose
33% to USD 1.43 billion; Energy Storage sales rose 70% and adjusted EBITDA rose 196% as realized
pricing rose 51%, volumes 14%, and inventory timing and cost actions helped. Operating cash flow was
USD 346 million and company-defined free cash flow USD 248 million. Asset sales funded a USD 1.3
billion debt reduction, leaving USD 1.9 billion total debt and USD 1.1 billion cash.

The recovery is economically meaningful but highly price-sensitive. Management's own scenarios put
2026 adjusted EBITDA at USD 0.9-1.0 billion around USD 10/kg LCE, USD 2.4-2.6 billion around USD
20/kg, and USD 4.2-4.4 billion around USD 30/kg. Forty percent of salts volume is on long-term
agreements, so spot and realized prices can diverge. Mandatory convertible preferred shares also
matter to common-share value.
