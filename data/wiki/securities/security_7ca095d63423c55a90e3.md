## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 revenue grew 64% to USD 200 million and GAAP gross margin reached 38.2%. Backlog exceeded
USD 2.2 billion, up 20% sequentially, with more than 70 contracted launches after 31 Electron/HASTE
and five Neutron contracts were signed. Launch, spacecraft, components, acquisitions, and defence
program participation make the exposure broader than one vehicle.

The business still consumed USD 50 million of operating cash and lost USD 45 million. Cash and
marketable securities were approximately USD 1.48 billion, but liquidity was built through USD 446
million of net ATM issuance in Q1. Q2 guidance assumes roughly 629 million basic shares including
convertible preferred and another adjusted EBITDA loss. Neutron's first launch follows a prior tank-
test setback and remains subject to qualification, schedule, reliability, and cost risk.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable valuation is recorded without a current validated mark and complete diluted-share
bridge. A model must separate launch and space-systems margins, probability-weight backlog, expense
Neutron development and failures, include acquisitions, preferred and convertible dilution, stock
compensation, and future financing.

Catalysts are reliable Electron cadence, backlog conversion at maintained margins, successful
Neutron qualification and first flight, and lower operating cash use. Invalidate if launch or mission
failures occur, Neutron slips or exceeds budget, fixed-price programs lose money, customer
concentration rises, acquisitions disappoint, or dilution outruns enterprise-value growth.
