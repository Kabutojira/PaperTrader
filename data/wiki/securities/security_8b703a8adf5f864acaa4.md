# Prosus N.V. ordinary shares N

## Research conclusion

Prosus provides discounted exposure to Tencent and a collection of consumer-internet platforms that now produce meaningful operating profit and free cash flow. The issuer's 16 October 2026 table reports **EUR60.5 NAV per share**, compared with the supplied 16 October market close of **EUR35.96**. That gap is not assumed to close completely: Tencent concentration, uncertain private-company marks, central debt, governance, acquisition execution and persistent holding-company discounts require substantial valuation haircuts.

The 12-month bear/base/bull fair values are **EUR27.0 / EUR48.5 / EUR63.0**, with probabilities of **25% / 55% / 20%**. Confidence is **medium**.

## Business economics and current evidence

Prosus's value is primarily look-through asset value rather than consolidated earnings. Its 16 October NAV comprised **US$149.1bn of assets**, **US$7.3bn of pro-forma net debt** and **US$141.8bn of NAV**, converted by the issuer to **EUR126.2bn** or **EUR60.5 per share**. Tencent contributed **US$108.8bn**, making it the dominant economic driver. Other listed holdings contributed approximately US$7.2bn, while unlisted holdings were marked at **US$33.1bn**.

The private marks require caution. Prosus says they use analyst consensus, post-money transaction values and internal valuation where needed; it also says the analyst estimates are not verified or endorsed by Prosus. The NAV is therefore a useful valuation framework but not an audited liquidation value.

Operating evidence outside Tencent improved in FY2026. Prosus reported ecosystem revenue of **US$9.7bn**, adjusted EBITDA of **US$1.3bn** and free cash flow of **US$1.5bn**. Reported segment evidence included US$400m adjusted EBITDA at iFood, US$481m at OLX and first-time positive adjusted EBITDA at PayU. These figures support assigning value to the operating portfolio, but they do not eliminate acquisition and execution risk, particularly around Just Eat Takeaway.com.

## Valuation method and arithmetic

A component-level NAV is used because quoted holdings, private assets and central obligations have materially different risk profiles.

### Bear — EUR27.0, 25%

- Tencent falls 30%: US$108.8bn to approximately US$76.2bn.
- Other listed assets fall 30%: approximately US$7.2bn to US$5.0bn.
- Unlisted assets receive a 50% haircut: US$33.1bn to US$16.6bn.
- Subtracting US$7.3bn net debt produces approximately US$90.5bn NAV.
- At the issuer's 0.89 USD/EUR conversion and approximately 2.088bn shares, NAV is about EUR38.6 per share.
- A 30% holding-company discount produces approximately **EUR27.0**.

### Base — EUR48.5, 55%

- Tencent and other quoted holdings remain near their 16 October values.
- Unlisted assets receive a 25% haircut to approximately US$24.8bn.
- After US$7.3bn net debt, NAV is approximately US$133.5bn, or EUR56.9 per share.
- A 15% discount for governance, tax, liquidity, execution and persistent-discount risk produces approximately **EUR48.5**.

The base case receives the highest probability because current quoted holdings and the issuer's current share count provide an observable anchor. It still discounts unlisted marks and does not assume full convergence to stated NAV.

### Bull — EUR63.0, 20%

- Tencent rises 20% to approximately US$130.6bn.
- Other listed holdings rise 15% to approximately US$8.3bn.
- Unlisted value is realized without a haircut as operating profitability broadens.
- After US$7.3bn net debt, NAV is approximately US$164.7bn, or EUR70.2 per share.
- A residual 10% holding-company discount produces approximately **EUR63.0**.

The bull probability is lower because both asset appreciation and better shareholder-value transmission are needed.

## Repurchases and shareholder transmission

The open-ended programme funds Prosus ordinary N share repurchases partly through regular Tencent sales. The issuer reports that the programme had repurchased 30% of Prosus's free float and generated 18% incremental NAV-per-share accretion by 30 September 2025. The current share table shows net N shares falling from approximately 2.280 billion at 31 March 2024 to approximately 2.087 billion at 9 October 2026.

Buying shares below NAV is mechanically accretive if asset-sale proceeds, debt and taxes are controlled. It does not guarantee that the market discount will narrow, and selling Tencent to fund repurchases changes the portfolio even when Tencent exposure per remaining share increases.

## Downside and contrary evidence

- Tencent represented roughly three quarters of reported asset value, creating material single-asset, Chinese regulatory, market and currency exposure.
- The US$33.1bn unlisted value is not independently verified and may differ substantially from realizable proceeds.
- Pro-forma debt is based on 31 March balances adjusted for disclosed non-operating events, not a new audited balance sheet at 16 October.
- Acquisitions can consume cash and offset repurchase accretion if expected synergies or turnarounds fail.
- Taxes, transaction costs, liquidity restrictions and governance can prevent stated NAV from being fully realized.
- The discount can remain wide even when operating results and NAV per share improve.

## Catalysts

Potential catalysts include continued repurchases below NAV, a declining net share count, sustained ecosystem free cash flow, further profitability at iFood, OLX and PayU, a successful Just Eat Takeaway.com improvement, private-asset realizations near current marks, Tencent appreciation, lower central net debt and clearer evidence that capital allocation compounds NAV per share.

## Invalidation conditions

The base case should be reconsidered if Tencent falls materially without offsetting accretion, private exits validate values far below current marks, ecosystem free cash flow reverses, acquisitions require substantial unplanned funding, central net debt rises persistently, repurchases cease while the discount remains wide, net shares stop declining, or capital allocation repeatedly reduces NAV per share.

## Idea exposure

- [[ideas/idea_digital_attention_gaming_ecosystems|Digital attention, gaming, and consumer ecosystems]] — positive, medium-confidence exposure through Tencent and Prosus's delivery, classifieds, travel and payments platforms. The mechanism is indirect: operating growth can raise NAV, and discounted repurchases can increase per-share exposure, but debt, acquisitions, governance and the holding-company discount can weaken transmission.

## Limitations

This assessment does not independently value Tencent from its own current financial statements; Tencent is represented by the quoted holding in Prosus's 16 October NAV. The private-company marks and the scenario discounts are uncertain. The FY2026 media release is issuer-authored and uses adjusted measures. The supplied market close is contextual rather than fair value. Review is required after a material Tencent move, new financial results, a large acquisition or disposal, a significant debt change, or evidence that buyback accretion has weakened.
