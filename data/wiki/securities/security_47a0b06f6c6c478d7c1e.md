## Business economics and thesis

Nebius operates a full-stack AI cloud spanning GPU infrastructure, networking, storage, model training, and inference. Its economic proposition is to secure power and computing equipment, bring capacity online quickly, and sell that capacity with an integrated software layer at attractive revenue per megawatt.

Primary Q2 evidence shows a sharp operating inflection:

- Group revenue was **USD 582.3 million**, up 454% year over year.
- Nebius AI-cloud revenue was **USD 575 million** and June annualized run-rate revenue was **USD 3.0 billion**.
- Group adjusted EBITDA was **USD 236.2 million**.
- Management reported four large Q2 AI-cloud agreements averaging more than **USD 1 billion** of total contract value, with annual contract value of **USD 20-25 million per MW**.
- Most capacity associated with those agreements was expected to arrive late in 2026 and contribute primarily to 2027 revenue.

These operating claims and contract economics come from the [Q2 2026 shareholder letter](https://assets.nebius.com/assets/4462517b-ce83-41f2-96ed-f2ac1bc06a05/SHLQ226%20%281%29.pdf?cache-buster=2026-08-12T11:57:07.944Z) (`source_web_2afb9581d431698ce52ac166`). They are issuer statements rather than independently reviewed customer contracts.

The thesis is that scarce deployable AI capacity, higher pricing per megawatt, customer prepayments, and a differentiated software stack can support rapid revenue growth and operating leverage. The contrary case is that construction and hardware spending, concentration in a few large customers, debt, convertibles, and equity issuance consume much of the value created.

## Valuation

**Research status: supported, with low confidence.**  
**Method: scenario enterprise value to forward revenue.**  
**Horizon: 12 months.**

A revenue method is preferable to earnings valuation because current GAAP results include heavy depreciation, financing costs, and investment revaluations while the operating base is changing rapidly. The model uses a rounded **303 million diluted-share proxy**, based on the reported six-month diluted weighted-average count of 302.619 million.

### Scenario rationale

- **Bear:** Capacity delays, utilization weakness, lower pricing, or financing strain limit revenue to USD 5.5 billion. The 4x multiple and USD 3 billion net-debt assumption reflect capital intensity and execution risk.
- **Base:** Revenue reaches USD 7.0 billion as the June USD 3.0 billion ARR base expands and large Q2 agreements begin contributing during 2027. A 7x multiple balances high growth against GAAP losses, concentration, and financing needs.
- **Bull:** Timely delivery, premium pricing, high utilization, and successful contract conversion produce USD 9.0 billion of revenue. A 10x multiple and modest net-cash position require strong capital efficiency and meaningful use of customer-funded or partner-funded capacity.

The probabilities total 100%. They place most weight on the base case, substantial weight on execution and financing downside, and less weight on exceptional capacity and pricing outcomes.

## Dilution limitation

At June 30, Nebius reported 271.855 million issued and outstanding Class A and Class B shares, excluding 50.186 million treasury Class A shares. The six-month diluted weighted-average count was 302.619 million. The same financial package reports pre-funded warrants, convertible notes, options, and treasury-share activity but does not provide a complete quarter-end fully diluted reconciliation in the pages examined.

Consequently, 303 million shares is a transparent modeling proxy, not a verified current fully diluted count. If the actual horizon denominator is materially higher, every scenario’s per-share value would be lower.

## Downside

Material downside mechanisms include:

1. late delivery of power, buildings, networking equipment, or GPUs;
2. cancellation, repricing, or poor utilization of large customer agreements;
3. concentration in a small number of customers and counterparties;
4. customer-prepayment refunds or obligations that outlast the associated cash;
5. capex and financing requirements exceeding operating cash generation;
6. rapid hardware obsolescence and lower residual values;
7. interest expense, secured claims, convertible dilution, warrants, and equity awards;
8. adjusted EBITDA failing to convert into free cash flow after normalized capex; and
9. revenue-multiple compression as supply expands or growth slows.

## Catalysts

Potential catalysts are on-time commissioning of late-2026 capacity, visible 2027 revenue from the four landmark agreements, sustained annual contract value above USD 20 million per MW, broader customer diversification, and free-cash-flow improvement excluding new advances. Successful partner-funded capacity, execution of the plan to deploy more than 1 GW per year beginning in 2027, and a complete diluted-share reconciliation would also reduce uncertainty.

## Invalidation conditions

Invalidate the base valuation if next-12-month revenue is unlikely to approach USD 7 billion, major agreements are cancelled or repriced, capacity delays materially impair 2027 revenue, or pricing per MW falls without offsetting utilization gains. Also invalidate it if operating cash flow excluding new customer advances remains structurally weak, horizon net debt materially exceeds USD 1 billion, or fully diluted shares materially exceed the 303 million proxy.

The broader operating thesis would be impaired by persistent GAAP losses after capacity matures, inability to secure power or hardware, loss of major customers, or evidence that partner-financed capacity cannot preserve Nebius’s margins and service quality.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **low**. The financial statements and results are primary issuer evidence, but the forecast depends on management’s contract and capacity claims, a wide revenue range, subjective high-growth revenue multiples, assumed future net debt, and an unreconciled diluted-share denominator. The model does not assign separate values to Avride, TripleTen, ClickHouse, Toloka, or other investments because current standalone evidence was not available in the opened materials. SEC-hosted copies returned HTTP 403, so the accessible issuer-hosted financial package was used.
