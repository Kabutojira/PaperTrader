## Valuation: present appraisal versus horizon value

Method: operating EV / calendar-2027 AI-cloud revenue at **24 October 2027**, less forward net debt plus other net assets, with a simplified economic convertible adjustment. Discount horizon equity value by an **assumed 15% equity return requirement** to 24 October 2026. All forecast inputs, multiples, funding allowances and probabilities below are analyst judgments, not measured peer medians or management forecasts.

| Operating EV, USD bn | 24 | 60 | 126 |
| Net debt before conversion adjustment, USD bn | 16 | 12 | 8 |
| Other net assets, USD bn | 0.75 | 1.50 | 3.00 |
| Common allowance before converts, m | 350 | 335 | 330 |
| Economic shares after conversion adjustment, m | 350.000 | 361.680 | 398.598 |
| One-year horizon value, USD/share | **25.00** | **146.16** | **337.31** |
| Present value in canonical CSV, USD/share | **21.74** | **127.09** | **293.31** |

Weighted horizon value is about **USD 148.04**; weighted present value about **USD 128.73**, roughly 19% below USD 158.74. Rounding does not imply forecast precision. A 25% discount to base present value is about USD 95, a sensitivity rather than an automatic buying rule.

Let `P` be horizon share value, `S` pre-convert common shares in millions, and `D_i`/`N_i` each note's original principal in billions / potential shares in millions. Solve:

```text
P*S/1000 + sum(max(0, P*N_i/1000 - D_i))
    = operating EV - net debt + other net assets
```

All notes start in modeled net debt; only the claim above original principal is incremental equity dilution. Equivalently, add converted principal back and include its shares. Base arithmetic: `(60 - 12 + 1.5 + 3.3625)bn / 361.680m = USD 146.16`; divide by 1.15 for USD 127.09 present value. Bull includes all note groups, bear none. It is an intrinsic approximation, omitting option time value and exact accreted contractual settlement. Net debt includes an assumed funding/accretion reserve less unrestricted cash. Operating leases remain operating costs. Deferred revenue is not deducted twice: operating EV must already include service-delivery cost. Future issuance and its cash proceeds must be changed together.

## Economics after scarcity and equipment replacement

Illustrative one-MW cohort: USD 25m gross cost (hardware USD 20m, site USD 5m); cash operating cost USD 2.1875m fixed plus 17.5% of revenue; site life 15 years, hardware life four/five years, zero salvage; 25% normalized tax on positive operating profit. These are **analyst assumptions**, not disclosed Nebius cohort costs.

Annual recurring cash after replacement equals revenue less cash operating cost, replacement reserves and normalized tax. Dividing by USD 25m gross cost gives a steady-state **after-tax cash yield**, not reported company ROIC or project IRR; growth capex, working capital and advances are excluded.

## Downside, catalysts, invalidation and confidence

Principal downside mechanisms are commissioning/acceptance delays, contract remedies or repricing, 59% top-three customer concentration, prepayment obligations, GPU obsolescence, weak renewal returns, secured debt and refinancing, convertible/award issuance and revenue-multiple compression. Financing distress can exceed the modeled bear loss. [S03, S07-S16, S30-S31]

Catalysts are independently visible billable capacity and recognized revenue, successful Citi takeout, a reconciled current capital structure, customer diversification, durable software/inference margins, advances converting into cash flow after service/replacement costs, and realizable non-core proceeds. Contract or capacity announcements without those economics are insufficient.

Rebuild the base if 2027 sales trend below USD 8bn, material delivery slips impair contract economics, or modeled net debt/common allowance exceeds USD 12bn / 335m without corresponding earning capacity. Major contract impairment or inability to refinance Citi threatens the broader thesis. Conversely, credible sales above USD 12bn, better recurring capital returns and a lower verified funding burden would challenge the cautious conclusion. These are review triggers, not company guidance.

**Confidence remains low.** The full 2025 Form 20-F and H1 notes were retrieved; the former retrieval-size/SEC-access limitation no longer applies. Remaining gaps are non-public contracts, site commissioning/acceptance schedules, current debt/share registers, Citi draws and takeout, actual cohort economics, current ClickHouse ownership/preferences and instrument-level convertible valuation. Probabilities and net-debt/share allowances remain judgmental. Peer EV uses dated June balances with October prices. The research supports a conditional appraisal, not a high-confidence automatic Buy/Sell recommendation.

The optional five-factor rubric export remains unscored: this reviewed report did not assign rubric-level numeric components. Low confidence separately prevents automatic ranking under current PaperTrader rules. Re-review on a material financing, deployment, contract or financial-release event, and no later than **23 November 2026**; no issuer-confirmed Q3 release date was established.
