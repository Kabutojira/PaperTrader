## Business and financial evidence

Nidec supplies small precision motors, automotive products, appliance and commercial/industrial
motors, machinery, and electronic and optical components. Its first-half fiscal 2025 statements
reported net sales of **JPY 1.302 trillion**, up 0.7%, but operating profit fell **82.5%** to **JPY
21.1 billion**. Automotive generated a **JPY 82.8 billion operating loss** after contract-loss,
impairment and supplier-claim charges.

There are still operating strengths. Small precision-motor profit rose 20% to JPY 34.9 billion, with
near-line HDD motor sales growth; appliance, commercial and industrial products produced JPY 57.4
billion of operating profit. Management also identified demand from AI-data-centre water cooling,
backup generators and battery storage. First-half operating cash flow was **JPY 112.3 billion** and
free cash flow was **JPY 45.2 billion**.

The June 16 preliminary figures reported fiscal-2025 sales of **JPY 2.700 trillion**, cash of **JPY
850 billion**, interest-bearing debt of **JPY 1.110 trillion**, and net debt of **JPY 260 billion** at
March 31, 2026. They are not audited valuation inputs. Nidec states that the figures exclude the
accounting corrections and the financial impacts of the quality and customs investigations.

The April 17 final accounting report identified a cumulative **JPY 166.4 billion** negative effect on
operating profit and **JPY 160.7 billion** negative effect on profit through fiscal Q1 2025. It also
flagged roughly JPY 250 billion of automotive goodwill and fixed assets for possible additional
impairment. Separately, Nidec estimated **USD 69.7 million** of additional U.S. customs duties and
interest through September 2025, subject to change and further-period calculation. These facts
replace the baseline's description of accounting merely as suspected or unquantified.

The committee found several distinct methods: unrecorded inventory valuation losses, impairment
testing built on low-probability sales plans, labor costs capitalized and deferred through
depreciation, improper reversals of provisions, grants misclassified as revenue, and inadequate bad-
debt allowances. It attributed the misconduct to excessive performance-target pressure and weak
checks and balances. Those findings make the risk structural and governance-related, not a single
isolated adjustment.

The quality review found potential issues at multiple production sites. Most involved changes to
materials, processes or designs without customer approval; testing/inspection-data handling and
origin labeling were also identified. Nidec said no immediate functionality or safety impact had
been found, but customer consultations and an external investigation were continuing, with the
financial impact undetermined and completion targeted for the end of August 2026.

## Thesis, contrary evidence, catalysts, and invalidation

The constructive thesis remains that Nidec's motor expertise and global manufacturing footprint can
benefit from automation, energy efficiency, near-line storage, data-centre thermal management,
backup power, storage and industrial electrification. Better automotive order discipline and fixed-
cost reductions could eventually recover profitability from the current loss base.

Contrary evidence is stronger for decision support. Automotive economics deteriorated sharply;
accounting misconduct is confirmed and quantified only provisionally; preliminary fiscal-year
figures exclude corrections and investigation effects; the annual filing is delayed; product-quality
review spans multiple sites; and the thin Frankfurt line adds execution risk. Growth themes cannot
be converted into dependable per-share earnings or cash-flow assumptions until audited corrected
accounts and investigation outcomes establish a reliable base.

Catalysts are the external quality investigation report, an audited annual securities report by the
extended September 30 deadline, quantified corrections and liabilities, restored full-year guidance,
credible remediation milestones, normalized automotive margins, and evidence that data-centre and
industrial demand converts to cash. Maintain the hard blocker or downgrade further if filing is
again delayed, the auditor qualifies or disclaims its opinion, restatements are material, additional
misconduct emerges, quality claims create material customer or cash costs, liquidity tightens, or the
Frankfurt line remains impractically thin.
