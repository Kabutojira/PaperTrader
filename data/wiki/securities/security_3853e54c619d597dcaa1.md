# Nidec Corporation Frankfurt ordinary shares

## Assessment

**Valuation unsupported.** Nidec's current disclosure record does not provide a sufficiently reliable and accessible basis for bear, base and bull per-share values. The issuer's September 30 disclosure index lists annual FY2025 results, nine-month results and a notice that the independent auditor disclaimed its opinion on the securities report. The detailed linked PDFs were unreadable through this research interface, so their financial figures could not be independently examined or used in valuation ([source](https://www.nidec.com/en/corporate/internal-control-improvement/reference/) — `source_web_015c0fffceffd7a1c97f733a`).

The accounting overview remains materially adverse. Nidec describes misconduct involving inventory valuation, fixed-asset impairment, capitalization of labor costs, subsidy and provision accounting, and bad-debt allowances. It states that corrections are expected to reduce consolidated net assets as of FY2025 Q1 by approximately **JPY 160.7 billion**. It also identifies approximately **JPY 250 billion** of automotive goodwill and fixed assets as subject to examination for possible additional impairment, with possible derivative effects on depreciation, amortization and tax ([source](https://www.nidec.com/en/corporate/internal-control-improvement/overview/) — `source_web_f7d1a737aaedc9fe805c2c32`).

These disclosures establish material uncertainty but not a defensible valuation denominator. No prior preliminary earnings, debt, share-count or fair-value figures are carried forward as verified inputs.

## Business economics and constructive thesis

Nidec is an established motor and industrial-equipment manufacturer. Its potential economics depend on engineering capability, manufacturing scale, demand for efficient motion systems and disciplined conversion of revenue into recurring cash earnings. End markets such as industrial automation, storage, cooling and electrification may provide long-run demand.

The constructive thesis requires more than thematic exposure. It requires evidence that automotive contracts earn acceptable returns, industrial growth converts into cash, corrected accounts provide a stable earnings base, and governance reform prevents recurrence. Nidec's financial-information index continues to direct readers to consolidated trends and outlook materials, but that index alone does not establish current normalized earnings ([source](https://www.nidec.com/en/ir/financial/) — `source_web_7d285b8ff8cfbfdc6d8f0eab`).

## Accounting, governance and contrary evidence

The principal contrary evidence is structural rather than a single weak quarter. Nidec's accounting overview identifies misconduct across several accounting categories and business bases. The September 30 reference index then records an auditor disclaimer for the FY2025 securities report as well as a disclaimer of review conclusion for the third-quarter results. An auditor disclaimer means the newly published figures cannot serve as a clean normalized base without further evidence.

The accessible evidence therefore leaves unresolved:

1. corrected and auditable normalized earnings;
2. final impairment, depreciation and tax effects;
3. current diluted shares and any share-class conversion required for NIB.F;
4. dependable net debt and financing requirements;
5. normalized automotive segment economics; and
6. the financial cost of quality remediation and customer outcomes.

## Quality investigation

Nidec's quality portal says its group-wide review identified more than 1,000 potential cases by May 13, including unapproved changes to materials, processes or designs, handling of testing or inspection data, and country-of-origin descriptions. The portal says no immediate functionality or safety problem had been identified at that stage ([source](https://www.nidec.com/en/corporate/disclosure/quality/overview/) — `source_web_5c894919c6faab789289ac95`).

The current quality-response page links a September 4 investigation report and company response, showing that the process advanced after the prior review. Its accessible HTML, however, does not quantify customer compensation, remediation, recall, legal or other cash exposure. It continues to describe customer communication, technical verification and control-system improvement as ongoing activities ([source](https://www.nidec.com/en/corporate/disclosure/quality/) — `source_web_e0da6759c21ee2bdde67e0e3`). That unresolved amount is material to both normalized earnings and downside analysis.

## Valuation method and scenario coverage

An **earnings-multiple** framework remains the appropriate eventual method for a mature diversified manufacturer. The required arithmetic would be:

`normalized issuer earnings ÷ verified diluted shares = normalized EPS`

`normalized EPS × scenario multiple = JPY value per issuer share`

That value would then require a verified NIB.F share ratio and contemporaneous JPY/EUR conversion to produce a Frankfurt-line fair value. None of those steps should be populated with unverified inputs.

### Bear case

No bear value or probability is assigned. Relevant conditions would include larger corrections or impairment, material quality or customer costs, persistent automotive losses, financing pressure, or ineffective remediation. Their financial magnitude and EUR per-share effect cannot currently be verified.

### Base case

No base value or probability is assigned. A base case requires corrected audited earnings, reliable net debt and diluted shares, normalized segment margins, quantified quality costs and a reconciled Frankfurt share ratio. Those inputs remain unavailable or insufficiently verifiable.

### Bull case

No bull value or probability is assigned. A constructive case would require clean subsequent assurance, credible internal-control reform, automotive recovery and cash conversion from industrial and data-centre demand. Accessible evidence does not establish the timing or magnitude needed for per-share arithmetic.

Because all three cases lack a reliable common financial base, assigning probabilities that total 100% would imply unsupported precision. All scenario numbers therefore remain empty.

## Downside, catalysts and invalidation

### Downside

Material downside could result from further restatement, additional automotive impairment, continuing auditor disclaimers, customer compensation or remediation costs, weaker contract economics, tighter financing conditions, or ineffective governance reform. The Frankfurt listing also presents price-discovery and execution uncertainty. Downside magnitude is not quantified because the corrected balance sheet, earnings base and liability range are not verifiable.

### Catalysts and evidence gates

- accessible corrected annual and interim financial statements;
- an unqualified subsequent audit opinion;
- verified diluted shares, net debt and Frankfurt share mechanics;
- quantified quality-related customer and remediation costs;
- normalized automotive margins and cash generation; and
- measurable completion of internal-control reforms.

These are evidence gates, not assumptions that positive outcomes will occur.

### Invalidation conditions

Any constructive thesis would be invalidated by further material misconduct, continued auditor inability to obtain sufficient evidence, materially larger impairment or quality costs, financing deterioration, persistent automotive losses, or ineffective remediation. The valuation-unsupported conclusion should be revisited only when corrected financial and per-share inputs become accessible and independently verifiable.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **low**. The accessible issuer pages establish the disclosure chronology and seriousness of the accounting and quality issues, but they do not provide enough current, readable financial detail for valuation. The September 30 PDFs linked by the issuer were unreadable through this research interface, and no figures were inferred from them. The quality portal does not quantify final liabilities. The issuer pages also do not independently reconcile the Frankfurt share class or conversion mechanics.
