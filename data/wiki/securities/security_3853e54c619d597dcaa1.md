# Nidec Corporation Frankfurt ordinary shares

## Decision

**Watch; valuation unsupported and no strategy.** Nidec retains broad motor, industrial,
automotive, appliance and data-centre exposure, but current issuer evidence does not support a
scenario valuation. The latest interim statements were not subject to interim review, warn that
suspected inappropriate accounting could have a material and pervasive effect, and omit full-year
guidance. The audited annual securities report for the year ended March 31, 2026 remains pending
under an extension to September 30. A separate external investigation is examining product-quality
issues across multiple production sites, with the financial impact undetermined.

The July 31 bullish MACD crossover followed a 6.16% 20-session decline and coincided with a 5.31%
one-session rebound. Its very small positive histogram, neutral RSI and exceptionally low Frankfurt
volume do not outweigh the accounting, disclosure and quality uncertainties. This is a technical
rebound to monitor, not a paper-trade opportunity.

## Business and financial evidence

Nidec supplies small precision motors, automotive products, appliance and commercial/industrial
motors, machinery, and electronic and optical components. Its first-half fiscal 2025 statements
reported net sales of **JPY 1.302 trillion**, up 0.7%, but operating profit fell **82.5%** to **JPY
21.1 billion**. Automotive generated an **JPY 82.8 billion operating loss** after an JPY 36.5 billion
contract-loss provision, JPY 31.7 billion impairment and JPY 19.5 billion supplier-claim charge.

There are still operating strengths. Small precision-motor profit rose 20% to JPY 34.9 billion, with
near-line HDD motor sales growth; appliance, commercial and industrial products produced JPY 57.4
billion of operating profit. Management also identified demand from AI-data-centre water cooling,
backup generators and battery storage. First-half operating cash flow was **JPY 112.3 billion** and
free cash flow was **JPY 45.2 billion**.

The balance sheet reported **JPY 344.5 billion cash**, **JPY 712.0 billion interest-bearing debt**,
**JPY 367.6 billion net debt**, and **JPY 1.759 trillion equity attributable to owners** at September
30, 2025. Those figures suggest adequate liquidity in isolation, but they are not decisive valuation
inputs: the report says ongoing investigations could materially and pervasively affect the financial
statements, the possible impact was not reflected, and the statements were not subject to interim
review.

The quality review found potential issues at multiple production sites. Most involved changes to
materials, processes or designs without customer approval; testing/inspection-data handling and
origin labeling were also identified. Nidec said no immediate functionality or safety impact had
been found, but customer consultations and an external investigation were continuing, with the
financial impact undetermined and completion targeted for the end of August 2026.

## Thesis, contrary evidence, catalysts, and invalidation

The constructive thesis is that Nidec's motor expertise and global manufacturing footprint can
benefit from automation, energy efficiency, near-line storage, data-centre thermal management,
backup power, storage and industrial electrification. Better automotive order discipline and fixed-
cost reductions could eventually recover profitability from the current loss base.

Contrary evidence is presently stronger for decision support. Automotive economics deteriorated
sharply; full-year guidance is absent; debt increased; accounting reliability is unresolved; the
annual filing is delayed; product-quality review spans multiple sites; and the thin Frankfurt line
adds execution risk. Stated growth themes cannot be converted into dependable per-share earnings or
cash-flow assumptions until audited accounts and investigation outcomes establish a reliable base.

Catalysts are the external quality investigation report, an audited annual securities report by the
extended September 30 deadline, quantified corrections and liabilities, restored full-year guidance,
credible remediation milestones, normalized automotive margins, and evidence that data-centre and
industrial demand converts to cash. Maintain the hard blocker or downgrade further if filing is
again delayed, the auditor qualifies or disclaims its opinion, restatements are material, additional
misconduct emerges, quality claims create material customer or cash costs, liquidity tightens, or the
Frankfurt line remains impractically thin.

## Valuation

The selected repository template is `mature_compounder`, with an `earnings_multiple` method, because
a diversified established motor manufacturer would ordinarily be valued on normalized per-share
earnings, recurring margins, reinvestment, debt and dilution. **Valuation is unsupported in this
review.** No bear, base or bull fair values or probabilities are supplied.

The exact missing decisive evidence is the audited fiscal-year 2025 financial base for the year ended
March 31, 2026, including quantified accounting corrections, dependable net debt and diluted share
count, a resolved auditor conclusion, quantified quality-related liabilities, and normalized segment
earnings after remediation. The June 30 issuer notice confirms that the annual filing deadline was
extended to September 30. Until those inputs exist, assigning normalized earnings or a multiple would
create false precision.

The assessment is therefore `unsupported` and ineligible. Low confidence, absent accepted
relationship research, poor Frankfurt-line liquidity, adverse governance and disclosure timing, and
the hard `valuation_unsupported` blocker prevent strategy research.
