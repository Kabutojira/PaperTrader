## Bear case and thesis invalidation

Queries become cheaper, workloads commoditize and customers optimize spend.

Treat the hypothesis as invalidated if the expected activity growth does not produce incremental paid demand, or if lower pricing, rising capital needs, competition or weak free cash flow overwhelms the extra volume.
