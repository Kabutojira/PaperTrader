## Economics and thesis

Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue grew 26% to USD 1.471 billion, subscription revenue grew 27% to USD 1.400
billion, and ending annual recurring revenue grew 25% to USD 5.841 billion. Quarterly net-new ARR
accelerated 51% to USD 332.8 million. Six-, seven-, and eight-plus-module adoption was 51%, 35%,
and 26%; Falcon Flex ARR reached USD 2.29 billion. These results strengthen the evidence that
continuity demand and platform consolidation are reaching company economics.

Quarterly operating cash flow was USD 530.3 million and company-defined free cash flow was USD
377.4 million, or 25.7% of revenue. GAAP operating loss narrowed to USD 33.2 million and GAAP net
income attributable to CrowdStrike was USD 5.3 million, but stock-based compensation and related
payroll taxes rose to USD 399.0 million. Strong growth and cash generation therefore coexist with
an equity cost that remains economically material to per-share value.
