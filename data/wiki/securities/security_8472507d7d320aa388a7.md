## Business economics and thesis

CrowdStrike presents Falcon as a unified cybersecurity platform covering endpoint protection, identity security, cloud security, SaaS security, data security, AI detection and response, security operations, threat intelligence and exposure management. This breadth creates a plausible consolidation model: customers can adopt multiple security capabilities through a common platform, agent and operating interface. If adoption is retained and priced well, that model can support recurring revenue and operating leverage.
