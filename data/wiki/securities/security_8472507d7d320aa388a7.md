## Economics and thesis

Fiscal Q1 2027 revenue grew 26% to USD 1.39 billion, subscription revenue was USD 1.32 billion, and
ending annual recurring revenue grew 24% to USD 5.51 billion. Net-new ARR grew 32% to USD 256
million. Six-, seven-, and eight-plus module adoption reached 51%, 35%, and 25%, supporting the
platform-consolidation mechanism.

Operating cash flow was USD 591 million and company-defined free cash flow USD 468.5 million, or
34% of revenue. GAAP operating loss nevertheless remained USD 30.6 million versus non-GAAP
operating income of USD 325.7 million. Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were USD
317.6 million, so dilution and excluded costs remain economically material when translating
platform growth into per-share value.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

The split-adjusted security closed at USD 180.11 on 2026-07-27. The 10-Q provides a pro-forma
split-adjusted diluted weighted-average share count of 1.0315 billion; the scenarios conservatively
use 1.05 billion shares for prospective dilution. Against fiscal-2027 revenue guidance of USD
5.915-5.959 billion and USD 3.807 billion of reported net cash, a downside case applies a 25% free-
cash-flow margin and 60x multiple to the low revenue guide, producing USD 88.12 per share. A base
case applies a 30% margin and 75x multiple to the high guide, producing USD 131.31. These deliberately
generous growth multiples still imply 51.1% downside and 27.1% base-case downside over 12 months.
The result is sensitive to cash-flow seasonality, equity compensation, acquisitions, incident
liabilities, and the assumed terminal multiple; it is a bounded comparison, not a price forecast.

Catalysts are sustained net-new ARR acceleration, improving GAAP operating margin, continued
multi-module adoption, and declining incident costs. Invalidate if retention weakens, platform
bundling compresses price, a product or service failure damages trust, competition slows growth, or
equity awards and excluded costs prevent per-share cash compounding.
