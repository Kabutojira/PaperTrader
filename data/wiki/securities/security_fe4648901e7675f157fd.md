## Business economics

Agronomics is a listed investment company focused on clean-food technologies. Shareholder value is driven primarily by changes in the carrying and realizable value of more than 20 portfolio companies, less corporate expenses and any dilution, rather than by recurring consolidated operating earnings. Most holdings are unquoted and are generally carried at cost or the most recent priced funding round, while quoted investments are marked to market. Consequently, reported NAV can lag economic developments and can differ materially from realizable proceeds.

At 30 June 2026, the company reported unaudited net assets of **£140.03 million**, investments of **£138.51 million**, cash and deposits of **£1.59 million**, and NAV of **12.93 pence per share**. NAV per share fell from 13.12 pence at 31 March despite an increase in aggregate net assets because shares were issued to fund a portfolio investment. The same release reported a 5.64-pence share price and a 56% discount to NAV at quarter-end. [NAV release](https://www.investegate.co.uk/index.php/announcement/rns/agronomics-limited--anic/net-asset-value-as-at-30-june-2026-/9663364) (`source_web_e37f35aebb739d5346620886`).

The earlier interim report illustrates both the potential and volatility of this model. For the six months ended 31 December 2025, Agronomics reported a £10.01 million profit driven primarily by unrealized investment and foreign-exchange gains, while cash and deposits fell to £2.15 million. It also disclosed that Meatable had been dissolved and its £11.9 million carrying value fully written down in the June 2025 financial statements. [Half-year financial report](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/agronomics-limited--anic/half-year-financial-report/9433406) (`source_web_cf3415f3b490030222da06c7`).

## Material contrary evidence

Liberation Bioindustries was valued at **£35.28 million**, approximately **25% of Agronomics' 30 June 2026 NAV**. On 29 September, Agronomics disclosed that a strategic review produced no acceptable sale or joint-venture offer, expected financing had not been received, costs had risen, and Liberation required approximately **US$25 million** more. Construction slowed and the previous Q4 2026 completion target would not be met; first production was expected six months after additional funding is secured. The issuer said it did not then expect a material NAV effect, but the concentration, financing gap and schedule slippage warrant an explicit valuation haircut. [Liberation update](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/agronomics-limited--anic/update-on-liberation-bioindustries-/9797369) (`source_web_3b258790b982145000f67d17`).

## Valuation

**Method:** discounted reported NAV over a 12-month horizon.

The 12.93-pence unaudited NAV is the starting point because Agronomics is an investment company and dependable portfolio-level earnings or cash-flow forecasts are unavailable. Discounts reflect private-asset opacity, round-based valuations, limited cash, financing dependence, corporate expenses, dilution risk and uncertain exits.

| Scenario | Probability | Fair value | Per-share arithmetic and drivers |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | 30% | **£0.0300** | Remove approximately 25% of NAV for a severe Liberation impairment: 12.93p × 75% ≈ 9.7p. Apply an approximately 69% discount to the remaining portfolio for additional write-down, financing and liquidity risk, giving about 3.0p. |
| Base | 50% | **£0.0800** | Assume aggregate June NAV is broadly recoverable and Liberation obtains funding, but retain an approximately 38% discount for illiquidity, valuation lag and execution risk: 12.93p × 62% ≈ 8.0p. |
| Bull | 20% | **£0.1100** | Assume successful Liberation financing, commercial progress and better exit conditions narrow the discount to approximately 15%: 12.93p × 85% ≈ 11.0p. No premium to reported NAV is assumed. |

The probability-weighted scenario value is approximately **£0.071 per share**. The 30% bear probability reflects demonstrated failures and current financing stress; the 50% base probability recognizes portfolio diversification and continued regulatory progress; the 20% bull probability reflects the additional commercial, funding and exit execution needed for a sustained discount narrowing.

## Thesis and assumptions

The security provides listed access to a diversified clean-food venture portfolio at a substantial discount to its last reported NAV. Upside depends on portfolio companies converting technical and regulatory achievements into revenue, follow-on financing and realizable exits. The discount is partly justified because private valuations can be stale, Agronomics has little uninvested cash relative to portfolio requirements, and Liberation creates material concentration risk.

Key assumptions are that the June NAV remains a useful—though not cash-realizable—anchor; no undisclosed post-September event has materially changed the portfolio; follow-on capital remains available, potentially with dilution; and portfolio milestones eventually create third-party valuation or liquidity evidence.

## Downside

- Material write-downs or failures among pre-profit holdings.
- Liberation financing failure, adverse financing terms or further commissioning delays.
- Dilution from additional Agronomics shares issued to fund portfolio investments.
- Private funding-round values proving higher than realizable exit proceeds.
- Continued corporate costs and follow-on funding needs against limited cash.
- Foreign-exchange losses on USD, EUR and AUD holdings.
- A persistent market discount even if stated NAV is maintained.

## Catalysts

- A fully funded Liberation package and credible commissioning timetable.
- Liberation first production or binding customer commitments.
- Commercial revenue and scale evidence across precision-fermentation holdings.
- Arm's-length portfolio funding rounds at supportive valuations.
- Profitable disposals, listings or other cash realizations.
- Capital returns or improved disclosure that narrow the holding-company discount.

## Invalidation conditions

The positive thesis would be invalidated by repeated material write-downs, failure of major holdings to finance operations, substantial impairment or dilution at Liberation, portfolio cash consumption without commercial validation, or Agronomics issuing material equity below NAV. The conservative discount framework would require reassessment if audited results and arm's-length exits show that NAV is readily realizable and portfolio proceeds consistently cover expenses and follow-on commitments.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: medium.** The NAV anchor and concentration risk come from issuer financial releases, but the latest opened NAV is unaudited and dated 30 June 2026. Most assets are unquoted; detailed cap tables, liquidation preferences, portfolio cash runways, revenue, contracts and financing terms were unavailable. The scenario discounts are analytical assumptions, not observable transaction prices. The supplied 16 October 2026 market snapshot is useful context but is not treated as fair value.
