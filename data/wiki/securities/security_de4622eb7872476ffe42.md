## Initial AI-agent economy investment thesis

**Agentic payments and commerce.** Authenticated agents purchasing services and paying invoices may generate more tokenized transactions.

This is a proposed revenue-transmission channel, not proof of agent-attributed revenue or durable margins. See [[ideas/idea_ai_agent_productivity_execution_economy|the agent-productivity-loop investment idea]].

## Bear case and thesis invalidation

Bank-to-bank rails, stablecoins and declining payment yield limit incremental fees.

Treat the hypothesis as invalidated if the expected activity growth does not produce incremental paid demand, or if lower pricing, rising capital needs, competition or weak free cash flow overwhelms the extra volume.
