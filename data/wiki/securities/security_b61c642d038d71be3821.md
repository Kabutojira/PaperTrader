# Materialise NV (NASDAQ: MTLS)

## Research update

This is the first research page for the security. It adds a current review of first-half 2026 operating performance, business economics, balance-sheet strength, contrary evidence, and a 12-month bear/base/bull valuation. The work uses Materialise's August 2026 financial release and the ECB's October 16, 2026 EUR/USD reference rate.

## Identity reconciliation

The issuer release identifies Materialise NV under ticker **MTLS** on Nasdaq and Euronext. The supplied security identity describes a Nasdaq-listed American Depositary Share, denominated in USD, with each ADS representing one ordinary share. The opened release does not independently state the ADS ratio, exact MIC, or current ADS-equivalent diluted share count. The supplied security and issuer IDs have therefore been retained without alteration, while the ADS ratio and valuation share count are explicitly treated as assumptions.

## Business economics

Materialise operates three distinct businesses:

- **Medical** supplies personalized medical devices and related software. It is the main growth and profit engine: first-half 2026 revenue increased 9.6% to €70.039 million and segment adjusted EBITDA was €20.787 million, a 29.7% margin.
- **Software** supplies additive-manufacturing software. First-half revenue declined 2.1% to €19.242 million, although segment adjusted EBITDA increased to €2.103 million. New CO-AM offerings may help, but a return to growth has not yet been demonstrated.
- **Manufacturing** provides additive-manufacturing services. First-half revenue declined 1.2% to €47.067 million, while adjusted EBITDA improved from a €1.185 million loss to approximately breakeven.

The portfolio therefore combines an attractive medical franchise with two businesses that currently produce much lower margins. Consolidated first-half gross margin was 57.0%, but adjusted EBIT margin was only 4.7%, showing that research, sales and administrative spending absorbs much of the gross profit.

## Current evidence

For the first half of 2026, Materialise reported:

- Revenue of €136.349 million, up 3.9% year over year.
- Adjusted EBITDA of €17.642 million, up 22.2%.
- Adjusted EBIT of €6.351 million, up 71.4%.
- Net profit of €5.152 million.
- Operating cash flow of €15.060 million and free cash flow of €11.368 million.
- Cash of €133.735 million, gross debt of €59.521 million, and net cash of €74.214 million at June 30.
- Reaffirmed 2026 revenue guidance of €273 million to €283 million.

Second-quarter revenue growth accelerated to 8.1%, helped by 12.2% Medical growth and renewed Manufacturing growth. However, Software revenue fell 2.7%, Medical's segment adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 31.3% from 32.7%, and consolidated gross margin declined to 56.8% from 58.3%. These are meaningful counterweights to the improved profit and cash-flow figures.

## Valuation

### Method

A forward enterprise-value-to-revenue method is used because consolidated earnings remain small relative to revenue and segment economics vary widely. The model uses:

- 2026 revenue: **€278 million**, the midpoint of management's €273–283 million guidance.
- Net cash: **€74.214 million**, reported at June 30, 2026.
- ADS-equivalent shares: **58.0 million**, an explicit analyst assumption because an exact current diluted count was not available in the opened release.
- EUR/USD: **1.1253 USD per EUR**, the ECB reference rate for October 16, 2026.

The arithmetic is:

`USD fair value per ADS = ((€278m × EV/revenue multiple) + €74.214m) / 58.0m × 1.1253`

| Scenario | Probability | EV/revenue | Fair value | Main drivers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | 25% | 0.9× | **$6.30** | Software remains in decline, Medical margins weaken, and Manufacturing stays near breakeven. |
| Base | 50% | 1.4× | **$9.00** | Guidance midpoint is achieved, Medical remains healthy, Software stabilizes, and Manufacturing sustains its improvement. |
| Bull | 25% | 2.0× | **$12.20** | Medical sustains double-digit growth, Software returns to growth, and Manufacturing becomes consistently profitable. |

The probabilities emphasize the base case because first-half execution and the balance sheet are supportive, while equal 25% tails recognize both the company's limited consolidated margin and the possibility of stronger operating leverage. The probability-weighted value is approximately **$9.03 per ADS**. The supplied October 16 market snapshot was $8.00, but that market price is context rather than an input to fair value.

## Thesis and assumptions

Materialise has a valuable Medical franchise, improving consolidated profit, positive free cash flow, and net cash. These attributes support a valuation above that of a structurally loss-making manufacturing-services business. A full premium software multiple is not appropriate because Software is currently contracting and consolidated adjusted EBIT margin remains below 5%.

The valuation assumes management delivers its 2026 revenue range, Medical remains the primary growth engine, Software stabilizes, Manufacturing avoids renewed material losses, and net cash is not consumed by a large transaction. It also assumes the supplied one-for-one ADS ratio and a 58.0 million ADS-equivalent denominator. The latter is not a verified reported figure and is the assessment's most important numerical limitation.

## Downside and contrary evidence

The main risks are persistent Software contraction, Medical margin compression, renewed Manufacturing losses, adverse currency movements, regulatory or reimbursement setbacks in medical applications, and investment or acquisitions that consume cash. Although first-half profit improved, the second-quarter gross-margin decline and continued low consolidated operating margin show that the business has not yet established strong group-wide operating leverage. The bear case produces approximately $6.30 per ADS.

## Catalysts

- Revenue above the midpoint of 2026 guidance.
- A return to growth in Software and adoption of new CO-AM products.
- Sustained positive Manufacturing EBITDA.
- Continued double-digit Medical growth without further margin erosion.
- Higher consolidated adjusted EBIT margin and continued free-cash-flow conversion.
- Share repurchases that reduce the diluted count without materially weakening net cash.
- Improved focus following the RapidFit and Eyewear divestitures.

## Invalidation conditions

Reassess or invalidate the thesis following a material guidance miss or reduction, sustained Software contraction, a reversal of Manufacturing's improvement, low-single-digit Medical growth combined with further margin compression, materially negative free cash flow, a large acquisition, a changed ADS ratio, or disclosure that the assumed share count is materially understated.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: Medium.** The operating and balance-sheet evidence is current and issuer-authored, and the currency conversion uses an official reference rate. However, the detailed half-year filing was inaccessible through the research interface. The opened release does not provide an exact current diluted ADS-equivalent share count or independently confirm the one-for-one ADS ratio. The 58.0 million denominator is therefore an assumption; each one-million-share difference changes per-ADS values by approximately 1.7%. The scenario multiples are analyst assumptions, and no directly comparable-company valuation evidence was opened.
