## Current economics and thesis

TSMC is the leading pure-play semiconductor foundry. Its advantage rests on process leadership,
customer trust, manufacturing learning, scale and a broad design ecosystem. The economic mechanism
is falsifiable: sustained leading-node and advanced-packaging demand should support utilization,
pricing and returns despite very high reinvestment; loss of node leadership or weak returns on the
overseas buildout would impair it.

Second-quarter revenue was NT$1.270 trillion, or USD 40.20 billion, up 36.0% year over year. Gross
margin was 67.7%, operating margin 60.3%, and diluted EPS USD 4.31 per ADR. Advanced technologies at
7nm and below were 77% of wafer revenue, including 30% at 3nm and the first 3% at 2nm. Management
guided third-quarter revenue to USD 44.6-45.8 billion and 2026 US-dollar revenue growth to slightly
above 40%, while guiding gross margin to 65-67% and operating margin to 56-58%.

Financial capacity is a strength rather than a financing constraint. At 30 June, cash and marketable
securities were NT$3.518 trillion versus NT$864 billion of long-term interest-bearing debt. Q2 cash
from operations was NT$783 billion, capital expenditure NT$496 billion and company-defined free cash
flow NT$287 billion. The 2025 filing nevertheless projected USD 52-56 billion of 2026 capital
expenditure, so overseas and leading-node expansion must earn adequate returns.

## Catalysts, risks and invalidation

Catalysts are delivery near the top of Q3 guidance, a clean 2nm ramp, sustained AI-accelerator and
advanced-packaging demand, pricing that offsets overseas cost dilution, durable free cash flow after
the investment peak, and continued technology leadership. A lower ADR price can also create an
opportunity without any thesis change.

Invalidate or downgrade if leading-node share or yield slips, AI demand or customer orders weaken,
gross margin structurally falls below the normalized range, overseas facilities fail to earn their
cost of capital, capital spending outruns cash conversion, customer concentration worsens, export
controls impair addressable demand, or Taiwan geopolitical and infrastructure risks materially rise.
