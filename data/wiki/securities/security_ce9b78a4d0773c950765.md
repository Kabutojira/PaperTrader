## Business economics and thesis

TSMC's economic advantage rests on advanced-process manufacturing capability, scale, accumulated yield learning and customer reliance on an independent foundry. CNBC reports that AI-server demand kept advanced 5-, 4- and 3-nanometer capacity fully booked during the second quarter and cites a 72.5% foundry share. That external share estimate was not independently verified in this review, but record August revenue corroborates strong current demand.

Yahoo Finance reports Q3 FY2026 revenue of NT$1.49 trillion, earnings of NT$763.06 billion and a 51.07% profit margin. It also reports trailing EPS of USD15.43 for the ADR. Together with August's 53.3% year-over-year revenue increase, these figures support the view that AI and high-performance-computing demand is translating into substantial earnings.

The principal counterweight is reinvestment and execution risk. Yahoo's current summary identifies new 2-nanometer and overseas factories as potential sources of margin pressure. The thesis therefore requires process leadership and utilization to remain strong enough to cover the cost of new capacity.

## Downside and invalidation

The bear case becomes more likely if AI infrastructure spending enters a sustained digestion phase, advanced-node utilization or pricing weakens, customers diversify foundry supply, or 2-nanometer and overseas facilities cause structural margin compression. Export restrictions and Taiwan geopolitical exposure can amplify operating risk even when the balance sheet remains sound.

The thesis would be invalidated or materially weakened by loss of leading-node share, persistent yield or schedule problems, a prolonged contraction in AI orders, deterioration in cash conversion despite reported earnings growth, or evidence that overseas manufacturing cannot earn adequate returns.

## Catalysts

Relevant operating catalysts include continued monthly revenue growth, confirmation that third-quarter profitability is durable, sustained demand for AI-server processors, successful 2-nanometer commercialization and evidence that overseas capacity can ramp without structural margin erosion. Accessible primary disclosure covering capital expenditure, packaging capacity and customer demand would increase analytical confidence.
