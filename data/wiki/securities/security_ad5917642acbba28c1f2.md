# Teledyne Technologies Incorporated common stock

## Identity

- Immutable security: `security_ad5917642acbba28c1f2`
- Issuer: `issuer_2794d6e0417d8830b853`

Teledyne supplies sophisticated digital-imaging products and software, instrumentation, aerospace
and defense electronics, and engineered systems. Its products sense, analyze, and distribute
information in high-reliability markets spanning defense, space, industrial automation, marine,
environmental monitoring, energy, medical imaging, and scientific research. No accepted canonical
idea-security relationship currently exists for this instrument.

## Economics and thesis

The investable thesis is that proprietary sensing and imaging technologies, demanding qualification
requirements, long customer relationships, a diversified installed base, disciplined acquisitions,
and recurring research investment support durable pricing, margins, and cash generation. Defense,
space, unmanned systems, infrared imaging, marine instrumentation, and industrial measurement offer
multiple independent growth vectors. The contrary case is that a material valuation premium embeds
continued execution: acquisition-heavy goodwill and intangibles, customer budgets, fixed-price
programs, tariffs, rare-earth and specialty-material supply, integration, and product mix can all
make earnings and cash flow less durable than the headline growth suggests.

For the quarter ended 28 June 2026, net sales rose 9.8% year over year to USD 1,662.5 million and
attributable net income rose 19.9% to USD 251.7 million, or USD 5.37 per diluted share. Operating
margin increased to 20.0% from 18.4%. Only USD 12.2 million of quarterly revenue was incremental
sales from recent acquisitions, so most of the advance was organic. Digital Imaging led the growth,
while all four reportable segments increased sales.

## Alert review, catalysts, and risks

From 7 July through 4 August the adjusted close rose 16.39% to USD 684.76. On 4 August it closed
3.68% above the USD 660.46 upper Bollinger band, while RSI was 75.05 and volume was not anomalous.
The move followed strong Q2 results, record orders and backlog, raised guidance, margin expansion,
and debt repayment, so it has a verified fundamental catalyst. However, the market now discounts
those improvements at a premium multiple and above the base and probability-weighted values. The
alert is therefore positive fundamental confirmation but unfavorable entry timing, not a paper-buy
signal.

Catalysts are conversion of the funded backlog, sustained infrared and unmanned-systems demand,
defense and space awards, marine and environmental growth, organic margin expansion without tariff
refunds, continued free-cash-flow conversion, disciplined bolt-on acquisitions, and further debt
reduction. Risks are government-budget or program timing, fixed-price execution, industrial and
commercial-aerospace cycles, tariffs and trade restrictions, rare-earth and specialty-material
availability, customer concentration, foreign exchange, acquisition integration, goodwill or
intangible impairment, non-GAAP normalization, and valuation compression.

Invalidate the thesis if organic growth stalls despite the backlog, Digital Imaging margins reverse
without a temporary explanation, funded orders fail to convert to revenue and cash, Instrumentation
mix remains persistently adverse, fixed-price losses or supply constraints rise, acquisitions
consume capital without adequate per-share returns, net leverage increases materially, or free cash
flow persistently fails to support reported earnings.
