# Veralto Corporation

## Research summary

Veralto is a NYSE-listed essential-technology company serving water quality and product quality applications. Its portfolio combines analytical instruments, treatment systems, consumables, software, and services. Approximately 60% of sales are recurring, supporting durable customer relationships and cash generation.

This initial review changes the page from unresearched to **supported**. A 12-month adjusted-EPS scenario model produces a probability-weighted fair value of **$109.03 per share**, modestly above the supplied **$104.35** closing reference for October 23, 2026. Confidence is **medium** because the latest opened financial results cover the quarter ended July 3, the pending Cleanwater1 transaction remains prospective, and current balance-sheet detail was not available in the opened evidence.

## Business economics

Veralto reported second-quarter sales of $1.474 billion, up 7.6%, with 4.2% core growth. Water Quality led with 5.7% core growth, while Product Quality and Innovation grew 2.0%. Adjusted operating margin was 24.6%, and trailing free cash flow was $1.047 billion on $5.696 billion of sales, an 18.4% margin. These results support a durable-business assessment, although reported operating margin declined to 21.4% from 22.8% and adjusted results excluded amortization, restructuring, and other items.

Management raised 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $4.35-$4.43 and expected 4.0%-4.5% full-year core growth, 25-50 basis points of adjusted margin expansion, and free-cash-flow conversion above 100% of GAAP net earnings. The guidance includes an approximately $0.05 benefit from tariff recoveries, which is treated as non-recurring in the valuation.

## Water-infrastructure thesis

The water thesis has direct operating support. Management attributed Water Quality demand to industrial treatment, water reuse, and environmental monitoring, while municipal water analytics remained steady. The proposed $465 million Cleanwater1 acquisition, or approximately $452 million after estimated tax benefits, would add hypochlorite disinfection, solids management, odor filtration, and an installed base of roughly 25,000 units. Cleanwater1 generated approximately $135 million of trailing sales, with about 25% aftermarket revenue, but its figures are unaudited and prospective synergies are not yet verified.

The relationship is positive rather than pure: Product Quality and Innovation is a separate business, and Veralto does not disclose revenue specifically attributable to reuse or monitoring.

## Downside and invalidation

Downside includes delayed municipal projects, constrained utility budgets, weaker industrial demand, slower digital-workflow adoption, currency and trade pressure, unsuccessful acquisition integration, and valuation-multiple compression. Paying approximately 17x Cleanwater1's trailing adjusted EBITDA before synergies raises execution discipline as a material issue.

The thesis would be weakened by sustained sub-profile core growth, deteriorating recurring revenue or cash conversion, persistent unexplained GAAP margin contraction, failure of water reuse and monitoring demand to generate revenue, acquisitions earning below the cost of capital, material refinancing stress, or forward adjusted EPS remaining below $4.25 without a temporary explanation.

## Catalysts

Potential catalysts include delivery of full-year core growth guidance, second-half acceleration, continued Water Quality demand, Product Quality and Innovation improvement, margin expansion, free-cash-flow conversion above 100%, and successful closing and integration of Cleanwater1.

## Limitations

The latest opened financial release covers the quarter ended July 3, 2026. No current detailed balance sheet or debt schedule was available in the opened evidence. Cleanwater1 data are unaudited and not conformed to Veralto's GAAP policies. Scenario EPS, multiples, probabilities, and synergies are analytical assumptions, not company forecasts.
