## Economics and thesis

The investment case is a transition from a price-sensitive concentrate producer into a vertically
integrated producer of separated NdPr, magnetic precursor products, and NdFeB magnets. The full Q2
review supports operating progress but not yet mature common-equity economics. Q2 NdPr output rose
41% year over year to 840 metric tons and sales rose 127% to 1,006 metric tons. Materials generated
USD 95.6 million of revenue and USD 32.5 million of segment adjusted EBITDA, while Magnetics
generated USD 16.5 million and USD 7.5 million, respectively. Independence qualification deliveries,
accelerating 10X construction, and a new long-term gadolinium customer extend the vertical-integration
case.

The contractual support is not equivalent to low-risk recurring earnings. Q2 consolidated revenue
was USD 108.5 million and price-protection income was USD 17.6 million, but GAAP net loss remained
USD 20.3 million. Results depend materially on government agreements, performance obligations and
appropriations while Mountain Pass pricing, construction execution and customer concentration remain
consequential. Six-month stock compensation of USD 24.2 million, start-up costs of USD 18.4 million
and capital expenditure of USD 307.7 million show why adjusted EBITDA cannot be capitalized as if it
were distributable common earnings.

## Catalysts, risks, and invalidation

Catalysts are sustained NdPr output and cost delivery, commercial magnet production at Independence,
commissioning of heavy-rare-earth separation, 10X construction milestones, contracted offtake and
price-protection cash conversion, and additional diversified customers. Robotics demand is optionality,
not a current valuation premise, because the company does not disclose material robotics-specific revenue.

Risks include commodity and China-linked pricing, single-site operating disruption, government
appropriations or contract changes, GM and public-sector concentration, construction and technology
scale-up, high capital needs, debt and preferred claims, stock compensation and dilution, and the
possibility that protected economics do not translate into durable free cash flow. Invalidate the
thesis if NdPr production or magnet qualification persistently misses plan, contracted protections
become unavailable or uneconomic, 10X or Independence materially slips, liquidity falls without
commensurate operating cash generation, or dilution prevents operating progress from improving
normalized common EPS.
