# Stratasys Ltd. ordinary shares (NASDAQ: SSYS)

## Research update

This is the first research page for the security. It establishes the issuer and instrument identity, summarizes current operating evidence, and adds a complete 12-month bear/base/bull valuation. No prior valuation figures were carried forward.

## Identity

The supplied identity is consistent with the issuer's filings and releases: Stratasys Ltd. identifies itself as the registrant and its ordinary shares as Nasdaq-listed under **SSYS**. The assessed instrument is the supplied Stratasys Ltd. ordinary share, with USD as its market and valuation currency. The immutable security and issuer identifiers have not been changed.

## Business economics

Stratasys sells polymer additive-manufacturing systems, consumables, software and services. Its economics combine comparatively cyclical capital-equipment sales with repeat materials and service demand from an installed base. The company says its portfolio spans manufacturing workflows and industries including aerospace, automotive and healthcare, supported by approximately 2,700 granted and pending additive-technology patents. It estimated that manufacturing solutions supplied more than 37.5% of 2025 revenue ([operating review](https://investors.stratasys.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-056548/ex992q2-26.htm), `source_web_7501755f3682ac03b14a266e`).

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was **$137.6 million**, nearly unchanged from $138.1 million a year earlier but 3.7% above the first quarter. Consumables reached a record **$66.3 million**, and aerospace and defense revenue increased 17% year over year. Against those positives, GAAP gross margin declined to 42.3%, adjusted EBITDA declined to $5.3 million, and the company recorded a $16.9 million GAAP net loss ([Q2 release](https://investors.stratasys.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/989/stratasys-releases-second-quarter-2026-financial-results), `source_web_b2a9e2eedad7d6151ef1f3b4`).

Management attributes weak systems demand partly to macroeconomic pressure on customer capital budgets and longer sales cycles. It also identifies tariffs, foreign exchange, inflation and energy costs as margin risks. This is important contrary evidence: growth in consumables and aerospace and defense has not yet produced broad revenue growth or consistent GAAP profitability ([operating review](https://investors.stratasys.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-056548/ex992q2-26.htm), `source_web_7501755f3682ac03b14a266e`).

## Balance sheet and cash flow

At June 30, 2026, Stratasys reported **$212.5 million** of cash, equivalents and short-term deposits and **no debt**. However, it used $18.7 million of operating cash during the second quarter. Because first-half cash usage exceeded earlier expectations, management no longer expected positive operating cash flow for full-year 2026, although it expected positive cash flow in the second half ([Q2 release](https://investors.stratasys.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/989/stratasys-releases-second-quarter-2026-financial-results), `source_web_b2a9e2eedad7d6151ef1f3b4`). The underlying quarterly filing and financial statements are available in the [Form 6-K](https://investors.stratasys.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-056548/ssys-20260630_d2.htm) (`source_web_3c796184ac88273141711788`) and [financial-statement exhibit](https://investors.stratasys.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-056548/ssys-20260630.htm) (`source_web_f37f925d3523693f6a316a14`).

## MarkForged transaction

Stratasys agreed to acquire MarkForged in an all-cash transaction valued at **$42.5 million**, subject to customary adjustments. The acquired business was described as having generated approximately $70 million of 2025 revenue, including a metal binder-jetting line that the seller will retain. Stratasys expects gross-margin accretion, cost synergies and positive adjusted EBITDA contribution within one year after closing, but these remain management expectations rather than demonstrated results ([transaction release](https://investors.stratasys.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/984/stratasys-to-acquire-markforged-inc-expanding-aerospace), `source_web_4e56c72bb198dfa22a2a6409`). The August results still described the acquisition as pending, so closing adjustments and integration performance are unknown.

## Outlook

Management's 2026 outlook calls for:

- Revenue of **$565 million-$575 million**.
- Non-GAAP operating margin of **0.7%-1.5%**.
- Adjusted EBITDA of **$25 million-$30 million**.
- Capital expenditures of **$20 million-$25 million**.
- GAAP net loss of **$83 million-$67 million**.

These figures are management guidance, not independently verified forecasts. The release also warns that its non-GAAP outlook cannot be fully reconciled to certain GAAP measures without unreasonable effort ([Q2 release](https://investors.stratasys.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/989/stratasys-releases-second-quarter-2026-financial-results), `source_web_b2a9e2eedad7d6151ef1f3b4`).

## Valuation method

A 12-month forward adjusted-EBITDA enterprise-value framework is used because GAAP earnings and near-term operating cash flow are negative and affected by acquisition, restructuring and working-capital items.

Estimated post-acquisition net cash is **approximately $170 million**:

- $212.5 million reported cash, equivalents and short-term deposits;
- less $42.5 million announced MarkForged cash consideration;
- no reported debt.

The share denominator is **approximately 89 million**, inferred from the reported $16.9 million quarterly loss and $0.19 loss per share. Because those figures are rounded, this is an approximation and a material limitation.

| Scenario | Probability | Forward adjusted EBITDA | EV multiple | Equity-value arithmetic | Fair value/share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Bear | 30% | $20m | 7x | ($20m × 7 + $170m) / 89m | **$3.48** |
| Base | 45% | $40m | 10x | ($40m × 10 + $170m) / 89m | **$6.40** |
| Bull | 25% | $60m | 12x | ($60m × 12 + $170m) / 89m | **$10.00** |

The bear case assumes prolonged systems weakness and margin pressure. The base case assumes delivery against 2026 guidance plus partial MarkForged contribution and synergies. The bull case requires strong aerospace and defense conversion, sustained consumables growth and substantial acquisition-driven operating leverage. The probability-weighted value is approximately **$6.38 per share**, compared with the supplied October 16, 2026 close of **$6.94**; the valuation therefore does not show an obvious discount at that price.

## Downside and contrary evidence

- Systems demand remains exposed to customer capital budgets and long sales cycles.
- Revenue was nearly flat year over year despite record consumables.
- GAAP losses remain substantial.
- Second-quarter operating cash use was $18.7 million, and full-year positive operating cash flow is no longer expected.
- Tariffs and foreign exchange are expected to reduce 2026 margins.
- MarkForged may fail to deliver forecast synergies or may require more cash than the announced consideration.
- The inferred share count and issuer-defined adjusted EBITDA reduce valuation precision.

## Catalysts

- Delivery of the 2026 revenue and adjusted-EBITDA outlook.
- Positive operating cash flow during the second half of 2026.
- Continued consumables records and conversion of the aerospace and defense pipeline.
- MarkForged closing followed by quantified accretion and cost savings.
- Recovery in systems orders or reduced tariff and currency pressure.

## Invalidation conditions

Reassess the valuation if revenue contracts persistently, second-half operating cash flow remains negative, adjusted EBITDA falls materially below guidance, consumables weaken, or aerospace and defense growth fails to create company-wide operating leverage. Recalculate immediately if the MarkForged transaction fails to close or changes materially, if its integration requires substantial additional cash, if the actual diluted share count differs materially from approximately 89 million, or if Stratasys announces another material transaction.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: medium.** The balance sheet, quarterly results and guidance are supported by current issuer materials, but evidence only extends through June 30, 2026. The acquisition's closing status and economics remain uncertain. The share count is inferred from rounded per-share disclosure, and adjusted EBITDA excludes real GAAP costs. The valuation multiples and forward EBITDA cases are analytical assumptions rather than issuer forecasts, and no comparable-company study was used.
