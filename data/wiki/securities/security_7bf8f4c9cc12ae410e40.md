AbCellera is a clinical-stage antibody company combining partnered discovery economics with an
internally led pipeline. No maintained idea or accepted relationship is currently linked to this

## Economics and thesis

The investable thesis is that AbCellera's discovery, T-cell-engager, GPCR and ion-channel platforms
can repeatedly create partnered programs while a successful internal drug retains more economics.
The Jazz and Vertex agreements are concrete platform validation: Jazz paid USD 56 million for two
initial programs with another USD 28 million due when a third starts, and Vertex paid USD 28 million
while funding research and development. These payments do not establish clinical success or durable
royalty revenue.

Q2 revenue was USD 4.1 million and net loss was USD 55.4 million. Partner-led programs with
downstream interests declined to 35 from 44 at year-end, and clinical molecules with downstreams
declined to 12. At 30 June, cash, equivalents and marketable securities were USD 540.1 million;
restricted cash and government claims lifted stated available liquidity above USD 675 million.
Management expects the current resources to fund at least 36 months, but continued losses and a
306.5-million share count make burn and dilution central to per-share value.

## Clinical evidence and decision gates

ABCL635 is the decisive near-term program. The company reported favorable Phase 1 tolerability,
target-engagement biomarkers and pharmacokinetics compatible with monthly dosing, then completed
Phase 2 enrollment with top-line efficacy data expected in August 2026. ClinicalTrials.gov identifies
`NCT07118891` as a randomized Phase 1/2 study with estimated enrollment of 136; the registry was
active but not recruiting and measures changes in vasomotor-symptom frequency and severity.

Phase 1 target engagement is not evidence of Phase 2 symptom efficacy. The 10-Q also states that the
FDA may require bridging or repeated U.S. studies before a Phase 3 start and that preliminary data
can change after fuller review. ABCL575 completed Phase 1 dosing, but management currently has no
plan to develop it beyond Phase 1. ABCL386 and ABCL688 remain preclinical or IND-enabling programs
with planned 2027 clinical starts.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable fair-value scenarios are recorded. The `biotechnology_binary` template requires a
risk-adjusted NPV with technical, regulatory, commercial, timing and dilution probabilities. Before
the Phase 2 dataset, the repository lacks defensible ABCL635 efficacy, program-specific approval
probability, development cost and timing, pricing, addressable treated population, peak penetration,
partner-option economics, durable burn, and fully diluted share inputs. Cash is not treated as
distributable because it funds ongoing clinical and platform work.

Catalysts are the August ABCL635 Phase 2 readout, ABCL575 Phase 1 data in Q4, Jazz's third-program
start, additional partner options, and 2027 clinical entries for ABCL386 or ABCL688. Risks are Phase
2 efficacy or safety failure, FDA bridging requirements, early-program attrition, partner control of
downstream execution, lumpy revenue, sustained losses, dilution, competitive menopause treatments,
manufacturing execution, and intellectual-property disputes.

Invalidate the thesis if ABCL635 does not show clinically meaningful symptom improvement with
acceptable safety, if partners discontinue programs faster than new funded programs replenish them,
if burn shortens the stated runway materially, or if dilution prevents operating progress from
accruing to each share.
