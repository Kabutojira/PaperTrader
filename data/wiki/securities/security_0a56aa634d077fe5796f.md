## Business economics

Applied Materials supplies deposition, etch, thermal-processing, planarization, metrology, inspection, packaging and implantation systems used in semiconductor manufacturing. Applied Global Services supplies spares, service agreements, equipment optimization and factory software, providing an installed-base revenue stream alongside more cyclical equipment sales.

Fiscal-Q3 2026 revenue reached USD 9.115 billion, up 25% year over year. GAAP gross margin was 50.3%, operating income was USD 3.075 billion, operating margin was 33.7%, and diluted EPS was USD 3.17. Semiconductor Systems generated USD 7.040 billion of revenue at a 37.7% operating margin. AGS generated USD 1.781 billion at a 30.1% operating margin. These figures support durable process-technology positions and strong service economics, although they also represent unusually strong cycle conditions.

Operating cash flow was USD 3.037 billion in the quarter and USD 5.568 billion through the first nine months. Nine-month operating cash flow trailed USD 7.370 billion of net income because operating assets and liabilities absorbed USD 1.877 billion, so fiscal-Q3 cash generation should not be extrapolated mechanically.

## Thesis and contrary evidence

The central thesis is that greater transistor complexity, HBM, advanced packaging and leading-edge foundry-logic raise materials-engineering intensity, while the installed base compounds service demand. The fiscal-Q3 release supports this mechanism through strong Semiconductor Systems and AGS results, new DRAM and advanced-packaging systems, and management's fiscal-Q4 outlook of USD 10.25 billion revenue plus or minus USD 500 million and non-GAAP diluted EPS of USD 4.02 plus or minus USD 0.20.

Material contrary evidence prevents high confidence:

- Customer investment is cyclical, and AI-related demand timing is difficult to forecast.
- Two customers represented 21% and 15% of fiscal-2026 revenue through Q3.
- China represented 28% of fiscal-Q3 revenue, compared with 35% a year earlier.
- Export rules have limited markets and can favor foreign or domestic Chinese competitors.
- Applied paid USD 253 million under a BIS export-control settlement during fiscal Q2 and remains subject to related compliance obligations.
- Receivables, inventories and capital investment are growing as the company prepares for anticipated demand that may not be sustained.

These issues are discussed in the [Form 10-Q](https://cdn.yahoofinance.com/prod/sec-filings/0000006951/000162828026058235/amat-20260726.htm) (`source_web_88496423b8d6ae60fd78a2e9`).

## Balance sheet

At 2026-07-26, cash and cash equivalents were USD 7.037 billion, short-term investments were USD 2.196 billion and long-term investments were USD 5.268 billion, totaling USD 14.501 billion. Short-term debt was USD 1.299 billion and long-term debt was USD 5.245 billion. Current assets of USD 25.095 billion exceeded current liabilities of USD 10.357 billion, and no balances were drawn under the principal revolving facilities.

This is ample liquidity with low near-term financing stress. The principal qualifications are a USD 1.199 billion note classified as current, growing inventories and receivables, and expanding manufacturing and R&D investment. These are manageable constraints rather than evidence of refinancing pressure.

## Catalysts, downside and invalidation

Potential catalysts include fiscal-Q4 guidance conversion, sustained foundry-logic and DRAM demand, HBM and advanced-packaging adoption, commercialization of new systems, EPIC Center partner activity, AGS growth and durable cash conversion.

Downside could arise from synchronized logic and memory spending weakness, order volatility from concentrated customers, additional export restrictions, displacement by local competitors, weak product adoption, inventory or capacity overbuild, lower cash conversion and valuation-multiple compression. Strong liquidity reduces financing risk but cannot prevent an earnings-cycle drawdown.

The thesis would require material revision if leading-edge logic and DRAM weaken together, AGS growth stalls, margins decline structurally, new products fail to gain adoption, free cash flow persistently trails normalized earnings, major customer losses emerge, or export restrictions and compliance failures materially reduce the addressable market. The valuation must also be revisited if normalized EPS cannot support the USD 13 bear floor.
