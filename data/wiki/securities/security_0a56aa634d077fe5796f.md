## Economics and thesis

Applied Materials supplies deposition, etch, thermal processing, planarization, metrology,
inspection, packaging and implantation systems used in semiconductor manufacturing. Applied Global
Services supplies spares, service agreements, equipment optimization and factory software. Fiscal
Q2 2026 revenue rose 11% to a record USD 7.91 billion, GAAP gross margin reached 49.9%, GAAP
operating margin reached 31.9%, and diluted EPS rose 33% to USD 3.51.

Semiconductor Systems revenue rose 10% to USD 5.965 billion and segment operating margin expanded
to 35.1%. Foundry, logic and other represented 67% of segment revenue, DRAM 29%, and flash 4%. AGS
revenue rose 17% to USD 1.665 billion with a 29.2% operating margin, providing a recurring installed-
base counterweight to equipment cyclicality. Management expects the semiconductor-equipment
business to grow more than 30% in calendar 2026 and guided fiscal Q3 revenue to USD 8.95 billion,
plus or minus USD 500 million, with non-GAAP diluted EPS of USD 3.36, plus or minus USD 0.20.

The thesis is that more complex leading-edge logic, DRAM and advanced packaging raise materials-
engineering intensity, while the installed base compounds service demand. The counter-thesis is
that the current price capitalizes prolonged above-cycle growth despite customer concentration,
China and export-control exposure, semiconductor cyclicality, supply execution and weak recent
free-cash-flow conversion.
