Coinbase is a direct platform exposure within [[ideas/idea_digital_finance_crypto_rails]].

## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 net revenue was USD 1.34 billion: transaction revenue USD 756 million and subscription and
services revenue USD 584 million. Stablecoin revenue rose 11% to USD 305 million as USDC balances
grew, supporting a payment-rails mechanism, but total subscription and services revenue declined
14% and transaction revenue fell materially with the market cycle. Net loss was USD 394 million,
adjusted EBITDA USD 303 million, and operating cash flow USD 183 million.

The balance sheet reports USD 10.4 billion of cash, equivalents, and marketable investments, but the
accounting now classifies corporate payment stablecoins as cash equivalents. Coinbase also held full
keys to USD 294 billion of customer crypto and payment stablecoins, creating custody obligations.
One counterparty produced 23% of total revenue, and lower earned rates already reduced corporate
interest income.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No supportable valuation is recorded without a validated current mark and a clean bridge between
corporate liquidity, customer assets, borrowings, crypto inventory, and diluted shares. A model must
scenario trading volumes and take rates, USDC balances and interest rates, counterparty terms,
custody costs and losses, credit, regulation, stock compensation, and acquisitions.

Catalysts are sustained USDC growth despite lower rates, recurring subscription growth, derivatives
and institutional share gains, and GAAP profitability. Invalidate if crypto activity contracts,
stablecoin revenue or counterparty economics weaken, fees compress, custody or credit losses occur,
regulation limits products, or equity compensation absorbs per-share cash generation.
