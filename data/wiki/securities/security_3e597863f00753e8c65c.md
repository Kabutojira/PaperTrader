## Current economics and thesis

Bloom sells modular solid-oxide fuel-cell systems for onsite electricity. The investment mechanism
is falsifiable: where utility interconnection cannot meet AI-data-centre schedules, rapidly deployed
firm onsite power can convert time-to-power scarcity into orders, revenue and operating leverage.
The mechanism weakens if fuel, permitting, emissions, cost, supply, financing, reliability, or
competing generation erase the deployment advantage.

Q2 revenue reached USD 1.065 billion, up 165.5% year over year, with USD 935 million of product
revenue. GAAP gross margin was 33.4%, operating income USD 182 million and operating cash flow USD
226 million. Management raised 2026 guidance to USD 3.9-4.2 billion of revenue, about 34% non-GAAP
gross margin, USD 800-900 million of non-GAAP operating income and USD 2.55-2.85 of non-GAAP EPS.
The release showed USD 2.689 billion of ending cash and restricted cash and 323.3 million diluted
weighted-average shares.

The Oracle master agreement supports up to 2.8 GW, with 1.2 GW contracted and deploying. The related
SEC filing also records a fully vested warrant for up to 3.53 million shares at USD 113.28. The
company says major U.S. hyperscalers and more than a dozen neocloud, AI-lab and colocation operators
have approved its systems, but that issuer statement is validation evidence rather than disclosed
customer-level economics.

## Catalysts, risks and invalidation

Catalysts are delivery of the contracted Oracle capacity, evidence that other approved AI customers
become material orders, completion of the Fremont expansion from 1 GW to 2 GW annual run-rate,
sustained gross margin and cash conversion, and a lower share price that restores scenario upside.

Risks include customer and quarter-to-quarter shipment concentration, production and installation
bottlenecks, warranties and service cost, natural-gas price or availability, carbon and permitting
rules, competition from turbines, engines, grids, storage and nuclear, convertible and warrant
dilution, and valuation compression. Bloom's July filing rejected third-party accounting and
scandium-supply allegations and stated its supply is not China-dependent; that is a company response,
not independent resolution, so supply and reporting scrutiny remain monitored contradictions.

Invalidate or downgrade the operating thesis if contracted deployments are cancelled or delayed,
non-related-party demand fails to broaden, gross margin or recurring cash conversion reverses,
manufacturing expansion slips or service liabilities rise materially, fuel/emissions economics
lose customer acceptance, or disclosed dilution and financing overwhelm per-share earnings.
