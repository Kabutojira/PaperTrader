## Business economics

Intuitive Machines is attempting to combine spacecraft manufacturing, lunar delivery, communications networks, mission operations, and ground infrastructure. Its potential economic advantage is a broader system offering across civil, commercial, and national-security customers. Scale and backlog can improve capacity utilization and customer relevance if contracts convert on acceptable margins.

The principal economic risks are contract concentration, government funding and termination rights, fixed-price cost overruns, milestone timing, mission failure, working-capital consumption, integration expense, and dilution. Reported backlog is management's estimate of future revenue from awarded contracts; it is not cash and does not establish timing, margin, funding certainty, or collectability by itself.

## Thesis and contrary evidence

The constructive thesis is that approximately USD 206 million of quarterly revenue, a USD 1.8 billion backlog, and USD 900 million-USD 1 billion of annual guidance demonstrate a company reaching meaningful aerospace scale. The acquired ground-network assets could broaden its communications offering, while commercial GEO and national-security programs could reduce reliance on individual lunar missions.

The contrary evidence is equally material. The opened release does not quantify sustainable gross margin, acquisition contribution, integration cost, post-closing debt, normalized free cash flow, or fully diluted Class A-equivalent claims. Positive adjusted EBITDA guidance is not the same as GAAP profitability or free cash flow. Backlog can also include performance, funding, and timing uncertainty. These omissions prevent verified enterprise value from being converted into per-share equity value.

## Valuation method and blocker

A forward revenue multiple is the appropriate provisional method for a rapidly growing company without established, durable earnings. The required arithmetic is:

`fair value per share = (forward revenue × selected EV/revenue multiple + cash − debt − other senior or acquisition claims) ÷ normalized fully diluted Class A-equivalent claims`

The release supplies an issuer revenue range and June cash, but not all other terms in that equation. In particular, this review lacks accessible primary evidence for:

1. post-Goonhilly cash, debt, working-capital and purchase-price adjustments;
2. acquired revenue, margins, integration expense and capital requirements;
3. a normalized fully diluted Class A-equivalent denominator covering other classes, warrants, awards, preferred stock and convertibles;
4. sustainable consolidated gross margin and free-cash-flow conversion; and
5. a current, evidence-based comparable-company or transaction multiple range.

Accordingly, bear, base, and bull fair values and probabilities remain empty. The October market price is not used as fair value, and no prior scenario number is carried forward.

### Scenario framework without unsupported outputs

- **Bear:** Start from the USD 900 million guidance floor and test poor backlog conversion, fixed-price pressure, continued cash burn, integration costs, and a conservative multiple. A value requires verified net debt and diluted claims.
- **Base:** Start near the guidance midpoint and assume adequate execution with gradual margin improvement. A value still requires post-closing capitalization and a defensible multiple.
- **Bull:** Require revenue near or above USD 1 billion, strong funded-award conversion, improving margins and cash generation, and successful communications expansion. The same capitalization gaps prevent a value or probability.

## Downside, catalysts, and invalidation

Downside includes cancelled or delayed awards, funding shortfalls, cost overruns, mission failures, integration disruption, persistent cash consumption, and financing-related dilution. The June cash balance is meaningful, but it cannot establish post-acquisition liquidity without current debt and cash-flow reconciliation.

Potential catalysts are backlog conversion, delivery against annual guidance, positive adjusted EBITDA accompanied by improving cash flow, commercial and national-security program execution, successful lunar missions, and quantified economics from Goonhilly and COMSAT.

Invalidate the constructive thesis if revenue materially misses guidance, headline backlog does not convert, fixed-price losses persist, liquidity deteriorates without operating progress, acquisition integration impairs delivery or controls, key missions fail, or dilution and senior claims grow faster than enterprise value.
