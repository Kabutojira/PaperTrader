## Business economics

Twist operates a synthetic-DNA platform serving diagnostics, therapeutics, industrial, agricultural, and research markets. Products and services include oligonucleotides, genes, proteins, libraries, characterization data, antibody-discovery solutions, and next-generation-sequencing workflow tools.

The economic case rests on miniaturized, high-throughput DNA synthesis and shared manufacturing infrastructure. If demand and utilization rise faster than manufacturing and commercial costs, the platform can produce gross-margin and operating leverage. Product breadth and revenue growth do not by themselves establish pricing power, customer retention, positive free cash flow, or per-share value creation.

## Thesis and contrary evidence

The thesis is that sustained DSPS and NGS growth, greater gene shipments, improving gross margin, and higher manufacturing utilization can move Twist toward positive cash economics. The balance sheet strengthened considerably through the equity offering, giving the company more time to pursue operating leverage.

Contrary evidence remains material. Twist was loss-making on both GAAP and adjusted-EBITDA bases in fiscal Q3. SG&A reached USD 80.7 million, compared with quarterly gross profit of approximately USD 62.5 million, and stock-based compensation remained significant. Adjusted EBITDA excludes stock compensation and other items and therefore is not a substitute for cash flow. The investment case depends on operating improvement occurring before continuing losses and dilution consume the benefit of growth.

## Valuation

**Research status: `supported`**  
**Method: forward enterprise value to revenue**  
**Horizon: 12 months**

Twist remains unprofitable, making near-term earnings and cash-flow methods unusually sensitive to the assumed date and level of profitability. The valuation therefore starts with the USD 456.5 million midpoint of fiscal-2026 revenue guidance and applies explicit fiscal-2027 growth assumptions.

The framework is:

`forward revenue = USD 456.5 million × (1 + scenario growth)`

`enterprise value = forward revenue × scenario EV/revenue multiple`

`equity value = enterprise value + scenario net financial resources`

`fair value per share = equity value ÷ 68 million estimated diluted shares`

The 68 million denominator comprises 62.4 million June weighted-average shares, 3.594 million offering shares, and an approximate 2 million reserve for unverified equity-award dilution. It is an analytical estimate rather than a verified post-offering fully diluted count.

- EV/revenue multiple: **6x**.
- Net financial resources: **USD 300 million**.
- Arithmetic: `(USD 525 million × 6 + USD 300 million) ÷ 68 million = approximately USD 51`.

This case reflects growth deceleration, persistent cash use and SG&A intensity, weaker operating leverage, and multiple compression.

### Bull: USD 107 per share — 20%

The probabilities total 100%. The revenue growth rates, multiples, net-resource balances, and dilution reserve are analyst assumptions, not management guidance. No quoted market price is used as a proxy for intrinsic value.

## Downside, catalysts, and invalidation

Downside mechanisms include slower customer adoption, pricing pressure, manufacturing underutilization, quality or turnaround failures, competition, technological substitution, critical-supplier dependence, regulation, trade disruption, continuing cash use, and dilution. Valuation is especially exposed to multiple compression because all scenarios apply a substantial revenue multiple to a company without sustained cash profitability.

Potential catalysts include confirmation of fiscal-2026 guidance, achievement of fiscal-Q4 adjusted-EBITDA breakeven, fiscal-2027 guidance at or above the base-case growth rate, sustained gross margin above 52%, positive operating cash flow, and a post-offering balance sheet confirming substantial liquidity. Demonstrated recurring economics from antibody-discovery and AI-enabled applications could support a higher growth outlook, but no incremental value is assigned solely for announced applications.

The thesis should be reduced or rejected if fiscal-2026 revenue misses guidance, fiscal-2027 growth falls materially below 15%, gross margin retreats below 50%, adjusted-EBITDA breakeven is missed or does not lead toward positive cash flow, SG&A continues to outgrow revenue, customer or shipment trends weaken, or diluted shares rise materially above 68 million.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **low**. The offering evidence resolves the previous uncertainty over share issuance, pricing, option exercise, and expected proceeds, making scenario arithmetic possible. Important uncertainties remain:

1. no fiscal-2027 guidance has been verified;
2. no post-offering balance sheet or current fully diluted share count is available;
3. the 2 million equity-award dilution reserve is an estimate;
4. subsequent operating and capital cash use is unknown;
5. the 6x, 9x, and 12x revenue multiples are judgmental rather than sourced from an opened peer analysis; and
6. small changes in growth or multiples produce large changes in fair value.
