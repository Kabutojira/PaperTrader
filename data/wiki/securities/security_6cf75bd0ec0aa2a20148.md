## Business economics

Twist operates a synthetic-DNA platform serving diagnostics, therapeutics, industrial, agricultural, and research markets. Its products and services include oligonucleotides, genes, proteins, libraries, characterization data, antibody-discovery solutions, and next-generation-sequencing workflow tools. The economic proposition is that miniaturized, high-throughput DNA synthesis can improve customer speed and cost while allowing shared manufacturing infrastructure to support several product categories.

The platform can create operating leverage if volume and revenue rise faster than manufacturing, commercial, and administrative costs. Product breadth alone does not establish pricing power, retention, positive free cash flow, or per-share value creation.
