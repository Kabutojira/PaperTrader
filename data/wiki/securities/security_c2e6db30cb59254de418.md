## Business economics

Xometry operates a two-sided marketplace for custom-manufactured parts and assemblies. Buyers submit designs and specifications; Xometry's software recommends processes, generates pricing and lead-time information, and routes work using supplier capability, quality and delivery data. Revenue is predominantly marketplace revenue from parts and assemblies, supplemented by higher-margin supplier and marketing services.

The marketplace can benefit from a data loop: more quoted and completed jobs provide information about geometry, process requirements, supplier suitability, quality and delivery. Better predictions can improve buyer conversion and supplier utilization. This advantage is not automatically durable, however. Buyers and suppliers can use multiple channels, fulfillment quality remains operationally demanding, and rapid volume growth can occur without expanding gross margin.

- Adjusted EBITDA of USD14.1 million versus USD3.9 million a year earlier.

These figures show buyer expansion and operating leverage, but there is contrary evidence. Quarterly marketplace gross margin declined to 34.7% from 35.4% year over year. First-half stock-based compensation was USD19.7 million, exceeding reported operating cash flow. International adjusted EBITDA was still negative, and consolidated GAAP net loss was USD10.6 million for the first half.

## Balance sheet and dilution

The balance-sheet increase came largely from USD248.4 million of net common-stock offering proceeds and USD50 million from a private placement.

## Valuation method

A revenue multiple is used because growth is high but GAAP earnings remain negative. Each scenario estimates 2027 revenue, applies an enterprise-value-to-revenue multiple, adds scenario net cash and divides by diluted shares. The multiples are analytical assumptions, not reported facts, and current peer comparables were not available in the opened evidence.

### Bear: USD47 per share — 25%

- 2027 revenue: USD1.00 billion
- Enterprise-value/revenue multiple: 3.0x
- Net cash: USD0.10 billion
- Diluted shares: 66 million
- Arithmetic: `(USD1.00bn × 3.0 + USD0.10bn) / 66m ≈ USD47`

This case assumes growth slows sharply, marketplace gross margin remains under pressure, cash conversion weakens and dilution continues.

### Base: USD79 per share — 50%

- 2027 revenue: USD1.10 billion
- Enterprise-value/revenue multiple: 4.5x
- Net cash: USD0.188 billion
- Diluted shares: 65 million
- Arithmetic: `(USD1.10bn × 4.5 + USD0.188bn) / 65m ≈ USD79`

This case assumes growth moderates after 2026, marketplace gross margin stabilizes and operating leverage continues, while GAAP profitability remains limited.

### Bull: USD116 per share — 25%

- 2027 revenue: USD1.20 billion
- Enterprise-value/revenue multiple: 6.0x
- Net cash: USD0.25 billion
- Diluted shares: 64 million
- Arithmetic: `(USD1.20bn × 6.0 + USD0.25bn) / 64m ≈ USD116`

This requires sustained marketplace growth, stronger pricing and routing economics, durable cash generation and contained dilution.

The probability-weighted value is USD80.25: `(USD47 × 25%) + (USD79 × 50%) + (USD116 × 25%)`. It is approximately 10% below the supplied USD88.94 market snapshot, while the base value is approximately 11% below it. The market therefore appears to discount an outcome above the modeled base case.

## Probability rationale

The base case receives 50% because reported buyer growth, larger-account growth, raised guidance and improving adjusted EBITDA support continued expansion, but do not yet establish mature margins or GAAP profitability. The 25% bear weight reflects marketplace gross-margin contraction, cyclicality, stock compensation and dilution. The 25% bull weight recognizes accelerating marketplace revenue and product improvements, while requiring execution beyond what the current financial evidence proves.

## Catalysts

- Delivery of the issuer's Q3 and full-year 2026 guidance.
- Marketplace revenue continuing to outgrow consolidated revenue.
- Stabilization or expansion of marketplace gross margin.
- Sustained operating cash flow after capitalized software and equipment spending.
- Continued growth in accounts spending at least USD50,000.
- Measurable evidence that process recommendation, cost prediction and adaptive sourcing improve conversion, delivery reliability or contribution economics.

## Risks and downside

- The USD47 bear value is approximately 47% below the supplied market price.
- Marketplace gross margin contracted year over year despite rapid marketplace growth.
- Stock-based compensation exceeded operating cash flow in the first half.
- The June equity transactions materially increased outstanding shares.
- Adjusted EBITDA excludes equity compensation and other costs that affect owner economics.
- International operations remained adjusted-EBITDA negative.
- Manufacturing demand is cyclical and sensitive to customer budgets.
- Buyer and supplier multi-homing can weaken marketplace network effects.
- Poor supplier quality or late delivery can damage buyer trust and increase support costs.

## Invalidation conditions

Invalidate the constructive operating thesis if marketplace growth trails consolidated growth for two consecutive quarters, active-buyer or larger-account growth stagnates materially, marketplace gross margin continues to contract, operating cash flow turns persistently negative, or dilution exceeds the modeled share counts. Revisit the base valuation if 2027 revenue expectations fall materially below USD1.10 billion, a 4.5x revenue multiple is no longer defensible, or net cash is consumed without corresponding durable growth or margin improvement.
