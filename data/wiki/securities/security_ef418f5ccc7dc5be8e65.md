# BASF SE registered shares — research

## Research update

This is the first research page for the security. Coverage now includes identity reconciliation, business economics, first-half 2026 performance, cash conversion, leverage, buybacks, the planned Agricultural Solutions IPO, exploratory Evonik takeover talks, and a 12-month bear/base/bull valuation.

## Identity reconciliation

The issuer identifies the instrument as registered BASF shares, reports Xetra trading in euros, and identifies `BAS` as the Frankfurt ticker. This supports the supplied BAS/XETR/EUR identity. `BAS.DE` is a market-data-provider symbol rather than an issuer identifier; no conflict was found, and the immutable security and issuer IDs are unchanged. The issuer page listed 892,522,164 registered shares as of its October 8 update, while first-half weighted-average shares were lower because of repurchases. That difference is treated as a valuation limitation rather than an identity change. [Share and ADRs](https://www.basf.com/global/en/investors/share-and-adrs) (`source_web_499e986d35a0b64389e2927f`).

## Business economics

BASF combines cyclical upstream and intermediate chemical activities with differentiated materials, industrial solutions, nutrition and care products, surface technologies, and agricultural solutions. Earnings therefore depend on plant utilization, feedstock and energy costs, chemical spreads, industrial demand, currency, and the ability to price products above variable costs. Its Verbund model can provide integration and utilization benefits, but the fixed-cost base makes cash generation sensitive to volume and working-capital swings. The 2025 baseline was EUR 6.6 billion of EBITDA before special items and EUR 1.3 billion of free cash flow. [BASF Report 2025](https://report.basf.com/2025/en/) (`source_web_62afdf42ab98da00c11635b9`).

## Current evidence

First-half 2026 sales increased to EUR 33.2 billion and EBITDA before special items increased to EUR 4.8 billion. Volumes rose 5.7% and prices rose 4.8%, while currency was a headwind. Management raised 2026 EBITDA-before-special-items guidance to EUR 6.9 billion-EUR 7.7 billion but retained free-cash-flow guidance of EUR 1.5 billion-EUR 2.3 billion. [Half-year release](https://www.basf.com/global/en/media/news-releases/2026/07/p-26-123) (`source_web_9bcbcc77301d7a471def22e6`) and [key financial data](https://www.basf.com/global/en/investors/basf-at-a-glance/key-financial-data) (`source_web_574d7b817f078a2b4dbf2f48`).

The quality of the recovery is mixed. Contribution-margin improvements in Materials, Chemicals, and Industrial Solutions supported earnings, and BASF reported restructuring progress and higher utilization. However, first-half operating cash flow was negative EUR 273 million and free cash flow was negative EUR 1.6 billion because cash was tied up in working capital. Reported net income also included a nonrecurring EUR 3.5 billion after-tax gain from selling the Coatings business. Net debt was EUR 17.1 billion at June 30.

BASF announced a further buyback of up to EUR 1.0 billion through April 2027, within its longer-term EUR 4 billion program, and said it intended to reduce net debt. The company reported that EUR 1.5 billion of shares had already been repurchased between November 2025 and June 2026. [Buyback release](https://www.basf.com/global/en/media/news-releases/2026/07/p-26-142) (`source_web_d55c87b510c2c2d5a0718016`).

Agricultural Solutions is being prepared for potential IPO readiness by mid-2027. Approximately 80% of the business had moved into separate legal entities, but BASF explicitly stated that no decision on execution or timing had been made. The unit generated EUR 9.6 billion of 2025 sales, but no issuer-supported IPO valuation is available, so no speculative sum-of-the-parts uplift is included. [Agricultural Solutions release](https://www.basf.com/global/en/media/news-releases/2026/09/p-26-155) (`source_web_2fc0713d4f6bcc994fc6703e`).

Material contrary evidence is the September confirmation that BASF was in exploratory talks concerning a potential takeover of Evonik. BASF said both course and outcome remained open. With no price, financing, synergy, remedy, or probability information, the potential transaction cannot responsibly be embedded in a numeric base case. [Evonik talks release](https://www.basf.com/global/en/media/news-releases/2026/09/p-26-172) (`source_web_cd9c32115ea1ef21b6422c16`).

## Valuation method

A 12-month EV/EBITDA approach is used because BASF is capital intensive and cyclical, and 2026 net income is distorted by the Coatings disposal gain. Each scenario applies an assumed multiple to normalized EBITDA, subtracts scenario net debt, and divides by approximately 0.87 billion economic shares. The denominator approximates the first-half weighted average after repurchases; uncertainty against the 892.5 million registered-share figure is explicitly recognized.

| Scenario | EBITDA | EV/EBITDA | Net debt | Approximate arithmetic | Fair value | Probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Bear | EUR 6.5bn | 6.5x | EUR 18bn | (6.5 × 6.5 − 18) / 0.87 | **EUR 27.9** | 25% |
| Base | EUR 7.4bn | 8.0x | EUR 15bn | (7.4 × 8.0 − 15) / 0.87 | **EUR 50.8** | 50% |
| Bull | EUR 8.3bn | 9.0x | EUR 13bn | (8.3 × 9.0 − 13) / 0.87 | **EUR 70.9** | 25% |

The base EBITDA is close to the midpoint of current company guidance. Multiples are analytical assumptions, not issuer guidance or freshly verified peer multiples. The supplied October 16, 2026 close was EUR 53.23, modestly above the base fair value, suggesting that the quotation already recognizes much of the operating recovery.

## Scenario drivers and probability rationale

- **Bear — 25%:** chemical pricing, spreads, or utilization weaken; working-capital pressure persists; the Agricultural Solutions IPO is delayed; net debt rises to EUR 18 billion; and M&A uncertainty produces a lower 6.5x multiple.
- **Base — 50%:** EBITDA holds near the guidance midpoint, cost savings offset softer macro conditions, cash conversion improves enough to reduce net debt to EUR 15 billion, and no unmodeled Evonik transaction occurs. This receives the highest probability because it is closest to disclosed guidance and observed first-half performance.
- **Bull — 25%:** restructuring, contribution margins, and Zhanjiang utilization lift EBITDA to EUR 8.3 billion; debt falls to EUR 13 billion; Agricultural Solutions separation reveals value; and Evonik talks end or yield demonstrably accretive terms. Equal tail probabilities recognize unusually high two-sided portfolio uncertainty.

## Downside

The strongest warning is the gap between first-half EBITDA improvement and negative free cash flow. Further risks include weaker global industrial production, adverse energy or raw-material prices, currency pressure, Zhanjiang ramp costs, restructuring and ERP charges, delayed Agricultural Solutions separation, and a leveraged or value-destructive Evonik transaction. The bear value represents substantial downside from the supplied market quotation.

## Catalysts

Catalysts include delivery within or above raised EBITDA guidance; a second-half working-capital release that supports full-year free-cash-flow guidance; visible debt reduction; execution of the buyback; more detailed Agricultural Solutions standalone economics; further Ludwigshafen savings and Zhanjiang utilization; and resolution of the Evonik talks on terms that preserve value.

## Invalidation conditions

Reassess if BASF withdraws or materially reduces its 2026 EBITDA or free-cash-flow guidance, net debt fails to decline despite disposal proceeds, normalized EBITDA falls below EUR 6.5 billion without a recovery path, the share denominator is materially different from the approximation, Agricultural Solutions separation is abandoned, or BASF announces binding Evonik terms that materially change leverage, share count, or business mix.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: medium.** Operating, balance-sheet, portfolio, and capital-return evidence comes directly from BASF and is sufficiently detailed for a scenario valuation. Confidence is capped because the last detailed results cover only the first half ended June 30, 2026; third-quarter results were not available in the opened sources; cash conversion remains unproven; no current peer-multiple study was verified; the current economic share count is approximate; and the potential Evonik transaction is unquantifiable.
