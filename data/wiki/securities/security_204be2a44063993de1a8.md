# Microsoft Corporation common stock

## Research conclusion

Microsoft retains unusually strong operating momentum, but the current valuation requires continued AI monetization and disciplined conversion of infrastructure spending into per-share earnings. Fiscal 2026 revenue increased 18% to USD 331.839 billion, operating income increased 21% to USD 155.237 billion, and adjusted diluted EPS increased 22% to USD 17.28. Azure grew 41% for the year and 43% in fiscal Q4, while commercial remaining performance obligation reached USD 678 billion. Management guided to approximately 45% constant-currency Azure growth for fiscal Q1.

The 12-month valuation remains **USD 418 bear, USD 567 base, and USD 698 bull**. The probability-weighted value is **USD 562.50 per share**. Against Microsoft's verified **USD 524.51 Nasdaq close on 23 October 2026**, the deterministic valuation score is **57.2**, indicating moderate rather than compelling valuation support. Confidence is medium because current demand is strong while the return on the infrastructure buildout remains uncertain.

## Business economics

Microsoft combines recurring productivity subscriptions, cloud infrastructure and platform consumption, enterprise applications, security, developer tools, advertising, Windows, devices, and gaming. Its principal economic advantage is distribution: established enterprise relationships allow the company to sell infrastructure, models, developer services, applications, and security through a common commercial channel.

Fiscal 2026 revenue was USD 331.839 billion, operating income was USD 155.237 billion, and operating cash flow was USD 182.935 billion. Adjusted diluted EPS excluding the reported OpenAI investment effect was USD 17.28, compared with USD 14.13 in fiscal 2025. Microsoft Cloud revenue reached USD 214.4 billion for the year, while Azure exceeded USD 100 billion and grew 41%. In fiscal Q4, Azure growth accelerated to 43%, Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 27% to USD 59.3 billion, and commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to USD 678 billion.

The economics are not uniformly improving. Microsoft Cloud gross-margin percentage declined to 66% for fiscal 2026 as AI infrastructure investment and usage increased. More Personal Computing revenue fell 1% for the year, while fiscal Q4 Windows OEM and Devices revenue declined 7% and XBOX content and services revenue declined 10%. Cloud and productivity therefore carry most of the current growth thesis.

## Fiscal 2027 outlook

Management expects another fiscal year of double-digit revenue and operating-income growth, mid- to high-single-digit operating-expense growth, and a full-year operating-margin decline of less than one percentage point. Fiscal Q1 guidance included:

- Total revenue of USD 89.85–90.95 billion, representing 16%–17% growth.
- Intelligent Cloud revenue of USD 40.95–41.25 billion, representing 33%–34% growth.
- Approximately 45% constant-currency Azure growth.
- Productivity and Business Processes revenue of USD 36.7–37.0 billion.
- Relatively stable sequential Microsoft Cloud gross-margin percentage.
- Capital expenditures above USD 50 billion.

Fiscal 2027 first-quarter actual results were not available as of this assessment, making them the nearest major test of the guidance.

## Capital intensity and balance sheet

The annual report strengthens both sides of the capital-allocation assessment. Microsoft reported USD 76.8 billion of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments and USD 36.3 billion of equity and other investments at June 30, 2026. It generated USD 182.9 billion of operating cash flow during the year and stated that existing liquidity, operating cash flow, and capital-market access should be sufficient to fund operations and commitments.

Those commitments are nevertheless substantial. At June 30, 2026, Microsoft reported:

- USD 46.136 billion of long-term debt principal payments.
- USD 34.566 billion of construction commitments.
- USD 443.506 billion of operating and finance lease payments, including imputed interest.
- USD 194.060 billion of purchase commitments, primarily related to datacenters.

Management expects approximately USD 175 billion of calendar 2026 capital expenditures after extending the expected useful life of certain assets and shifting more future datacenter leases from finance to operating classification. This reduces comparability because operating leases remain economic commitments even when excluded from reported capital expenditures.

## Thesis and assumptions

The constructive thesis rests on Microsoft's enterprise distribution, recurring Microsoft 365 revenue, Azure scale, developer ecosystem, security portfolio, and ability to offer multiple models through a common cloud platform. More than 30 million paid Microsoft 365 Copilot seats, growing usage-based products, and a large commercial obligation base provide several monetization routes.

The base valuation assumes normalized fiscal 2027 EPS of USD 21.00, continued double-digit Azure and Microsoft 365 growth, profitable conversion of commercial obligations, contained dilution, and no structural collapse in cloud margins. It does not assume that current Azure growth continues indefinitely. Remaining performance obligations are demand evidence rather than proof of recognition timing, margin, or cash conversion.

## Downside and invalidation

Material risks include overbuilding AI capacity, lower model or cloud pricing, depreciation and power costs rising faster than revenue, contractual obligations reducing flexibility, semiconductor or power shortages, cybersecurity incidents, regulation, tax disputes, and stronger competition from other hyperscalers and model providers.

The constructive case would be materially weakened if:

- Azure growth falls well below near-term guidance without a corresponding reduction in investment.
- Microsoft Cloud margins compress structurally rather than temporarily.
- AI infrastructure remains underutilized or commercial obligations convert slowly.
- Per-share free-cash-flow conversion deteriorates over a full investment cycle.
- Normalized forward EPS cannot approach USD 21.00.
- Security, regulatory, tax, partnership, or capital-allocation failures impair per-share economics.

## Catalysts

Relevant catalysts are delivery against the approximately 45% fiscal Q1 Azure-growth outlook, profitable conversion of the USD 678 billion commercial obligation base, acceleration in Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud from Copilot and usage billing, stable cloud gross margin, improved AI throughput, and evidence that custom silicon or software optimization lowers serving costs.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **medium**. The fiscal 2026 release, annual report, and earnings-call transcript provide current primary evidence for operating performance, liquidity, commitments, and guidance. Fiscal 2027 first-quarter actual results were not yet available. Scenario EPS, multiples, values, and probabilities are research assumptions rather than issuer forecasts and remain highly sensitive to AI utilization, depreciation, margins, and durable growth.
