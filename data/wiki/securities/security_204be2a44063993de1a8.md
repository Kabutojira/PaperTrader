revenue grew 27%, Azure and other cloud services grew 41%, operating income grew 21%, and
commercial remaining performance obligation rose 84% to USD 678 billion. The July 30 price move is

## Business and financial evidence

Fiscal 2026 revenue rose 18% to USD 331.839 billion and operating income rose 21% to USD 155.237
billion. Microsoft Cloud revenue reached USD 214.4 billion, Azure and other cloud services grew
41%, Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud grew 17%, and commercial remaining performance obligation
rose 84% to USD 678 billion. These figures support durable enterprise distribution, broad product
bundling, and an unusually large contracted-demand base for cloud and AI services.

GAAP net income was USD 133.749 billion and diluted EPS was USD 17.95. Because OpenAI investment
marks affected comparability, the issuer also reported adjusted diluted EPS of USD 17.28 versus
USD 14.13 in fiscal 2025. Operating cash flow was USD 182.935 billion. Additions to property and
equipment nearly doubled to USD 115.948 billion, leaving USD 66.987 billion after those additions
before acquisitions and other investing uses. The investment burden is affordable but materially
raises the required future revenue and utilization conversion.

At June 30, cash and short-term investments were USD 76.843 billion, while current and long-term
debt totaled USD 40.294 billion, a USD 36.549 billion net liquid cushion before lease and purchase
obligations. That balance-sheet capacity is strong, but the filing also reports USD 34.566 billion
of construction commitments, USD 194.060 billion of purchase commitments, and very large lease
obligations, mostly associated with datacenters. Balance-sheet strength does not eliminate the risk
of weak returns on accelerated AI infrastructure spending.

## Thesis, contrary evidence, catalysts, and invalidation

The constructive thesis is that Microsoft's installed enterprise base, recurring subscriptions,
developer ecosystem, cloud scale, security portfolio, and distribution allow it to monetize AI
across Azure, Microsoft 365, GitHub, and industry applications. Fiscal 2026 growth in Cloud, Azure,
Microsoft 365, adjusted EPS, cash flow, and contracted obligations provides strong current support.

Contrary evidence is the scale and speed of datacenter investment before all associated revenue is
proven. Competition from other hyperscalers and model providers can pressure price and economics;
capacity, power, chips, cybersecurity, regulation, and customer concentration can constrain growth;
and OpenAI investment marks add noise to GAAP earnings. Windows OEM and Devices and Xbox content
and services declined in fiscal Q4, showing that not every portfolio component shares cloud's
momentum.

Catalysts are sustained Azure growth, Microsoft 365 Copilot seat and revenue conversion, contracted
backlog conversion, stable cloud margins despite infrastructure depreciation, and operating cash
flow growing faster than capital commitments. Invalidate or materially downgrade the constructive
case if Azure growth decelerates without moderating investment, AI capacity remains underutilized,
cloud margins compress structurally, free-cash-flow conversion deteriorates, contracted obligations
fail to convert, or material security, regulatory, partnership, or capital-allocation failures
impair per-share economics.
