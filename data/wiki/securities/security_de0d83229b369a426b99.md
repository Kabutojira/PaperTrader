## Identity, evidence and thesis

The immutable instrument is the 1:1 TCEHY unsponsored USD ADR at XOTC. Tencent reported 11% group
revenue growth, 13% gross-profit growth and 9% non-IFRS operating-profit growth. Games revenue was
about 32% of group revenue; marketing grew 22% and fintech/business services 9%. Contrary evidence
is RMB52.8 billion quarterly capex, RMB13.8 billion negative free cash flow and lower net cash as AI
procurement accelerated. Listed investees were RMB487.2 billion, but realizability and private-value
economics require haircuts. Catalysts are sustained games/ads growth, AI monetization, restored cash
conversion and buybacks. Invalidate on regulatory impairment, engagement weakness, poor compute
returns, declining net cash or unreliable ADR transmission.
