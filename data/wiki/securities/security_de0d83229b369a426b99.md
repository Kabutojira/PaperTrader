# Tencent Holdings Limited unsponsored ADR

## Research conclusion

Tencent's operating evidence remains constructive, but a current USD valuation for TCEHY is **not supportable** from the opened sources. Tencent reports results and earnings per Hong Kong ordinary share in RMB, while the evidence reviewed here does not verify the conversion ratio between those ordinary shares and one TCEHY unsponsored ADR. The prior USD40/USD65/USD90 scenarios are therefore withdrawn rather than carried forward without verification.

## Identity and evidence boundary

The immutable tracked security is `security_de0d83229b369a426b99`, identified as the TCEHY unsponsored USD ADR quoted on XOTC. The issuer remains `issuer_b1a9356dc6568f4c7f55`. ## Business economics and current evidence

For 2Q2026, Tencent reported RMB204.8 billion of revenue, up 11% year over year; RMB118.4 billion of gross profit, up 13%; and RMB67.3 billion of IFRS operating profit, up 12%. Domestic-games revenue increased 17%, marketing-services revenue 22%, and fintech-and-business-services revenue 9%. Combined Weixin and WeChat monthly active users reached 1.439 billion. This breadth supports the view that Tencent can monetize engagement through games, advertising, payments and cloud rather than relying on a single product.

AI is both an opportunity and the principal current counterpoint. Tencent attributed advertising improvements and greater cloud demand partly to AI, but capital expenditure rose 176% year over year to RMB52.8 billion. Quarterly free cash flow was negative RMB13.8 billion because operating cash flow was outweighed by capital spending, compute prepayments, content payments and lease payments. Management stated that free cash flow excluding compute-procurement prepayments would have been RMB37.6 billion, but that adjusted observation does not establish the eventual return on the spending.

## Balance sheet

At 30 June 2026, Tencent reported RMB511.2 billion of total cash and RMB58.2 billion of net cash. Quarterly adjusted EBITDA was RMB91.4 billion, compared with RMB3.2 billion of interest and related expenses. Liquidity therefore appears resilient, but net cash was down 22% year over year and the AI investment cycle has increased cash demands. Listed investee holdings had a stated fair value of RMB487.2 billion; their market value can fluctuate and should not be treated as immediately realizable cash at full value.

## Valuation status

An earnings-multiple method remains conceptually suitable for a mature platform company with meaningful investee assets and repurchases. A defensible calculation would require:

1. normalized RMB earnings attributable to ordinary shareholders;
2. a justified bear/base/bull earnings range and multiple;
3. the verified number of Tencent ordinary shares represented by each TCEHY ADR; and
4. an explicit RMB/USD conversion convention appropriate to the valuation date.

Items 3 and 4 were not established by the opened evidence. Accordingly, bear, base and bull values and probabilities are left empty. The supplied USD49.41999816894531 close for TCEHY on the 2026-10-23 session is not used for a valuation score because there is no supported USD per-ADR fair value against which to compare it.

## Fundamental rubric v1

- **Business quality: 78/100.** Broad-based growth, high ecosystem reach and profitable operations place Tencent modestly above the durable-advantages anchor. Heavy AI investment and weak current cash conversion prevent an exceptional score.
- **Thesis support: 68/100.** Games, advertising, fintech and cloud growth corroborate the ecosystem thesis, while the size and uncertain return of compute investment provide material contrary evidence.
- **Balance sheet: 72/100.** Cash resources, positive net cash and substantial EBITDA indicate resilience, tempered by declining net cash and the current spending cycle.
- **Risk penalty: 63/100.** Material-to-high residual risk reflects cyclicality, platform and regulatory exposure, AI-return uncertainty, investee volatility and incomplete ADR evidence. Leverage is assessed in the balance-sheet factor and is not deducted again here.
- **Valuation score: unsupported.** The ADR entitlement and USD valuation bridge are unverified.

These are research judgments, not probabilities of investment profit.

## Downside, catalysts and invalidation

Downside centers on persistent infrastructure spending without adequate AI revenue, weaker game or advertising demand, regulatory impairment, investee-value volatility and continued erosion of net cash. Potential catalysts include sustained games and advertising growth, monetization of new AI products, stronger cloud demand, recovery in free cash flow and continued repurchases.

Invalidate the operating thesis if Weixin engagement or major games weaken persistently, advertising monetization deteriorates, regulation materially impairs core platforms, AI spending fails to produce revenue and cash returns, or the net-cash position erodes materially. Reject any future ADR valuation unless the depositary ratio, quote units and share-class entitlement are verified.

## Idea exposure

Tencent remains a positive beneficiary of the digital-attention, gaming and consumer-ecosystems idea. Games, Weixin, advertising, payments and cloud provide a specific monetization pathway, but the pathway depends on incremental AI revenue and operating benefit exceeding the added capital and operating costs.
