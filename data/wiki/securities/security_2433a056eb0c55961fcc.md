## Business economics

Amazon combines businesses with distinct but complementary economics:

- **AWS** supplies cloud compute, storage, databases, networking, model services, and custom chips. Q2 sales were USD 42.232 billion and operating income was USD 16.621 billion.
- **North America** generated USD 116.177 billion of sales and USD 9.123 billion of operating income. Delivery density, marketplace fees, subscriptions, and advertising can improve the economics of the consumer network.
- **International** generated USD 42.197 billion of sales and USD 1.717 billion of operating income. It provides additional scale potential but remains exposed to currency, regulation, trade policy, and uneven local profitability.
- **Advertising services** grew 26% to USD 19.809 billion. Advertising monetizes customer purchase intent and seller demand without the inventory burden of first-party retail.

The reinforcing mechanism is that customer traffic attracts sellers and advertisers, fulfillment density improves convenience, Prime encourages engagement, and AWS monetizes enterprise computing demand. The central economic tension is that AI and fulfillment infrastructure require large expenditures before utilization, pricing, and per-share cash returns are established.

## Valuation method and scenarios

A normalized forward earnings multiple is used because reported earnings contain an unusually large investment gain. Scenario EPS represents estimated recurring earnings after operating costs, depreciation, recurring financing costs, taxes, stock compensation, and dilution. The model uses approximately 11 billion diluted shares or share equivalents.

- **Bear — 25% probability: USD 200 per share.** USD 8.00 normalized forward EPS multiplied by 25x. AWS slows, retail margins weaken, infrastructure utilization disappoints, and depreciation, energy, and financing costs catch up with investment while free cash flow remains negative.
- **Base — 50% probability: USD 270 per share.** USD 9.00 normalized forward EPS multiplied by 30x. AWS and advertising remain strong, retail margins broadly hold, and operating growth absorbs higher infrastructure costs, but capital intensity prevents material multiple expansion.
- **Bull — 25% probability: USD 360 per share.** USD 10.00 normalized forward EPS multiplied by 36x. AWS growth and margins remain unusually strong, AI and custom-chip capacity achieves high utilization, and free cash flow improves as revenue catches up with investment.

The probabilities total 100% and produce a probability-weighted value of USD 275. The EPS figures, multiples, and probabilities are analytical assumptions rather than company guidance.

## Assumptions and contrary evidence

The base case requires continued AWS and advertising growth, stable retail operating leverage, manageable dilution, and enough infrastructure utilization to offset higher depreciation, financing, semiconductor, and energy costs. It excludes the Anthropic remeasurement from recurring earnings.

Material contrary evidence includes:

- Negative trailing free cash flow despite strong operating cash flow.
- A large increase in property spending associated primarily with AI infrastructure.
- Higher debt and lease obligations.
- Dependence on AWS for approximately 61% of Q2 segment operating income.
- Semiconductor, power, and infrastructure availability constraints.
- Intense competition across cloud, retail, advertising, devices, content, logistics, and AI services.
- Regulatory, tariff, labor, cybersecurity, inventory, international, and supplier risks.
- The possibility that AI products or capacity fail to earn adequate returns on investment.

## Catalysts

Potential catalysts include sustained AWS growth and margins, rising utilization of AI infrastructure, successful Trainium and Bedrock monetization, continued advertising growth, retail and fulfillment productivity, stronger International profitability, and a return to positive free cash flow without sacrificing durable revenue growth.

## Invalidation conditions

The thesis would be materially weakened if AWS growth or margins deteriorate while infrastructure spending remains high; AI capacity remains underutilized; normalized earnings fail to absorb depreciation, financing costs, taxes, and dilution; retail margins reverse; or free cash flow remains negative without a credible recovery path. Material regulatory, security, labor, semiconductor-supply, energy, or capital-allocation failures would also invalidate important assumptions. Reported net income should not be treated as confirming the thesis while it is dominated by non-operating investment gains.
