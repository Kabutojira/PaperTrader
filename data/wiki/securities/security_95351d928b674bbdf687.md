## Business economics and thesis

Ginkgo sells biological R&D services, laboratory automation and related tools to government and commercial customers. Its autonomous laboratory uses reconfigurable automation systems to perform wet-lab workflows and generate biological data. The economic opportunity is to replace manual experimental work with reusable infrastructure, improving laboratory throughput and allowing Ginkgo to earn service, data and equipment revenue across multiple programs.

The contrary evidence remains substantial. The [Q2 2026 results release](https://cdn.yahoofinance.com/prod/sec-filings/0001830214/000162828026053319/ex991earningspr.htm) (`source_web_c4808607c097ee11773cbc85`) reported revenue of approximately USD 20 million, down 48% year over year, a USD 57 million continuing-operations net loss and negative USD 36 million adjusted EBITDA. Management attributed much of the revenue decline to program rationalization during restructuring. Those results do not yet demonstrate durable product-market fit.

Management reported autonomous-lab awards involving MIT, Caltech, Northwestern, the University of Maryland and a U.S. national laboratory, more than 100 robots at Nebula, and 17 initial ADME-One customers. These are relevant adoption indicators, but disclosed awards and customer counts are not equivalent to recurring revenue, attractive margins or positive cash flow.

## Valuation

A 12-month revenue-multiple method is used because current earnings and free cash flow are negative. Per-share value is calculated as:

`(forward revenue × enterprise-value/revenue multiple + residual net financial resources) ÷ diluted shares`

Residual net financial resources are scenario estimates after forecast cash use, restricted cash and other financial claims. They are not the reported cash balance.

| Scenario | Probability | Per-share value | Arithmetic and operating assumptions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| Base | 50% | USD 7.57 | USD 100 million revenue × 3.0x + USD 230 million residual resources = USD 530 million; divided by 70 million diluted shares. Autonomous-lab and tool activity stabilizes revenue, but losses and dilution remain material. |
| Bull | 20% | USD 16.47 | USD 140 million revenue × 6.0x + USD 280 million residual resources = USD 1.12 billion; divided by 68 million diluted shares. Utilization and repeat adoption accelerate, losses fall sharply and dilution slows. |

The probabilities total 100%. The base case receives the largest weight because restructuring and new offerings could stabilize the business, but the observed revenue contraction and cash use justify a larger bear weight than bull weight. The supplied October 20 market snapshot of USD 9.87 exceeds the USD 7.57 base value. The share-price increase from the prior USD 7.71 reference does not itself improve revenue, cash flow or dilution evidence.

## Downside and contrary case

The principal risk is that autonomous-lab awards and early customers do not convert into repeatable, high-margin revenue. Continued operating losses could reduce residual financial resources faster than modeled. Restricted cash, lease obligations, stock compensation and additional at-the-market issuance can also make headline liquidity overstate per-share downside protection.

The USD 3.33 bear value assumes both weaker revenue and more shares. Even if Ginkgo avoids near-term liquidity distress, financing the transition through equity could transfer enterprise progress away from existing shares. Government awards also introduce funding, milestone and execution risks, while restructuring can reduce expenses at the cost of delivery capacity.

## Catalysts and invalidation

Potential positive catalysts are:

- recurring revenue and retention disclosures for Nebula, Datapoints, Solutions or ADME-One;
- conversion of autonomous-lab awards into recognized revenue and cash receipts;
- higher utilization of Nebula's installed automation;
- sustained reductions in operating losses and cash use; and
- reduced reliance on share issuance.

Invalidate the constructive thesis if continuing revenue keeps contracting without a credible transition path, awarded programs fail to become economically material, customer activity remains episodic, cash use exceeds management's range, or dilution offsets operating improvement. Sustained recurring growth and progress toward cash breakeven without material issuance would weaken the bear case and justify revisiting the multiple and probability weights.

## Confidence and limitations

Confidence is **medium**. The financial statements and issuer release support the reported revenue, losses, liquidity, restricted cash, cash use and issuance figures. However, the evidence only extends through June 30, 2026, and the opened source set contains no later quarterly report. Management does not disclose enough contract-level backlog, revenue, utilization, retention or margin information to validate the adoption narrative quantitatively. Forward revenue, valuation multiples, residual resources and diluted share counts therefore remain assumptions with wide error ranges.

## Research disposition

Research status: **supported** through **2026-11-19**. Ginkgo has sufficient primary financial evidence for a scenario valuation, but the current evidence does not establish profitable or self-funding economics. The autonomous-laboratory transition merits continued fundamental coverage; its success should be judged by recurring revenue, cash conversion and dilution rather than announcements or share-price movement.
