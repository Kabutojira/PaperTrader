# Nomad Foods Limited ordinary shares

## Research conclusion

Nomad Foods has valuable European frozen-food brands and demonstrated Q2 gross-margin improvement, but its defensive characteristics are being tested by pronounced volume contraction and weaker earnings. The current research range is **USD 9.54 / USD 15.08 / USD 19.36** for bear/base/bull scenarios, with probabilities of **30% / 50% / 20%** and a probability-weighted value of approximately **USD 14.27**.

This is a scenario valuation rather than confirmation that a turnaround has occurred. The issuer retained its 2026 organic-revenue and adjusted-EBITDA guidance but reduced adjusted-EPS guidance because refinancing and variable-rate interest expense increased ([Q2 2026 results](https://www.nomadfoods.com/news/nomad-foods-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results/) — `source_nomd_q2_2026_results`).

## Identity reconciliation

The Q2 issuer release identifies **Nomad Foods Limited** and **NYSE: NOMD**, consistent with the supplied issuer and provider symbol. The company reports in euros but translated 2026 adjusted-EPS guidance to **USD 1.59-1.76**, which is used for this USD per-share valuation. The accessible release did not independently establish the full ordinary-share legal description or the supplied `XNYS` MIC; those identifiers are retained unchanged, with that evidence limitation disclosed.

## Business economics and contrary evidence

Nomad owns established European frozen-food brands including Birds Eye, Findus, iglo, Ledo and Frikom. The economic case depends on recurring grocery demand, brand pricing, product innovation and supply-chain scale producing margins and cash flow.

Q2 provided evidence for both sides of the thesis. Pricing and productivity lifted adjusted gross margin by 110 basis points to 28.9%, and management continued to expect adjusted free-cash-flow conversion of at least 90%. Conversely, reported revenue fell 3.1%, organic revenue fell 2.9%, and volume fell 5.9%, only partly offset by 3.0% positive price/mix. Adjusted EBITDA declined 4.3%. For the first half, adjusted EBITDA declined 13.3% and adjusted EPS fell to EUR 0.62. These figures show that pricing and productivity have not yet restored broad earnings growth ([Q2 2026 results](https://www.nomadfoods.com/news/nomad-foods-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results/) — `source_nomd_q2_2026_results`).

## Valuation method and scenarios

An **adjusted-EPS multiple** is used because the current issuer release provides explicit USD adjusted-EPS guidance but only a cash-conversion percentage, not enough absolute cash flow and net-debt detail for a defensible free-cash-flow-yield calculation.

- **Bear — USD 9.54, 30%:** USD 1.59 low-end adjusted EPS multiplied by 6x. Volume contraction persists, pricing loses effectiveness, operating costs remain elevated and financing expense limits recovery.
- **Base — USD 15.08, 50%:** USD 1.675 guidance midpoint multiplied by 9x. Volume contraction moderates, gross-margin improvement holds and at least 90% adjusted free-cash-flow conversion is delivered.
- **Bull — USD 19.36, 20%:** USD 1.76 high-end adjusted EPS multiplied by 11x. Retailer momentum and brand investment restore volume while productivity produces durable margin improvement.

The probabilities favor the base case because management retained revenue and EBITDA guidance, but the bear case receives substantial weight given observed first-half deterioration. The bull case has the lowest probability because renewed volume growth has not yet been demonstrated. The multiples are valuation assumptions, not issuer-reported facts.

## Downside

The key risk is sustained unit contraction. Additional pricing could worsen consumer elasticity or retailer relationships, while supply-chain inflation and higher operating expenses could offset productivity. Refinancing already reduced adjusted-EPS guidance through higher interest expense. Persistent restructuring and other exclusions could also make adjusted EPS a poor representation of recurring shareholder economics.

## Catalysts

- Sequential moderation of volume declines and restoration of organic growth.
- Continued gross-margin gains from pricing and supply-chain productivity.
- Delivery of at least 90% adjusted free-cash-flow conversion.
- Stabilization or growth in adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EPS.
- Evidence that innovation and improved retailer momentum are restoring demand.
- Lower financing pressure and better visibility into leverage reduction.

## Invalidation conditions

Reassess the valuation if adjusted EPS falls below USD 1.59, free-cash-flow conversion misses 90%, volume remains near current contraction rates without compensating margin gains, price/mix turns negative, adjusted EBITDA continues declining at a double-digit rate, or financing expense rises further. A material discrepancy in the supplied share class, MIC or USD trading identity would also invalidate application of the per-share scenarios until reconciled.

## Confidence and limitations

**Confidence: medium.** The operating and guidance evidence comes directly from the issuer, but the valuation relies on non-IFRS adjusted EPS and judgmental multiples. Detailed debt, cash, leases, diluted shares and maturity data were not incorporated, so leverage and per-share dilution were not independently reconciled. Adjusted measures exclude restructuring, acquisition-accounting, share-based-payment and other items that may recur.
