# Quanta Services, Inc. common stock

## Research conclusion

Quanta Services remains a high-quality beneficiary of electric-grid modernization, power generation, large-load interconnections and mission-critical construction. Its second-quarter results supported the operating thesis: revenue, adjusted earnings, cash generation, remaining performance obligations and backlog reached record levels, and management raised full-year guidance. The valuation is now much less extended than at the prior review, but the supplied USD 546.35 closing price remains close to the USD 534.40 base fair value. The evidence therefore supports the company thesis more strongly than it supports a large valuation margin of safety.

The latest reported quarter remains Q2 2026. Quanta's investor-relations page schedules Q3 results for October 29, 2026, so this assessment should be revisited promptly when that release becomes available.

## Business economics

Quanta supplies specialized infrastructure services to utilities, power-generation developers, load-center and technology customers, communications operators, pipeline owners and industrial customers. Its services span design, installation, repair and maintenance. The economic proposition is a scaled, self-perform workforce and integrated capabilities in markets where craft labor, engineering coordination and safe execution can be scarce.

The model benefits when customers commit sustained capital to transmission, substations, distribution hardening, generation, interconnections and mission-critical facilities. Scale and labor depth can improve project certainty and customer relevance. The counterweight is that construction economics remain sensitive to contract structure, labor availability, project timing, cost estimates, collections and execution.

## Current financial evidence

For Q2 2026, Quanta reported USD 9.56 billion of revenue, USD 451.4 million of net income attributable to common stock, USD 2.96 of GAAP diluted EPS and USD 4.24 of adjusted diluted EPS. Quarterly operating cash flow was USD 1.1 billion and free cash flow was USD 0.9 billion. The issuer reported USD 33.6 billion of remaining performance obligations and USD 53.4 billion of total backlog.

For the first half, revenue was USD 17.43 billion and adjusted diluted EPS was USD 6.92. Management raised full-year expectations to revenue of USD 39.3-39.7 billion, adjusted diluted EPS of USD 16.45-16.95 and free cash flow of USD 2.00-2.50 billion. The adjusted-EPS midpoint used in this valuation is therefore USD 16.70.

Quanta also completed or announced the completion of Phalcon, Enerfab, Percheron and PSD during Q2 and July. Management expected these businesses to contribute approximately USD 1.2-1.4 billion of 2026 revenue and USD 120-140 million of adjusted EBITDA. Aggregate upfront consideration was approximately USD 1.24 billion, including about USD 1.07 billion in cash and USD 173.3 million in stock, with potential contingent consideration of up to approximately USD 242.3 million. The cash portion was funded with cash and existing debt arrangements. These acquisitions broaden capabilities but make integration, leverage and return on invested capital important valuation offsets.

## Contrary evidence and risks

Backlog is not equivalent to guaranteed revenue. Its realization can change with project timing, cancellations, permitting, weather, customer constraints and commercial disputes. Remaining performance obligations are firmer than the broader backlog measure but still depend on execution and customer performance.

Material risks include fixed-price cost overruns, skilled-labor shortages and wage pressure, supply-chain disruption, collections and change-order recovery, operational hazards, acquisition integration, contingent payments, goodwill impairment and debt-funded growth. Even if earnings rise, the share price can decline if investors assign a lower premium multiple.

The strongest near-term contrary consideration is information freshness: the financial period ended June 30, while Q3 results are due October 29. A material guidance or margin change would require immediate scenario revision.

## Valuation method and scenarios

A 12-month adjusted-earnings multiple is used because management provides a current full-year adjusted diluted-EPS range and the filing provides enough operating, contract and acquisition context to frame a bounded multiple sensitivity. This is not a precise appraisal or a substitute for a full discounted-cash-flow model.

The arithmetic uses the USD 16.70 midpoint of management's USD 16.45-16.95 adjusted diluted-EPS guidance:

- **Bear — USD 417.50, 25% probability:** USD 16.70 multiplied by 25x. Project timing, margin normalization, integration costs, leverage or fixed-price losses compress the valuation premium.
- **Base — USD 534.40, 50% probability:** USD 16.70 multiplied by 32x. Guidance is broadly achieved, backlog converts profitably, Electric demand remains resilient and cash generation supports acquisition integration.
- **Bull — USD 668.00, 25% probability:** USD 16.70 multiplied by 40x. Grid and mission-critical demand sustain exceptional growth, margins remain strong, acquisitions integrate well and the market maintains a scarcity-quality premium.

The probabilities emphasize management's current guidance through the 50% base weight while assigning equal 25% tail weights to adverse execution and sustained premium growth. The supplied USD 546.35 close sits modestly above the base value and well below the bull value. Market price is used only for context and is not carried into fair-value arithmetic.

## Assumptions, catalysts and invalidation

### Key assumptions

The central case assumes the guidance midpoint remains usable until Q3 results, RPO and backlog convert into profitable revenue, Electric demand and margins remain resilient, free cash flow progresses toward guidance, and acquired businesses contribute without disproportionate integration costs, dilution, impairment or leverage pressure.

### Catalysts

- Q3 results that confirm or raise 2026 guidance.
- Continued Electric margin strength and profitable backlog conversion.
- Free-cash-flow delivery and declining leverage relative to earnings.
- Successful integration of the four recent acquisitions.
- Major utility, generation, interconnection or large-load awards.
- Expanded domestic equipment supply that reduces critical-path constraints.

### Invalidation conditions

Invalidate or materially revise the constructive thesis if RPO or backlog contracts without conversion, Electric margins deteriorate persistently, project losses recur, receivable or change-order collection worsens, free cash flow materially misses guidance, acquisitions generate impairments, leverage rises without commensurate earnings, or customers cancel or defer significant projects. Revise the valuation immediately if Q3 evidence materially changes the USD 16.70 earnings anchor.
