## Economics and thesis

Rio Tinto is a diversified miner whose iron ore, aluminium, copper, and emerging lithium cash flows
make the ADR less sensitive to copper than a pure producer. H1 2026 copper-equivalent output rose 3%.
Oyu Tolgoi production rose 31% and its C1 net unit-cost guidance improved to USD 0.30-0.50/lb, but
group Q2 copper output of 213 kt was 7% below both prior year and Q1. Full-year copper guidance of
800-870 kt was unchanged. Iron ore remains much larger and links the equity to China and steel.

The latest full-year financial evidence is mixed. For 2025, revenue rose 7% to USD 57.638 billion,
underlying EBITDA rose 9% to USD 25.363 billion, and operating cash flow rose 8% to USD 16.832
billion. Free cash flow nevertheless fell 28% to USD 4.025 billion as property, plant, equipment,
and intangible investment rose to USD 12.335 billion. Net debt increased 162% to USD 14.362
billion after the Arcadium acquisition and project investment. The balance sheet still funds the
portfolio, but capital intensity and leverage reduce the margin for commodity or execution errors.

The Oyu Tolgoi underground ramp can increase low-cost copper exposure, while Resolution and Winu
offer longer-dated options. Simandou, Pilbara renewal, lithium integration, national partnerships,
capex, and commodity diversification can either fund copper growth or dilute its thesis purity.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

The 2026-07-27 deterministic ADR mark is USD 91.95. A bounded 12-month earnings scenario applies
10x and 13x to 2025 underlying EPS of USD 6.692, assuming one ADR represents one Rio Tinto plc
ordinary share. This produces USD 66.92 downside value and USD 87.00 base value, or returns of
-27.2% and -5.4%. The range is deliberately conservative for a cyclical miner because 2025 free
cash flow fell, net debt rose, and 2026 half-year financial results are due on 29 July. It is not a
full sum-of-parts and must be refreshed for commodity prices, segment cash flow, capex, net debt,
project ownership, taxes, and any change to the ADR ratio.

Catalysts are Oyu Tolgoi ramp and cost delivery, half-year cash results, and de-risking of Winu,
Resolution, and Simandou. Invalidate if underground ramp slips, copper cost guidance reverses,
iron-ore demand weakens, large projects overrun, or capital allocation prevents higher copper
production from improving per-share cash flow.
