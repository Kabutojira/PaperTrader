## Business economics

Q2 net sales were EUR23.165 billion, increasing 1.9% at constant exchange rates. Comparable sales excluding gasoline grew 1.2%, online sales grew 8.6% at constant rates and underlying operating margin was 3.9%, down 0.1 percentage points. U.S. comparable sales grew only 0.8% as pharmacy-pricing changes, egg-price deflation and lower SNAP benefits weighed on sales. Europe produced 1.7% comparable growth and a 3.9% underlying margin.

For H1 2026, net sales were EUR45.440 billion, underlying operating income was EUR1.803 billion and diluted underlying EPS was EUR1.26. Management retained guidance for an approximately 4% underlying operating margin, mid- to high-single-digit diluted underlying EPS growth at constant exchange rates, at least EUR2.3 billion of free cash flow and approximately EUR2.7 billion of gross cash capital expenditure. Scale, recurring food demand, local brands, own-brand penetration above 40% and online density support durability, but low growth and a thin operating margin constrain pricing mistakes and cost absorption.

## Cash generation and financial position

At June 28, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were EUR3.196 billion and loans were EUR4.861 billion. H1 operating cash flow was EUR2.500 billion, but reported free cash flow was only EUR302 million, down 57.8% year over year. H1 lease repayments were EUR980 million, dividends were EUR644 million and share-buyback cash outflow was EUR579 million. These figures support manageable financing capacity, while also showing why lease obligations, working capital and distributions must be assessed alongside headline earnings.

## Valuation method and scenarios

A normalized earnings-multiple method fits a mature food retailer better than a high-growth model. The method begins with H1 diluted underlying EPS of EUR1.26 and management's retained constant-currency growth and margin guidance, then varies normalized earnings and the multiple for operating resilience and risk:

- **Bear — EUR26, 25%:** approximately EUR2.40 normalized EPS at about 11 times earnings. U.S. volume, regulation, price investment and operating costs keep margins and cash conversion under pressure.
- **Base — EUR34, 50%:** approximately EUR2.60 normalized EPS at about 13 times earnings. Low-single-digit growth, a margin near 4%, online expansion and a lower share count broadly offset regulatory and currency pressure.
- **Bull — EUR42, 25%:** approximately EUR2.75 normalized EPS at about 15.25 times earnings. U.S. headwinds ease, European execution persists and online economics improve without requiring a structurally high-growth outcome.

The probability-weighted value is **EUR34**: 25% of EUR26 plus 50% of EUR34 plus 25% of EUR42. EPS estimates, multiples and probabilities are research assumptions rather than issuer forecasts.

## Thesis, contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis rests on high-frequency grocery demand, regional scale, local customer relevance and omnichannel density sustaining cash generation through ordinary consumer cycles. Q2 constant-currency growth, online expansion and reiterated guidance support that thesis.

Contrary evidence is meaningful. U.S. comparable growth was weak, the Q2 underlying margin declined slightly and first-half free cash flow fell sharply because of working-capital and investment effects. Price competition, labor and utility costs, pharmacy regulation, food deflation, consumer-benefit changes, foreign exchange, supply disruption, food safety and cyber incidents could all impair earnings.

Potential catalysts are improving U.S. volumes, easier pharmacy and SNAP comparisons, sustained European execution, better online profitability, delivery of free-cash-flow guidance and accretive share-count reduction without weaker liquidity.

Invalidate the thesis if volume or market share deteriorates persistently, underlying margin remains materially below approximately 4%, earnings fail to convert into cash, online growth remains structurally uneconomic, or distributions crowd out customer-value investment and financial resilience.
