# 3D Systems Corporation (NYSE: DDD)

## Research summary

This is the first research page for the security. The issuer's current release identifies 3D Systems Corporation as NYSE:DDD, consistent with the supplied issuer and provider-symbol identity. The common-stock share class and USD denomination rely partly on the supplied security record; no IDs were changed.

3D Systems supplies additive-manufacturing systems, materials, software, and related services. Its economics depend on printer demand, the associated installed base and recurring materials/services activity, product mix, and operating leverage over substantial research and commercial costs. Healthcare was the largest reported segment in Q2 2026, while Industrial remained exposed to uneven capital spending.

The emerging recovery is credible but incomplete. Q2 revenue was $94.6 million, down 0.3% as reported but up 1.4% excluding divestitures. Healthcare revenue rose 6.8% to $48.1 million, whereas Industrial revenue declined 6.7% to $46.5 million. Management reported double-digit growth in metal and polymer printer systems and said Med Tech, Dental, Aerospace & Defense, and Data Center Infrastructure each grew more than 20% in the first half. These positives were offset by a lower 36.4% gross margin, a $12.9 million quarterly net loss, and Q3 adjusted EBITDA guidance of negative $3 million to negative $1 million. [Q2 2026 results](https://investor.3dsystems.com/news/news-details/2026/3D-Systems-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

## Thesis

DDD provides leveraged exposure to a recovery in additive-manufacturing capital expenditure and greater adoption of new metal and polymer systems. Prior cost reductions have improved operating performance: adjusted EBITDA improved from a $4.7 million loss in the prior-year quarter to a $0.8 million loss, and first-half adjusted EBITDA was positive $1.3 million. However, current earnings do not yet establish durable profitability. The investment case requires modest revenue growth to translate into better gross margin and sustained positive EBITDA.

Material contrary evidence includes nearly flat reported Q2 revenue, declining Industrial sales, lower gross margin, continuing GAAP losses, negative near-term EBITDA guidance, and dilution. The company issued 18.9 million shares during Q2 for $53.2 million net proceeds. This strengthened liquidity but transferred part of future enterprise value across a larger share base. [Q2 2026 results](https://investor.3dsystems.com/news/news-details/2026/3D-Systems-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

## Valuation method

A forward EV/revenue method is used because negative GAAP earnings and near-term adjusted EBITDA guidance make P/E and normalized free-cash-flow methods unreliable.

The issuer reported $190.1 million of first-half revenue, implying an approximately $380 million annualized starting scale. It also reported $129.0 million of total cash and disclosed $3.9 million of debt due in Q4 2026 plus $92.0 million due in 2030. The model therefore uses approximately $33.1 million of net cash.

The exact current diluted share count could not be verified from a usable filing. A 153 million-share denominator is estimated from the rounded Q2 loss-per-share information and the disclosed 18.9 million-share issuance. This is a model assumption and an important source of valuation uncertainty.

## Scenario drivers

### Bear — $1.79

Revenue falls to $370 million and the multiple contracts to 0.65x as Industrial demand remains weak, gross-margin pressure persists, and adjusted EBITDA stays negative. Continued dilution or cash consumption would create additional downside not fully captured by the static net-cash adjustment.

### Base — $2.83

Revenue reaches $400 million and the company receives a 1.00x multiple. Healthcare and priority industrial markets produce modest growth, while cost actions move adjusted EBITDA toward sustainable break-even. This scenario does not assume a rapid return to strong GAAP profitability.

### Bull — $4.15

Revenue reaches $430 million and the multiple expands to 1.40x. New printer adoption broadens, priority markets continue to outperform, Industrial returns to growth, and gross-margin recovery makes positive EBITDA repeatable.

## Downside and risks

- Industrial capital spending can remain weak or deteriorate.
- Hardware mix, pricing, and tariffs can pressure gross margin.
- New systems may refresh the installed base without producing adequate lifecycle returns.
- Adjusted EBITDA excludes costs that still reduce equity value.
- Cash consumption or further equity issuance could reduce per-share value.
- The valuation is sensitive to an estimated rather than filing-verified share count.

## Catalysts

- Revenue at or above the Q3 outlook of $96 million to $99 million.
- A transition from negative guidance to sustained positive adjusted EBITDA.
- Gross-margin recovery after the Q2 decline.
- Continued double-digit growth in new metal and polymer printer systems.
- Stronger Industrial results and durable growth across the four priority markets.

## Invalidation conditions

The constructive thesis would be invalidated by sustained organic revenue contraction, continued gross-margin erosion, material cash depletion, repeated dilutive financing, or evidence that cost reductions cannot create positive adjusted EBITDA. This valuation should be refreshed if a current filing establishes a materially different share count, net-debt position, or revenue base.
