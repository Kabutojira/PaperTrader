Roblox is a creator-platform exposure within [[ideas/idea_digital_attention_gaming_ecosystems]].

## Economics and thesis

Q2 2026 showed that engagement growth continues but monetization has weakened sharply. Daily active
users grew 10% to 123 million, hours grew 5% to 29 billion, and monthly unique payers grew 15% to 27
million. Revenue rose 36% to USD 1.469 billion, but bookings grew only 8% to USD 1.557 billion, at
the low end of guidance, as lower-monetizing content and discovery-algorithm changes reduced
monetization per hour, particularly among younger users in the United States and Canada.

Cash generation remained positive in Q2: operating cash flow rose 60% to USD 318 million and
company-defined free cash flow rose 66% to USD 294 million. The forward cash-flow premise changed,
however. Management withdrew full-year guidance, guided Q3 bookings down 14–18%, and guided Q3 free
cash flow to negative USD 60 million through positive USD 5 million as monetization softness,
fixed-cost deleveraging, creator-payout timing, capital spending, and AI infrastructure investment
weigh on the next quarter. The filing reports USD 6.084 billion of cash and investments against USD
1.009 billion of long-term debt, but stock compensation and dilution remain material. Management's
fully diluted share measure rose 2% to 752 million even after USD 380 million of Q2 repurchases.

## Market move and interpretation

The deterministic July 31 mark is USD 35.60, down 26.85% in one session and 35.75% from July 1,
with 63.0 million shares traded. It closed below its lower Bollinger band with a 26.64 RSI and a
4.09 volume z-score. The decline followed the July 30 Q2 release and is economically consistent
with the bookings miss, materially negative Q3 bookings outlook, withdrawal of full-year guidance,
and near-zero Q3 free-cash-flow range rather than technical noise alone.

The old USD 25.23 downside and USD 38.80 base values depended on management's prior USD
1.05–1.275 billion full-year free-cash-flow guidance. Because management no longer provides that
annual range and Q3 guidance is materially weaker, the old range is not a valid current buy zone.
The full review classifies the move as a fundamental risk repricing, not technical noise and not a
standalone opportunity. The lower price permits a new bounded valuation, but does not by itself
establish adequate margin of safety or downside payoff.

## Catalysts, risks, and invalidation

Catalysts are stabilization after age checks, sustained payer and older-user growth, creator returns,
advertising and subscriptions, and free-cash-flow growth per diluted share. Invalidate if safety
failures or regulation impair trust, engagement or acquisition weakens, DevEx and infrastructure
costs absorb monetization, hit-driven content concentration rises, or dilution offsets cash growth.
