Roblox is a creator-platform exposure within [[ideas/idea_digital_attention_gaming_ecosystems]].

## Economics and thesis

Q1 2026 daily active users grew 35% to 132 million, hours 43% to 31 billion, monthly unique payers
52% to 31 million, revenue 39% to USD 1.4 billion, and bookings 43% to USD 1.7 billion. Operating
cash flow was USD 629 million and company-defined free cash flow USD 596 million. Content outside
the top ten experiences supplied 65% of Robux-spend growth, supporting a broad creator ecosystem.

Safety and creator economics are not peripheral costs. Age-check rollout reduced communication,
acquisition, and expected 2026 growth; full-year bookings guidance fell to 8–12%. Infrastructure and
trust-and-safety expense rose 47% to USD 197 million, DevEx fees rose 50% to USD 423 million, and
USD 57 million was accrued for youth-safety settlements or proposals. Net loss widened to USD 248
million, while fully diluted shares reached 749 million.

## Valuation, catalysts, and risks

No entry valuation is recorded without a validated current mark. A model must reconcile bookings,
27-month revenue deferral, operating and free cash flow, creator liabilities, safety and settlement
costs, capital expenditure, stock compensation, and fully diluted shares.

Catalysts are stabilization after age checks, sustained payer and older-user growth, creator returns,
advertising and subscriptions, and free-cash-flow growth per diluted share. Invalidate if safety
failures or regulation impair trust, engagement or acquisition weakens, DevEx and infrastructure
costs absorb monetization, hit-driven content concentration rises, or dilution offsets cash growth.
