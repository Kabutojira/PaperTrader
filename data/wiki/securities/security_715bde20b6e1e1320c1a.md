## Business economics

[dLocal's issuer-authored product page](https://www.dlocal.com/our-solution/) (`source_web_1754f688d5e640a606252bbf`) describes a unified integration for receiving and settling payments across Africa, Asia, and Latin America. Its offering includes local-method Payins, cross-border Payouts, fraud management, platform payment services, and invoice collection.

The prospective economic mechanism is straightforward: merchants can outsource local payment-method integration, currency conversion, regulatory handling, payouts, and settlement complexity to one provider. If transaction growth produces durable gross profit and cash conversion, that infrastructure could support scalable economics. The opened page does not disclose current transaction volume, pricing, profitability, retention, customer concentration, cash flow, or per-share outcomes, so durability and financial strength cannot be established.

## Thesis and contrary considerations

A supportable thesis would require evidence that dLocal's integration and local infrastructure reduce merchant complexity in a way that produces durable gross profit and normalized per-share cash flow. Current product descriptions establish the service mechanism but not its financial success.
