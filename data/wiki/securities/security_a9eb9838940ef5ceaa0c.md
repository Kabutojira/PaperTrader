## Business economics

Fluence supplies grid-scale battery storage products and solutions, services, and asset-optimisation
software. Revenue is project-heavy and recognised as systems progress toward completion, so backlog
quality, procurement, construction estimates, commissioning, and milestone cash flows matter more
than headline order intake alone.

For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Fluence reported USD 649.8 million of revenue, a 5.1% GAAP gross
margin, a USD 44.3 million net loss and negative USD 29.3 million adjusted EBITDA. Order intake
exceeded USD 1.44 billion and backlog reached a record USD 6.4 billion. Through July, management
reported about USD 850 million of data-centre business, including USD 550 million of hyperscaler
awards. Demand evidence improved, but production at new contract manufacturers lagged plan and
project delays, liquidated damages, new-platform costs and battery-price revisions impaired margins.

The balance sheet remains adequate but not protective. At 30 June, unrestricted cash was USD 339.3
million and total cash including restricted cash was USD 365.0 million, versus USD 400 million
principal of 2.25% convertible notes due 2030. The first nine months used USD 366.5 million of
operating cash and USD 299.0 million of company-defined free cash flow. Reported USD 863 million of
liquidity includes restricted cash and undrawn working-capital capacity rather than only cash.

Working capital became more intensive: inventory rose to USD 783.0 million from USD 455.0 million
at 30 September 2025, unbilled receivables rose to USD 328.2 million from USD 239.6 million, and
supplier advances rose to USD 226.4 million from USD 126.8 million. Deferred revenue also increased
to USD 956.5 million, providing customer financing but creating delivery obligations. The top three
customers represented 48% of nine-month revenue, up from 40% for the top two a year earlier.

At 30 June, storage products and solutions had 7.4 GW deployed and 12.6 GW of contracted backlog,
up 9% and 38% respectively from September. Services assets under management reached 6.3 GW and
digital assets under management 22.8 GW. These metrics confirm direct exposure, but the filing
explicitly warns that backlog may be delayed, cancelled or converted below historical margins.

## Thesis

Fluence is a direct, positive-sensitivity exposure to storage deployment and grid flexibility.
Record backlog, order intake and data-centre awards make it a plausible long-run beneficiary if
project execution and cash conversion normalise. The equity case weakened materially: gross margin
fell, full-year adjusted EBITDA is now guided around breakeven at the midpoint, and nine-month cash
use shows that bookings have not yet become self-funded growth.

## Valuation

The 5 August 2026 deterministic close is USD 14.23. Earnings and free cash flow remain negative, so
a 12-month revenue-multiple sensitivity is more honest than an earnings target. Using the new
USD 3.0 billion guidance midpoint, 184.596 million Class A and Class B-1 economic interests at
31 July, USD 339.3 million of cash and equivalents, and USD 392.2 million of convertible notes
produces this bounded range:

| Case | Fair value | Probability | Key assumptions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Bear | USD 4.59 | 35% | About 0.3x revenue as delays persist, margins remain near Q3 levels, cash burn and dilution continue, and backlog conversion disappoints. |
| Base | USD 11.09 | 50% | About 0.7x revenue as deferred projects convert in fiscal 2027, but margins and cash conversion recover only gradually. |
| Bull | USD 20.84 | 15% | About 1.3x revenue as manufacturing ramps, data-centre awards convert profitably, gross margin normalises and cash burn ends without material dilution. |

The probability-weighted fair value is about **USD 10.28**, roughly **27.8% below** the current mark;
base value is about **22.1% below** it and the bear case implies about **67.7% downside**. The
scenario set is complete but medium-confidence. It explicitly normalizes growth, margin, cash burn,
dilution and terminal economics; the wide range reflects fragile project economics rather than
false precision.

## Catalysts and confirmation

- production reaches targeted levels early in fiscal 2027 and delayed revenue converts without
  further working-capital build;
- consecutive positive operating-cash-flow quarters and declining contract/inventory intensity;
- backlog conversion with stable or rising normalised gross margin;
- data-centre awards converting into profitable revenue; and
- warranty and project-cost revisions remaining contained.

## Risks and invalidation

The baseline profitability gate has already failed because guidance was cut. Invalidate the remaining
positive case if deferred projects slip again, backlog is cancelled or converted below sustainable
margin, operating cash use remains structurally high, new manufacturing facilities fail to reach
target output, customer or supplier financing tightens, warranty recoveries fail, trade or
domestic-content rules raise project costs, or additional capital is required on unattractive terms.
