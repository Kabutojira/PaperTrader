## Business economics

Fluence supplies grid-scale battery storage products and solutions, services, and asset-optimisation
software. Revenue is project-heavy and recognised as systems progress toward completion, so backlog
quality, procurement, construction estimates, commissioning, and milestone cash flows matter more
than headline order intake alone.

For the quarter ended 31 March 2026, Fluence reported USD 464.9 million of revenue, a 10.0% GAAP
gross margin, an 11.1% adjusted gross margin, a USD 29.2 million net loss, and negative USD 9.4
million adjusted EBITDA. Backlog reached USD 5.6 billion and year-to-date order intake through
6 May reached roughly USD 2.0 billion. Management reaffirmed fiscal-2026 revenue guidance of
USD 3.2-3.6 billion and adjusted EBITDA guidance of USD 40-60 million.

The balance-sheet and cash evidence is less settled. Cash including restricted cash was USD 412.9
million at 31 March, while the first six months used USD 347.9 million of operating cash and
USD 285.4 million of company-defined free cash flow. The company also had USD 400 million principal
of 2.25% convertible notes due 2030 and USD 70.8 million of supplier obligations under a supply-chain
finance facility. Management reported USD 900 million of total liquidity, but that metric includes
restricted cash and undrawn working-capital facilities.

## Thesis

Fluence is a direct, positive-sensitivity exposure to storage deployment and grid flexibility.
The record backlog, growing deployed fleet, hyperscaler master agreements, and improved quarterly
margin make it a plausible survivor if project execution and cash conversion normalise. The equity
case is not confirmed: the fiscal-year EBITDA midpoint is only about 1.5% of the revenue midpoint,
and first-half cash use shows that volume and bookings have not yet become self-funded growth.

## Valuation

No supportable valuation range is recorded. The repository has no fresh deterministic market mark
for this newly researched security, GAAP earnings and free cash flow are negative, and backlog
margin/timing are insufficiently observable. Applying a revenue multiple would manufacture
precision around project economics that have not stabilised. A later valuation should use a fresh
mark, diluted share count, net debt including restricted cash treatment, and scenario-tested
normalised EBITDA and free cash flow.

## Catalysts and confirmation

- fiscal-2026 guidance delivered without a second-half working-capital build;
- consecutive positive operating-cash-flow quarters and declining contract/inventory intensity;
- backlog conversion with stable or rising normalised gross margin;
- hyperscaler agreements converting into profitable orders; and
- warranty and project-cost revisions remaining contained.

## Risks and invalidation

Invalidate the positive case if backlog is delayed, cancelled, or converted below sustainable
margin; full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance is missed; operating cash use remains structurally high;
customer or supplier financing tightens; warranty recoveries fail; trade or domestic-content rules
raise project costs; or additional capital is required on unattractive terms.
