# Digital finance and crypto rails

## Thesis

US stablecoin law, tokenized-securities guidance, and growing regulated platform activity improve
the foundation for continuous settlement, custody, payments, and tokenization. Platforms with
distribution, compliance, liquidity, and trusted custody can diversify beyond spot trading. Their
economics remain exposed to crypto prices and volumes, interest rates, reserve-income sharing,
take-rate compression, cyber loss, regulatory implementation, and stock-based compensation.

Current assessment: **regulatory legitimacy and rail usage are advancing, but normalized platform
earnings remain highly cyclical**.

## Causal mechanism

1. Clear issuer, reserve, redemption, custody, and AML rules reduce legal uncertainty for compliant
   stablecoin and tokenization infrastructure.
2. Distribution platforms earn transaction, custody, subscription, reserve-sharing, financing, and
   software revenue across consumers, institutions, businesses, and agents.
3. Scale can lower acquisition cost and deepen cross-sell, while 24/7 settlement broadens use cases.
4. Shareholder value requires recurring revenue and cash earnings to endure lower token prices,
   volumes, rates, and take rates without security or regulatory failures.

## Value-chain hypotheses

- Exchanges and custodians benefit from compliance and liquidity but remain activity-sensitive.
- Stablecoin distributors earn reserve and payment economics that vary with rates and partner terms.
- Payment processors, brokerages, and banks can embed rails, increasing competition and distribution.
- Treasury vehicles add leveraged token exposure rather than diversified operating-rail economics.

## Confirmation gates

- stablecoin payment and settlement use grows outside speculative trading;
- custody and tokenized assets generate recurring fees with strong controls;
- subscription and services cash earnings remain positive through a crypto drawdown;
- take rates, reserve-sharing terms, and customer acquisition remain defensible; and
- GAAP cash generation supports the capital structure without excessive dilution.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis weakens if adoption remains trading-led, rates fall faster than balances grow, or banks
and payment incumbents capture the economics. It is invalid for a platform after custody or cyber
failure, restrictive implementation, sustained take-rate compression, large credit loss, or a
capital structure that magnifies a token-market drawdown.
