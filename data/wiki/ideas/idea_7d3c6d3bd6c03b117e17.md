# Water reuse, treatment, and monitoring infrastructure

**Unresearched candidate — no current valuation or rating.**

Industrial expansion, AI data centers, semiconductor fabrication, tighter water constraints, and aging municipal systems may drive spending on water reuse, treatment, monitoring, metering, and network optimization. The value chain runs from sensing and analysis through treatment equipment, pumps, controls, and lifecycle services. Xylem identifies growing water demand from data centers, semiconductor manufacturing, and energy systems and presents reuse and network efficiency as offsets; Veralto supplies technologies for managing, treating, purifying, and protecting municipal and industrial water. Uncertainty includes project delays, constrained utility budgets, changing regulation, and customers choosing lower-cost conservation measures. The thesis weakens if industrial water demand is materially decoupled from growth, infrastructure budgets contract, or reuse and monitoring adoption fails to translate into supplier revenue.
