# Terafab and the vertically coordinated AI hardware stack

## Thesis

Terafab is an investable industrial-stack idea only as a staged, hybrid buildout. Current primary
records support a proposed Texas campus, a SpaceX goal of one terawatt of annual compute-hardware
production, logic, memory and advanced-packaging ambitions, shared SpaceX/Tesla engineering, and a
proposed Intel contribution. They do not establish a finished fab, a production process, binding
customer volumes, competitive yield, or Nvidia silicon production.

The base case is therefore that SpaceX, xAI and Tesla create unusually large captive demand while
incumbent suppliers remain essential. [[securities/security_33d9c44facc75c726c7d|NVDA]] can
benefit first through systems, networking, software and orbital-compute products;
[[securities/security_664f93a7eaca72e76e9b|SPCX]] bears the direct capital and execution risk;
[[securities/security_dc7a111e297be528d96b|TSLA]] contributes specialized edge-compute demand;
[[securities/security_dfa34d4b9050964b465e|INTC]] may monetize process and packaging expertise;
and [[securities/security_ef35e41886220d51c22c|ASML]],
[[securities/security_ce9b78a4d0773c950765|TSM]] and
[[securities/security_567d0d575bbd30aaa91d|SSU.VI]] remain enabling or bridge suppliers. Every
edge has now been reviewed: six are accepted with explicit conditions, while Intel is rejected until
its proposed framework becomes binding and financially measurable.

Current assessment: **credible project and demand architecture, medium-confidence economics, very
low confidence in a fast vertically integrated leading-edge ramp**.

## Causal mechanism

1. SpaceX, xAI and Tesla expand demand for terrestrial, vehicle, robotics and orbital compute.
2. Nvidia supplies the general-purpose compute, interconnect, networking and software platform while
   Tesla and SpaceX retain workload-specific ASICs.
3. Terafab first internalizes lower-risk packaging, test, support silicon and specialized products;
   leading-edge compute dies require a much later process, design-kit, yield and qualification gate.
4. Intel can transfer manufacturing and packaging know-how, and ASML can supply incumbent
   lithography, if binding commercial arrangements and tool orders emerge.
5. TSMC, Samsung and other memory and packaging suppliers bridge the ramp and remain structurally
   necessary until internal products prove quality, cost, uptime and scale.
6. Shareholder value appears only if incremental revenue, capacity security and design-cycle gains
   exceed capital intensity, yield losses, governance friction and technology obsolescence.

## NVIDIA candidate disposition

NVIDIA's completed 7 August repeat review consumes the Terafab edge without capitalizing speculative
economics. Space-1, the prospective SpaceXAI adoption of Vera and continued third-party sourcing
support a real product and customer channel, but disclose no order value, volume, investment,
capacity guarantee or wafer-production agreement. The USD 180/USD 252/USD 374 bear/base/bull values
and 25%/50%/25% probabilities therefore remain unchanged.

At the current USD 218.99 mark, probability-weighted return is 20.8%, base return 15.1% and bear
downside 17.8%. Deterministic research is Buy/Initiate, but conviction remains Watch and allocation
is ineligible solely because bear/base payoff is below minimum. The accepted relationship resolves
the old graph blocker without creating a strategy, signal or paper order.

## Scenario map

- **Base — staged hybrid stack:** packaging, test and selected custom silicon come first; Nvidia,
  TSMC, Samsung and other incumbents remain essential; Intel and ASML support a bounded ramp; xLight
  stays in prototype validation.
- **Bull — competitive integrated platform:** multiple fab modules reach competitive yield, Nvidia
  qualifies selected products or capacity, Intel transfers useful process and packaging expertise,
  and later xLight trials improve source economics.
- **Bear — capital-heavy pilot:** frameworks remain non-binding, utilities and tools slip, yields
  disappoint, leading-edge products remain external, and the campus contributes only specialty
  silicon, packaging or test after absorbing substantial capital.

## Catalysts and confirmation gates

- executed site, utility, financing and phase-construction commitments rather than applications;
- binding Intel process, packaging, IP and economic terms;
- disclosed ASML tool orders and a named process-design kit;
- first patterned wafers, defect density, cycle time and product qualification;
- Nvidia package, chiplet, capacity or investment agreement;
- Tesla and SpaceX product migrations with measurable cost, performance and volume;
- durable HBM and advanced-packaging supply through the ramp; and
- an xLight production-scanner trial only after prototype power, dose stability, optics lifetime,
  uptime and redundancy are demonstrated.

## Contrary evidence, risks and invalidation

The idea weakens if filings remain promotional while binding phase commitments do not appear, if
external foundries stay decisively cheaper and more reliable, if Nvidia or Intel withholds required
IP, or if memory and packaging cannot scale with logic. It is invalid as a vertically integrated
leading-edge thesis if competitive process and yield evidence does not emerge, or if repeated cost,
schedule, utility, governance and product-qualification failures confine Terafab to an expensive
pilot. Export controls, water and power constraints, related-party governance, rapid architecture
changes, supply commitments and common-mode failure from centralized infrastructure remain material
throughout the buildout.

## Confidence and review

Confidence is **medium** that a hybrid campus can create supplier demand and strategic option value,
and **low** that Terafab rapidly displaces leading foundries, memory suppliers or Nvidia's merchant
platform. Review by **2026-09-30**, or sooner after a binding phase agreement, named process or tool
order, first-wafer evidence, material financing change, a quantified NVIDIA or SpaceXAI order,
Nvidia qualification, or an xLight scanner trial.
