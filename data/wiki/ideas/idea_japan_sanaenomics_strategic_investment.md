# Japan strategic-investment value chain

## Thesis

Japan's Takaichi government has formally selected 17 strategic fields and is developing multi-year
public-private investment roadmaps spanning supply support, procurement, regulation, talent, and
international expansion. This improves policy visibility for selected semiconductor, AI, defence,
space, power, automation, and advanced-technology suppliers. Equity value still requires funded
programs to become company orders, profitable backlog, and cash flow rather than policy targets.

Current assessment: **the political premise is confirmed, while investable transmission remains
selective and early**. Yen, JGB yields, export controls, labour constraints, and priced-in
expectations can dominate the policy benefit.

## Causal mechanism

1. Multi-year budget commitments, tax incentives, government procurement, and regulatory reform
   reduce uncertainty around strategic capacity investment.
2. Private operators add plants, equipment, R&D, and qualified domestic supply in targeted fields.
3. Suppliers gain order visibility and potentially improve utilization and product mix.
4. Shareholder returns require incremental funded demand, delivery capacity, pricing discipline,
   and valuation support after currency and financing effects.

## Value-chain hypotheses

- Semiconductor equipment, test, materials, and domestic capacity can benefit from sustained AI and
  semiconductor support, subject to export controls and global wafer-fab cycles.
- Defence, shipbuilding, aerospace, and space suppliers can gain procurement visibility, but fixed-
  price execution, labour, and capacity determine margin conversion.
- Power equipment, robotics, and automation can enable domestic investment across fields; policy-
  attributable revenue must be separated from ordinary industrial demand.
- Broad Japan equity exposure adds governance, domestic-demand, yen, and interest-rate factors that
  can dilute the strategic-sector signal.

## Confirmation gates

- final roadmaps specify funding, procurement volumes, milestones, and accountable agencies;
- issuers disclose attributable orders, backlog, capacity, and margin conversion;
- private investment is incremental rather than re-labeled existing capex;
- skilled labour and supply capacity support delivery without cost overruns; and
- yen, JGB yields, export controls, and valuation do not erase earnings upgrades.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis weakens if roadmaps remain aspirational, budgets fragment, or public-private projects do
not become orders. It is invalid as a broad rerating thesis if delivery constraints, fiscal-market
stress, yen appreciation, export restrictions, or already-rich valuations offset incremental
earnings.

## Bounded research candidates

The seed names several Japanese issuers, but the repository does not hold verified immutable
identities for the most direct named semiconductor-equipment, test, heavy-industry, and diversified
industrial candidates. No ticker-only operation, relationship, strategy, signal, or order is
created. A future security review should begin only after identity intake and an attributable
funded order or company disclosure.

## Confidence and review

Confidence is **high** in the stated policy framework and **medium** in implementation, but **low-to-
medium** in broad equity attribution. Review by **2026-10-31**, or sooner after final roadmaps,
budget allocations, or material issuer orders.
