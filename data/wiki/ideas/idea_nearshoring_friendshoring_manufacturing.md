# Nearshoring and friendshoring manufacturing capacity

## Thesis

Trade-policy uncertainty, continuity mandates, and manufacturing incentives can redirect selected
capacity toward Mexico, North America, India, and parts of Southeast Asia. The investable signal is
not an announcement count: it is conversion into operating plants, occupied industrial space,
cross-border freight, supplier utilization, and cash earnings.

Current assessment: **regional capacity investment is observable, but the broad equity thesis is
conditional**. The 2026 USMCA review can reinforce North American integration or make rules of
origin and investment economics less predictable. India has explicit production incentives, while
local power, water, labour, security, permitting, and supplier depth remain binding constraints.

## Causal mechanism

1. Tariffs, economic-security rules, customer continuity requirements, and incentives change the
   risk-adjusted cost of distant production.
2. Manufacturers qualify regional plants and suppliers, creating demand for industrial sites,
   utilities, rail and intermodal links, contract manufacturing, automation, and local materials.
3. Beneficiaries earn durable returns only when announced projects reach production, utilization
   rises, contracts protect margins, and infrastructure costs do not absorb the advantage.
4. Policy volatility, duplicated capacity, or weak end demand can strand capital even when the
   strategic rationale remains intact.

## Value-chain hypotheses

- **Industrial property and utilities:** scarcity can lift occupancy and pricing, but power, water,
  security, and permitting can delay tenants and require costly enabling investment.
- **Rail and intermodal:** cross-border volume can compound as qualified supply chains ramp; trade
  disputes, congestion, and weaker production remain direct offsets.
- **EMS and local suppliers:** qualification and customer commitments can raise utilization, yet
  customer concentration and price competition limit margins.
- **Automation and components:** new factories support sensors, controls, connectors, and robotics;
  orders must be distinguishable from the broader industrial cycle.
- **Materials:** locally compliant steel and other inputs may gain share, while commodity cycles and
  large project capex can overwhelm the regionalization benefit.

## Confirmation gates

- announced projects begin production on schedule and disclose utilization or customer awards;
- industrial occupancy, cross-border rail/intermodal volumes, and qualified supplier orders rise;
- USMCA review outcomes preserve workable rules and investment visibility;
- incentives translate into incremental output rather than subsidized displacement;
- power, water, labour, security, and logistics constraints do not erase delivered-cost gains; and
- beneficiaries convert growth into margins and free cash flow at defensible valuations.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis weakens if end-market capex slows, regional projects slip, or Asia retains a decisive
cost and ecosystem advantage. It is invalidated as a broad equity premium if USMCA or tariff rules
fragment integrated supply chains, infrastructure bottlenecks prevent production, incentive-backed
plants remain underutilized, or valuation already discounts the expected earnings.
