# Digital attention, gaming, and consumer ecosystems

## Thesis

Creator platforms can compound when more useful content raises engagement and monetization, which
funds creator rewards, safety, infrastructure, and discovery. Engagement alone is not sufficient:
bookings or subscriptions, cohort retention, creator earnings, free cash flow after platform costs,
and share-count discipline must improve together. Safety and regulatory controls are core operating
costs and trust inputs, not optional overlays.

## Mechanism and value chain

Creators expand content breadth; recommendation and social systems improve discovery; user time can
be monetized through virtual goods, advertising, subscriptions, and commerce. Platforms retain
value only after creator payouts, app-store and payment fees, infrastructure, moderation, and
stock-based compensation. Music, video, gaming, peripherals, and IP owners have different royalty,
hardware-cycle, and working-capital economics and should not be treated as one valuation basket.

## Confirmation gates

- engagement and bookings grow across cohorts and geographies, not from one viral experience;
- creator earnings expand without permanently compressing platform contribution margins;
- free cash flow remains positive after infrastructure and safety investment;
- safety controls improve trust without an enduring collapse in retention or monetization; and
- diluted share growth stays below per-share cash-flow growth.

## Invalidation

The thesis fails where attention fragments, content supply becomes undifferentiated, safety or
regulatory failures damage trust, distribution and creator payments absorb incremental revenue, or
cash flow depends mainly on deferred-revenue growth while dilution erodes per-share value.

## Bounded research candidate

Roblox (`security_c9a37d277445869a8809`) has a verified identity and unusually current disclosure
linking engagement, bookings, safety headwinds, free cash flow, and dilution. One review is queued
to test cohort durability, creator economics, safety, cash conversion, share count, valuation, and
downside. No relationship or strategy is created.

## Confidence and review

Confidence is **medium** in the creator-platform flywheel and **low-to-medium** in predictable
near-term monetization because current guidance shows safety-related volatility. Review by
**2026-10-31**, or sooner after Q2 results, a material safety or regulatory action, or a large change
in bookings guidance.
