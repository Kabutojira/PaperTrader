# Mobility-platform network economics and autonomy

## Thesis and mechanism

A scaled mobility marketplace can use rider demand, driver and courier supply, routing, pricing,
payments, membership, delivery and advertising to increase trips and cash generation without owning
every vehicle. Autonomous fleets can strengthen that network if third-party operators need demand,
dispatch and utilization, but can harm it if vehicle providers vertically integrate and bypass the
marketplace. The investable mechanism is therefore network monetization after insurance, incentive,
regulatory, stock-compensation and autonomy-investment costs—not trip growth alone.

Uber reported Q2 2026 gross bookings growth of 24%, trips growth of 18%, revenue growth of 12%,
USD1.9 billion operating income and USD2.8 billion free cash flow. These facts support current
network scale and cash conversion. They do not establish durable take rates, autonomous-vehicle
economics or a justified valuation. The issuer also identifies autonomy investment and partner
execution as material variables.

Primary evidence: https://investor.uber.com/news-events/news/press-release-details/2026/Uber-Announces-Results-for-Second-Quarter-2026/default.aspx

## Catalysts, risks and invalidation

Catalysts are durable bookings and trip growth, expanding free cash flow, improving advertising and
membership economics, autonomy partnerships that add supply without destroying take rates, and
evidence that insurance and incentives scale below gross profit. Risks include platform
disintermediation, driver classification, local regulation, insurance severity, competitive
subsidies, stock compensation, investment losses and valuation.

Invalidate the thesis if major autonomous fleets bypass Uber, trip growth no longer produces
incremental cash, regulation or insurance structurally compresses marketplace economics, or
dilution and capital deployment absorb the value created for common shareholders.

Confidence is **medium** in current network scale and **low-to-medium** in the autonomy transmission
path. Review by **2026-09-30**, or after material autonomy agreements, regulatory changes, insurance
developments or the next results.
