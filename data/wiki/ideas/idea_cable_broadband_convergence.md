# Cable broadband convergence and leveraged FCF

## Thesis

US cable operators are using mobile bundles, simpler pricing, rural expansion, and network upgrades
to defend broadband relationships. Mobile growth is real, but current results do not yet show broad
fixed-broadband stabilization. Highly leveraged equity can rerate sharply if losses slow and capex
normalizes, yet the same leverage magnifies competitive, refinancing, and merger-integration risk.

Current assessment: **convergence is gaining adoption, but the core stabilization thesis has not
passed**. Cable must prove that mobile attach improves whole-customer retention and cash economics,
not merely add lower-margin lines alongside broadband losses.

## Causal mechanism

1. Cable uses its fixed network and wholesale wireless access to bundle home and mobile connectivity.
2. Simpler pricing, rural passings, Wi-Fi offload, and network upgrades can reduce churn and increase
   relationship value.
3. If capex crests, stable EBITDA can create meaningful free cash flow for debt reduction and equity.
4. Persistent fibre and fixed-wireless losses, ARPU pressure, high rates, or merger obligations can
   absorb that cash and permanently impair leveraged equity.

## Value-chain hypotheses

- Cable operators gain if mobile attach lowers churn and Wi-Fi offload preserves unit economics.
- Fibre and fixed-wireless operators are both substitutes and useful hedges against cable share loss.
- Network-equipment vendors benefit from upgrade capex but are exposed if operators defer spending.
- Cable debt can offer a different payoff than common equity when deleveraging is uncertain.

## Confirmation gates

- fixed-broadband losses improve for several quarters without destructive pricing;
- mobile cohorts show lower total relationship churn and positive contribution margins;
- capex declines after upgrade and rural programs without degrading network quality;
- EBITDA and free cash flow per share stabilize before buybacks or merger financing; and
- refinancing, preferred distributions, dilution, and integration remain covered under stress.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis weakens while broadband losses, revenue, EBITDA, and FCF deteriorate despite mobile
growth. It is invalid for a leveraged operator if fibre or fixed wireless sustains share gains,
pricing falls, capex stays elevated, refinancing costs rise, or merger obligations prevent durable
debt reduction and per-share cash-flow recovery.
