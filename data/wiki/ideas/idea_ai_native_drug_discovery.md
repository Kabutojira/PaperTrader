# AI-native drug-discovery platforms

## Thesis

AI models linked to proprietary biological and chemical data and automated wet labs can shorten
some discovery loops and improve candidate selection. Pharma investment and regulatory engagement
confirm adoption, but clinical translation—not model output—is the economic bottleneck. Platform
companies must demonstrate repeatable milestone revenue or owned-pipeline value before cash burn,
dilution, and partner bargaining power consume the benefit.

Current assessment: **tool adoption is confirmed; a generalized improvement in clinical success is
not**. Early clinical programs provide testable proof points, not validation of the whole platform.

## Causal mechanism

1. Models search chemical and biological spaces and prioritize experiments using proprietary data.
2. Automated labs generate feedback, creating a faster design-make-test-learn loop.
3. Better candidates could reduce cycle time and failed experiments, attracting licenses,
   milestones, co-development funding, or acquisition interest.
4. Shareholder value exists only if the efficiency survives wet-lab, toxicology, clinical, and
   regulatory validation and the platform retains meaningful economics.

## Value-chain hypotheses

- Compute, scientific software, data infrastructure, and lab automation sell picks-and-shovels, but
  pharma may capture most downstream value.
- AI-native platforms can earn partnerships and pipeline optionality; milestone timing and
  therapeutic concentration make revenue volatile.
- Large pharma can license, acquire, or internalize platforms, strengthening its bargaining power.
- Traditional CRO and discovery workflows face substitution only where digital triage measurably
  reduces outsourced work rather than adding another validation layer.

## Confirmation gates

- platform-derived candidates produce replicated clinical efficacy and acceptable safety;
- cycle-time or success-rate improvement is quantified against a relevant baseline;
- partnership milestones and recurring platform revenue become material and repeatable;
- cash runway extends through value-defining readouts without excessive dilution; and
- data rights, economics, and intellectual property remain defensible.

## Contrary evidence and invalidation

The thesis weakens if models improve preclinical ranking without improving clinical outcomes,
pharma internalizes the workflow, or partnerships remain lumpy. It is invalid for a platform if
clinical failures break the claimed mechanism, cash burn forces repeated dilution before readouts,
or most economics accrue to partners and compute suppliers.
